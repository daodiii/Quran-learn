### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | أَنَّ رَجُلًا | anna rajulan | that a man | أَنَّ means "that" and makes the word after it accusative, so رَجُلًا ends in ـًا. The tanwīn means he is not named. |
| 2 | قَالَ | qāla | said | Past tense, he, with a fixed fatḥa. |
| 3 | لِلنَّبِيِّ | li-n-nabiyyi | to the Prophet | لِ makes النَّبِيِّ genitive (ـِ). The alif of ال drops after لِ. |
| 4 | صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ | ṣallā llāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam | peace and blessings be upon him | A prayer for him, written as a past verb with the meaning of a wish. اللَّهُ is nominative (ـُ). عَلَى becomes عَلَيْ before ـهِ. |
| 5 | أَوْصِنِي | awṣinī | advise me | A command to one person, ending in a silent letter. The ـنِ is a protecting ن added before ـي ("me"). |
| 6 | قَالَ | qāla | he said | Past tense, he. |
| 7 | لَا تَغْضَبْ | lā taghḍab | do not be angry | Here لَا means "do not". It makes the present verb end in a silent letter. This is how a command not to do something is made. |
| 8 | فَرَدَّدَ | fa-raddada | and he repeated | فَ means "and then". The doubled د marks the pattern that means doing something again and again. Past tense, he. |
| 9 | مِرَارًا | mirāran | several times | Accusative (ـًا): it says how many times. |
| 10 | قَالَ لَا تَغْضَبْ | qāla lā taghḍab | he said: do not be angry | The same as rows 6 and 7. |
</GrammarTable>
