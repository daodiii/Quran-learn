The Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, related this from his Lord, blessed and exalted:

## The Whole Hadith

<ArabicExample
  arabic="إِنَّ اللَّهَ كَتَبَ الْحَسَنَاتِ وَالسَّيِّئَاتِ، ثُمَّ بَيَّنَ ذَلِكَ، فَمَنْ هَمَّ بِحَسَنَةٍ فَلَمْ يَعْمَلْهَا كَتَبَهَا اللَّهُ عِنْدَهُ حَسَنَةً كَامِلَةً، وَإِنْ هَمَّ بِهَا فَعَمِلَهَا كَتَبَهَا اللَّهُ عِنْدَهُ عَشْرَ حَسَنَاتٍ إِلَى سَبْعِمِائَةِ ضِعْفٍ إِلَى أَضْعَافٍ كَثِيرَةٍ، وَإِنْ هَمَّ بِسَيِّئَةٍ فَلَمْ يَعْمَلْهَا كَتَبَهَا اللَّهُ عِنْدَهُ حَسَنَةً كَامِلَةً، وَإِنْ هَمَّ بِهَا فَعَمِلَهَا كَتَبَهَا اللَّهُ سَيِّئَةً وَاحِدَةً"

Below, the hadith is explained part by part.

## 1. Allah Has Recorded Them

<ArabicExample
  arabic="إِنَّ اللَّهَ كَتَبَ الْحَسَنَاتِ وَالسَّيِّئَاتِ، ثُمَّ بَيَّنَ ذَلِكَ"
  transliteration="inna llāha kataba l-ḥasanāti wa-s-sayyiʾāti, thumma bayyana dhālika"
  translation="Allah has written down the good deeds and the bad deeds, and then He made clear what that means."
  reference="Hadith 37, part 1 · Narrated by Abdullah ibn Abbas"
/>

### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | إِنَّ اللَّهَ | inna llāha | indeed Allah | إِنَّ makes اللَّهَ accusative (ـَ). |
| 2 | كَتَبَ | kataba | has written | Past tense, he, with a fixed fatḥa. |
| 3 | الْحَسَنَاتِ وَالسَّيِّئَاتِ | al-ḥasanāti wa-s-sayyiʾāti | the good deeds and the bad deeds | Both are accusative: they are what He wrote. A feminine plural ends in ـَاتِ for the accusative, with a kasra. |
| 4 | ثُمَّ بَيَّنَ | thumma bayyana | then He made clear | ثُمَّ means "then". The doubled ي marks the pattern meaning "to make clear". |
| 5 | ذَلِكَ | dhālika | that | A pointing word. It is what He made clear, and it never shows a case vowel. |
</GrammarTable>

## 2. Intending a Good Deed

<ArabicExample
  arabic="فَمَنْ هَمَّ بِحَسَنَةٍ فَلَمْ يَعْمَلْهَا كَتَبَهَا اللَّهُ عِنْدَهُ حَسَنَةً كَامِلَةً، وَإِنْ هَمَّ بِهَا فَعَمِلَهَا كَتَبَهَا اللَّهُ عِنْدَهُ عَشْرَ حَسَنَاتٍ إِلَى سَبْعِمِائَةِ ضِعْفٍ إِلَى أَضْعَافٍ كَثِيرَةٍ"
  transliteration="fa-man hamma bi-ḥasanatin fa-lam yaʿmalhā katabahā llāhu ʿindahu ḥasanatan kāmilatan, wa-in hamma bihā fa-ʿamilahā katabahā llāhu ʿindahu ʿashra ḥasanātin ilā sabʿimiʾati ḍiʿfin ilā aḍʿāfin kathīratin"
  translation="Whoever intends a good deed and does not do it, Allah writes it with Him as one complete good deed. If they intend it and do it, Allah writes it with Him as ten good deeds, up to seven hundred times, up to many times more."
  reference="Hadith 37, part 2"
/>

### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | فَمَنْ هَمَّ | fa-man hamma | so whoever intends | فَ means "so". مَنْ starts a condition. هَمَّ is past tense, he, with a doubled م, and means "to set the heart on doing something". |
| 2 | بِحَسَنَةٍ | bi-ḥasanatin | to do a good deed | بِ makes حَسَنَةٍ genitive (ـٍ). |
| 3 | فَلَمْ يَعْمَلْهَا | fa-lam yaʿmalhā | and does not do it | لَمْ makes the verb end in a silent letter. The attached ـهَا means "it". |
| 4 | كَتَبَهَا اللَّهُ | katabahā llāhu | Allah writes it | The result of the condition. Past tense, he. The attached ـهَا means "it". اللَّهُ is nominative (ـُ). |
| 5 | عِنْدَهُ | ʿindahu | with Him | عِنْدَ is accusative (ـَ), a word for place. The attached ـهُ means "Him". |
| 6 | حَسَنَةً كَامِلَةً | ḥasanatan kāmilatan | one complete good deed | حَسَنَةً is accusative (ـًا): it is what it is written as. كَامِلَةً describes it and takes the same case. |
| 7 | وَإِنْ هَمَّ بِهَا فَعَمِلَهَا | wa-in hamma bihā fa-ʿamilahā | and if he intends it and does it | إِنْ means "if". The attached ـهَا means "it". |
| 8 | عَشْرَ حَسَنَاتٍ | ʿashra ḥasanātin | ten good deeds | عَشْرَ is accusative (ـَ): it is what it is written as. After a number from 3 to 10, the thing counted is plural and genitive, so حَسَنَاتٍ ends in ـٍ. |
| 9 | إِلَى سَبْعِمِائَةِ ضِعْفٍ | ilā sabʿimiʾati ḍiʿfin | up to seven hundred times | إِلَى makes سَبْعِمِائَةِ genitive (ـِ). ضِعْفٍ is genitive (ـٍ) because it completes it. |
| 10 | إِلَى أَضْعَافٍ كَثِيرَةٍ | ilā aḍʿāfin kathīratin | to many multiples | إِلَى makes أَضْعَافٍ genitive (ـٍ). كَثِيرَةٍ describes it and takes the same case. |
</GrammarTable>

## 3. Intending a Bad Deed

<ArabicExample
  arabic="وَإِنْ هَمَّ بِسَيِّئَةٍ فَلَمْ يَعْمَلْهَا كَتَبَهَا اللَّهُ عِنْدَهُ حَسَنَةً كَامِلَةً، وَإِنْ هَمَّ بِهَا فَعَمِلَهَا كَتَبَهَا اللَّهُ سَيِّئَةً وَاحِدَةً"
  transliteration="wa-in hamma bi-sayyiʾatin fa-lam yaʿmalhā katabahā llāhu ʿindahu ḥasanatan kāmilatan, wa-in hamma bihā fa-ʿamilahā katabahā llāhu sayyiʾatan wāḥidatan"
  translation="If they intend a bad deed and do not do it, Allah writes it with Him as one complete good deed. If they intend it and do it, Allah writes it as one bad deed."
  reference="Hadith 37, part 3"
/>
