### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | اتَّقِ | ittaqi | be mindful of | A command to one person, from a verb meaning "to guard yourself". The verb's last letter has dropped, so the command ends in kasra. |
| 2 | اللَّهَ | Allāha | Allah | Accusative (ـَ): it is who you are mindful of. |
| 3 | حَيْثُمَا | ḥaythumā | wherever | Made of حَيْثُ ("where") and مَا. It starts a condition, and its ending never changes. |
| 4 | كُنْتَ | kunta | you are | Past tense of "to be", with ـتَ ("you", masculine, singular). The long vowel of كَانَ drops before the silent ت, so it reads kun-. |
| 5 | وَأَتْبِعِ | wa-atbiʿi | and follow up | A command to one person, ending in a silent letter that takes a kasra before the ال that follows. The verb means "to make one thing follow another". |
| 6 | السَّيِّئَةَ | as-sayyiʾata | the bad deed | Accusative (ـَ): the first thing named after the verb. |
| 7 | الْحَسَنَةَ | al-ḥasanata | the good deed | Accusative (ـَ): the second thing named. The verb takes two objects: the bad deed is followed by the good deed. |
| 8 | تَمْحُهَا | tamḥuhā | it will wipe it out | Present tense, it, ending in a silent letter because it is the result of a command: "follow…, and it will wipe it out". The final و of the verb has dropped, leaving ḍamma. The attached ـهَا means "it". |
| 9 | وَخَالِقِ | wa-khāliqi | and deal with | A command to one person, from a verb meaning "to deal with someone". It ends in a silent letter that takes a kasra before ال. |
| 10 | النَّاسَ | an-nāsa | the people | Accusative (ـَ): it is who you deal with. |
| 11 | بِخُلُقٍ | bi-khuluqin | with character | بِ makes خُلُقٍ genitive (ـٍ). |
| 12 | حَسَنٍ | ḥasanin | good | Genitive (ـٍ): it describes خُلُقٍ, so it copies its case. |
</GrammarTable>
