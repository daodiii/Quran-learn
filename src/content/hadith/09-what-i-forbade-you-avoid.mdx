### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | مَا | mā | what | Means "that which". It opens the sentence as its topic, and its ending never changes. |
| 2 | نَهَيْتُكُمْ | nahaytukum | I forbade you | Past tense. The ـتُ ending means "I", and the attached ـكُمْ means "you" (plural). |
| 3 | عَنْهُ | ʿanhu | from it | عَنْ plus ـهُ ("it"), pointing back to مَا. |
| 4 | فَاجْتَنِبُوهُ | fa-ajtanibūhu | then avoid it | فَ opens the instruction. A command to several people drops the final ن, so it ends in ـُوا. The attached ـهُ means "it". |
| 5 | وَمَا أَمَرْتُكُمْ بِهِ | wa-mā amartukum bihi | and what I commanded you | Built the same way as rows 1–3. بِ takes a kasra before ـهُ, so it reads bihi. |
| 6 | فَأْتُوا | fa-ʾtū | then do (bring) | A command to several people, from the verb "to bring". The ن has dropped, leaving ـُوا. The hamza carries a sukūn. |
| 7 | مِنْهُ | minhu | of it | مِنْ here means "some of it". |
| 8 | مَا اسْتَطَعْتُمْ | mā staṭaʿtum | what you are able | مَا means "as much as". اسْتَطَعْتُمْ is past tense with ـتُمْ ("you", plural). Its opening hamza drops after مَا. |
| 9 | فَإِنَّمَا | fa-innamā | for only | فَ means "for" or "because". إِنَّمَا means "only". |
| 10 | أَهْلَكَ | ahlaka | destroyed | Past tense, he, with a fixed fatḥa. The أَ at the start marks the pattern meaning "to cause to perish". |
| 11 | الَّذِينَ | alladhīna | those who | A word meaning "those who". It does not change its ending. Here they are the ones destroyed. |
| 12 | مِنْ قَبْلِكُمْ | min qablikum | before you | مِنْ makes قَبْلِ genitive (ـِ). The attached ـكُمْ means "you". |
| 13 | كَثْرَةُ | kathratu | the abundance of | Nominative (ـُ): it is what did the destroying. The word order puts the ones destroyed first and the doer after. |
| 14 | مَسَائِلِهِمْ | masāʾilihim | their questions | Genitive (ـِ): it completes كَثْرَةُ. The attached ـهِمْ means "their". |
| 15 | وَاخْتِلَافُهُمْ | wa-ikhtilāfuhum | and their disagreeing | Nominative (ـُ): it is joined to كَثْرَةُ, so it is also a doer. The attached ـهُمْ means "their". |
| 16 | عَلَى أَنْبِيَائِهِمْ | ʿalā anbiyāʾihim | with their prophets | عَلَى makes أَنْبِيَاءِ genitive (ـِ). The attached ـهِمْ means "their". |
</GrammarTable>
