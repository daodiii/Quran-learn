## Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | بُنِيَ | buniya | was built | A past verb in the passive: the one who built is not named. The passive shows in the vowels: ḍamma on the first letter and kasra on the one before the last. It ends in a fixed fatḥa. |
| 2 | الْإِسْلَامُ | al-islāmu | Islam | Nominative (ـُ): with a passive verb, the thing that was acted on takes the doer's place, so it is nominative like a subject. |
| 3 | عَلَى | ʿalā | on | A preposition. The noun after it is genitive. |
| 4 | خَمْسٍ | khamsin | five | Genitive (ـٍ) because it follows عَلَى. The tanwīn leaves it open: the list that follows says which five. |
| 5 | شَهَادَةِ | shahādati | the testimony | Genitive (ـِ). It is the first item in the list and explains خَمْسٍ, so it takes the same case. |
| 6 | أَنْ | an | that | Joins شَهَادَة to what is being testified: "the testimony that…". |
| 7 | لَا | lā | no | Denies the whole kind: "no god at all". This is why the next word has no tanwīn. |
| 8 | إِلَهَ | ilāha | god | Fixed on fatḥa with no tanwīn, because لَا is denying the whole kind. |
| 9 | إِلَّا | illā | except | Makes an exception from the denial. |
| 10 | اللَّهُ | Allāhu | Allah | Nominative (ـُ). In a negative sentence, the word after إِلَّا takes the case of the word it picks out. Here it stands for the missing "exists": "no god exists except Allah". |
| 11 | وَأَنَّ | wa-anna | and that | وَ adds a second testimony. أَنَّ means "that" and puts the next word in the accusative. |
| 12 | مُحَمَّدًا | Muḥammadan | Muhammad | Accusative (ـًا), because أَنَّ makes the word after it accusative. |
| 13 | رَسُولُ | rasūlu | messenger of | Nominative (ـُ): it is the second part of the statement, "Muhammad is a messenger". It stays nominative because أَنَّ only changes the first word. |
| 14 | اللَّهِ | Allāhi | Allah | Genitive (ـِ) because it completes رَسُولُ: "messenger of Allah". |
| 15 | وَإِقَامِ | wa-iqāmi | and establishing | Genitive (ـِ). It is the second item of the list and takes the same case as شَهَادَةِ. It is a verbal noun: "establishing". |
| 16 | الصَّلَاةِ | aṣ-ṣalāti | the prayer | Genitive (ـِ) because it completes إِقَامِ: "establishing of the prayer". |
| 17 | وَإِيتَاءِ | wa-ītāʾi | and giving | Genitive (ـِ), third item of the list. A verbal noun for "to give". |
| 18 | الزَّكَاةِ | az-zakāti | the zakat | Genitive (ـِ) because it completes إِيتَاءِ. |
| 19 | وَحَجِّ | wa-ḥajji | and the pilgrimage to | Genitive (ـِ), fourth item of the list. |
| 20 | الْبَيْتِ | al-bayti | the House | Genitive (ـِ) because it completes حَجِّ. "The House" means the Kaaba. |
| 21 | وَصَوْمِ | wa-ṣawmi | and fasting | Genitive (ـِ), fifth item of the list. |
| 22 | رَمَضَانَ | Ramaḍāna | Ramadan | Genitive, but it shows fatḥa and has no tanwīn. This is a name ending in ـان, and names like that take fatḥa instead of kasra. |
</GrammarTable>
