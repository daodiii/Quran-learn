### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | مِنْ | min | part of, among | A preposition. It makes the next word genitive. The phrase it starts comes first in the sentence, and the thing it describes follows it. |
| 2 | حُسْنِ | ḥusni | the excellence of | Genitive (ـِ) after مِنْ. It is also the first word of a pair: "the excellence of…". |
| 3 | إِسْلَامِ | islāmi | the Islam of | Genitive (ـِ) because it completes حُسْنِ. |
| 4 | الْمَرْءِ | al-marʾi | the person | Genitive (ـِ) because it completes إِسْلَامِ. |
| 5 | تَرْكُهُ | tarkuhu | his leaving | Nominative (ـُ): it is what the sentence is about. It is a verbal noun, "leaving". The attached ـهُ means "his", meaning the person's. |
| 6 | مَا | mā | what | Means "that which": the thing that is left. Its ending never changes. |
| 7 | لَا يَعْنِيهِ | lā yaʿnīhi | does not concern him | لَا denies the verb. يَعْنِي is present tense, and its final ـِي is part of the verb. The attached ـهِ means "him" and takes kasra because of the ي before it. |
</GrammarTable>
