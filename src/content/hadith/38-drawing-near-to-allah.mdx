## 1. Enmity and the Obligations

<ArabicExample
  arabic="إِنَّ اللَّهَ تَعَالَى قَالَ: مَنْ عَادَى لِي وَلِيًّا فَقَدْ آذَنْتُهُ بِالْحَرْبِ، وَمَا تَقَرَّبَ إِلَيَّ عَبْدِي بِشَيْءٍ أَحَبَّ إِلَيَّ مِمَّا افْتَرَضْتُهُ عَلَيْهِ"
  transliteration="inna llāha taʿālā qāla: man ʿādā lī waliyyan fa-qad ādhantuhu bi-l-ḥarbi, wa-mā taqarraba ilayya ʿabdī bi-shayʾin aḥabba ilayya mimmā ftaraḍtuhu ʿalayhi"
  translation="Allah, the Most High, has said: “Whoever shows enmity to a friend of Mine, I have declared war on them. My servant draws near to Me with nothing more beloved to Me than what I have made obligatory for them.”"
  reference="Hadith 38, part 1 · Narrated by Abu Hurayra"
/>

### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | إِنَّ اللَّهَ تَعَالَى قَالَ | inna llāha taʿālā qāla | indeed Allah, the Most High, said | إِنَّ makes اللَّهَ accusative (ـَ). تَعَالَى is a phrase of praise. قَالَ is past tense, he. |
| 2 | مَنْ عَادَى | man ʿādā | whoever shows enmity | مَنْ starts a condition. عَادَى is past tense, he, and ends in a long ā. |
| 3 | لِي وَلِيًّا | lī waliyyan | to a friend of Mine | لِي means "to Me". وَلِيًّا is accusative (ـًا): it is who is treated as an enemy. |
| 4 | فَقَدْ آذَنْتُهُ | fa-qad ādhantuhu | then I have declared to him | فَ opens the result. قَدْ means "has indeed". آذَنْتُهُ is past tense with ـتُ ("I") and ـهُ ("him"). |
| 5 | بِالْحَرْبِ | bi-l-ḥarbi | war | بِ makes الْحَرْبِ genitive (ـِ). |
| 6 | وَمَا تَقَرَّبَ | wa-mā taqarraba | and does not draw near | Here مَا means "not". The doubled ر marks the pattern meaning "to draw near". |
| 7 | إِلَيَّ | ilayya | to Me | إِلَى plus ـي ("me"): the ي is doubled. |
| 8 | عَبْدِي | ʿabdī | My servant | Nominative: he is who draws near. The attached ـي means "my", so a kasra shows. |
| 9 | بِشَيْءٍ | bi-shayʾin | with anything | بِ makes شَيْءٍ genitive (ـٍ). |
| 10 | أَحَبَّ إِلَيَّ | aḥabba ilayya | more beloved to Me | أَحَبَّ describes شَيْءٍ. It is on the "more…" pattern, so it takes no tanwīn and shows fatḥa even though it is genitive. |
| 11 | مِمَّا افْتَرَضْتُهُ عَلَيْهِ | mimmā ftaraḍtuhu ʿalayhi | than what I made obligatory for him | مِنْ plus مَا ("that which"), written together. افْتَرَضْتُهُ is past tense with ـتُ ("I") and ـهُ ("it"). |
</GrammarTable>

## 2. Extra Acts and Love

<ArabicExample
  arabic="وَلَا يَزَالُ عَبْدِي يَتَقَرَّبُ إِلَيَّ بِالنَّوَافِلِ حَتَّى أُحِبَّهُ، فَإِذَا أَحْبَبْتُهُ كُنْتُ سَمْعَهُ الَّذِي يَسْمَعُ بِهِ، وَبَصَرَهُ الَّذِي يُبْصِرُ بِهِ، وَيَدَهُ الَّتِي يَبْطِشُ بِهَا، وَرِجْلَهُ الَّتِي يَمْشِي بِهَا"
  transliteration="wa-lā yazālu ʿabdī yataqarrabu ilayya bi-n-nawāfili ḥattā uḥibbahu, fa-idhā aḥbabtuhu kuntu samʿahu lladhī yasmaʿu bihi, wa-baṣarahu lladhī yubṣiru bihi, wa-yadahu llatī yabṭishu bihā, wa-rijlahu llatī yamshī bihā"
  translation="My servant keeps drawing near to Me with extra acts until I love them. When I love them, I am their hearing with which they hear, their sight with which they see, their hand with which they strike, and their foot with which they walk."
  reference="Hadith 38, part 2"
/>

### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | وَلَا يَزَالُ | wa-lā yazālu | and he keeps on | يَزَالُ with لَا means "keeps on doing". It is present tense, he, nominative (ـُ). |
| 2 | عَبْدِي | ʿabdī | My servant | Nominative (ـُ), shown as a kasra before ـي ("my"). |
| 3 | يَتَقَرَّبُ إِلَيَّ | yataqarrabu ilayya | draws near to Me | Present tense, he, nominative (ـُ). It completes يَزَالُ. |
| 4 | بِالنَّوَافِلِ | bi-n-nawāfili | with the extra acts | بِ makes النَّوَافِلِ genitive (ـِ). |
| 5 | حَتَّى أُحِبَّهُ | ḥattā uḥibbahu | until I love him | حَتَّى makes the present verb end in fatḥa. أُحِبَّ is present tense, I, with a doubled ب. The attached ـهُ means "him". |
| 6 | فَإِذَا أَحْبَبْتُهُ | fa-idhā aḥbabtuhu | and when I love him | إِذَا means "when". أَحْبَبْتُ is past tense with ـتُ ("I"): the two ب are written apart because of the silent ت. |
| 7 | كُنْتُ سَمْعَهُ | kuntu samʿahu | I am his hearing | كُنْتُ is "to be" with ـتُ ("I"). سَمْعَهُ is accusative (ـَ): it is what "I am". The attached ـهُ means "his". |
| 8 | الَّذِي يَسْمَعُ بِهِ | alladhī yasmaʿu bihi | with which he hears | الَّذِي means "which" (masculine). It describes سَمْعَهُ. يَسْمَعُ is present tense, he. |
| 9 | وَبَصَرَهُ الَّذِي يُبْصِرُ بِهِ | wa-baṣarahu lladhī yubṣiru bihi | and his sight with which he sees | The same pattern. بَصَرَهُ is accusative (ـَ), joined to سَمْعَهُ. |
| 10 | وَيَدَهُ الَّتِي يَبْطِشُ بِهَا | wa-yadahu llatī yabṭishu bihā | and his hand with which he strikes | الَّتِي means "which" (feminine), because يَد is feminine. The attached ـهَا means "it". |
| 11 | وَرِجْلَهُ الَّتِي يَمْشِي بِهَا | wa-rijlahu llatī yamshī bihā | and his foot with which he walks | The same pattern. |
</GrammarTable>

## 3. Asking and Seeking Refuge

<ArabicExample
  arabic="وَلَئِنْ سَأَلَنِي لَأُعْطِيَنَّهُ، وَلَئِنِ اسْتَعَاذَنِي لَأُعِيذَنَّهُ"
  transliteration="wa-la-in saʾalanī la-uʿṭiyannahu, wa-la-ini staʿādhanī la-uʿīdhannahu"
  translation="If they ask Me, I will certainly give them, and if they seek refuge in Me, I will certainly protect them."
  reference="Hadith 38, part 3"
/>

### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | وَلَئِنْ سَأَلَنِي | wa-la-in saʾalanī | and if he asks Me | لَ adds strength. إِنْ means "if". سَأَلَ is past tense, he. A protecting ن comes before the attached ـي ("me"). |
| 2 | لَأُعْطِيَنَّهُ | la-uʿṭiyannahu | I will certainly give him | لَ means "certainly". The verb is present tense, I, and the ـنَّ ending strengthens it and puts it in fatḥa. The attached ـهُ means "him". |
| 3 | وَلَئِنِ اسْتَعَاذَنِي | wa-la-ini staʿādhanī | and if he seeks refuge in Me | إِنْ takes a kasra before the joining hamza. اسْتَعَاذَ is past tense, he, with a protecting ن before ـي ("me"). |
| 4 | لَأُعِيذَنَّهُ | la-uʿīdhannahu | I will certainly protect him | The same pattern as row 2, from the verb "to protect". |
</GrammarTable>
