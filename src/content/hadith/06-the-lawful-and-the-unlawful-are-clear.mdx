## 1. The Clear and the Unclear

<ArabicExample

  transliteration="inna l-ḥalāla bayyinun, wa-inna l-ḥarāma bayyinun, wa-baynahumā umūrun mushtabihātun lā yaʿlamuhunna kathīrun mina n-nāsi, fa-mani ttaqā sh-shubuhāti fa-qadi stabraʾa li-dīnihi wa-ʿirḍihi, wa-man waqaʿa fī sh-shubuhāti waqaʿa fī l-ḥarāmi, ka-r-rāʿī yarʿā ḥawla l-ḥimā yūshiku an yartaʿa fīhi"

### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | إِنَّ | inna | indeed | A word that puts the noun after it in the accusative. |
| 2 | الْحَلَالَ | al-ḥalāla | the lawful | Accusative (ـَ) because it follows إِنَّ. |
| 3 | بَيِّنٌ | bayyinun | clear | Nominative (ـٌ): it is the second part of the statement. It stays nominative even after إِنَّ. |
| 4 | وَإِنَّ الْحَرَامَ بَيِّنٌ | wa-inna l-ḥarāma bayyinun | and the unlawful is clear | The same pattern: الْحَرَامَ is accusative (ـَ) after إِنَّ, and بَيِّنٌ is nominative (ـٌ). |
| 5 | وَبَيْنَهُمَا | wa-baynahumā | and between them | بَيْنَ is a word for place, shown with fatḥa. The attached ـهُمَا means "the two of them". This phrase comes first, and the thing that is between them follows. |
| 6 | أُمُورٌ | umūrun | matters | Nominative (ـٌ): it is what is between the two. |
| 7 | مُشْتَبِهَاتٌ | mushtabihātun | unclear | Nominative (ـٌ): it describes أُمُورٌ and takes its case. A feminine plural ends in ـَاتٌ. |
| 8 | لَا يَعْلَمُهُنَّ | lā yaʿlamuhunna | do not know them | لَا denies. يَعْلَمُ is present tense, nominative (ـُ). The attached ـهُنَّ means "them" (feminine plural), pointing to the matters. The clause adds a second description of them. |
| 9 | كَثِيرٌ | kathīrun | many | Nominative (ـٌ): it is the one who does not know. |
| 10 | مِنَ النَّاسِ | mina n-nāsi | of the people | مِنْ takes a fatḥa before ال. It makes النَّاسِ genitive (ـِ). |
| 11 | فَمَنِ اتَّقَى | fa-mani ttaqā | so whoever guards against | فَ means "so". مَنْ means "whoever" and takes a kasra before the joining hamza. اتَّقَى is past tense, he. It ends in a long ā, so no vowel shows. |
| 12 | الشُّبُهَاتِ | ash-shubuhāti | the unclear matters | Accusative: it is what he guards against. A feminine plural ends in ـَاتِ for the accusative, with a kasra. |
| 13 | فَقَدِ اسْتَبْرَأَ | fa-qadi stabraʾa | then he has cleared | فَ opens the result. قَدْ here means "has indeed", and takes a kasra before the joining hamza. اسْتَبْرَأَ is past tense, he. |
| 14 | لِدِينِهِ | li-dīnihi | for his religion | لِ makes دِينِ genitive (ـِ). The attached ـهِ means "his". |
| 15 | وَعِرْضِهِ | wa-ʿirḍihi | and his honor | Genitive (ـِ): it is joined to دِينِهِ, so it takes the same case. |
| 16 | وَمَنْ وَقَعَ | wa-man waqaʿa | and whoever falls | وَ adds a second condition. وَقَعَ is past tense, he. |
| 17 | فِي الشُّبُهَاتِ | fī sh-shubuhāti | into the unclear matters | فِي makes الشُّبُهَاتِ genitive (ـِ). |
| 18 | وَقَعَ فِي الْحَرَامِ | waqaʿa fī l-ḥarāmi | falls into the unlawful | The result of the condition. الْحَرَامِ is genitive (ـِ) after فِي. |
| 19 | كَالرَّاعِي | ka-r-rāʿī | like the shepherd | كَ means "like" and makes the next word genitive. The word ends in a long ī, so no vowel shows. |
| 20 | يَرْعَى | yarʿā | grazes | Present tense, he. The ending is a long ā, so no vowel shows. It describes the shepherd. |
| 21 | حَوْلَ الْحِمَى | ḥawla l-ḥimā | around the protected pasture | حَوْلَ is a word for place, shown with fatḥa. الْحِمَى is genitive, but it ends in a long ā, so no vowel shows. |
| 22 | يُوشِكُ | yūshiku | is about to | Present tense, he, nominative (ـُ). It means "is close to". |
| 23 | أَنْ يَرْتَعَ | an yartaʿa | to graze (freely) | أَنْ makes the verb end in fatḥa. |
| 24 | فِيهِ | fīhi | in it | فِي plus ـهِ ("it"), pointing to the pasture. |
</GrammarTable>

## 2. The Heart

<ArabicExample
  arabic="أَلَا وَإِنَّ لِكُلِّ مَلِكٍ حِمًى، أَلَا وَإِنَّ حِمَى اللَّهِ مَحَارِمُهُ، أَلَا وَإِنَّ فِي الْجَسَدِ مُضْغَةً إِذَا صَلَحَتْ صَلَحَ الْجَسَدُ كُلُّهُ، وَإِذَا فَسَدَتْ فَسَدَ الْجَسَدُ كُلُّهُ، أَلَا وَهِيَ الْقَلْبُ"
  transliteration="alā wa-inna li-kulli malikin ḥiman, alā wa-inna ḥimā llāhi maḥārimuhu, alā wa-inna fī l-jasadi muḍghatan idhā ṣalaḥat ṣalaḥa l-jasadu kulluhu, wa-idhā fasadat fasada l-jasadu kulluhu, alā wa-hiya l-qalbu"
  translation="Beware: every king has a protected pasture. Beware: Allah's protected pasture is what He has forbidden. Beware: in the body there is a piece of flesh. When it is sound, the whole body is sound, and when it is corrupt, the whole body is corrupt. Beware: it is the heart."
  reference="Hadith 6, part 2"
/>

### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | أَلَا | alā | beware | A word that calls attention. It never changes. |
| 2 | وَإِنَّ | wa-inna | and indeed | وَ adds a statement. إِنَّ puts the noun after it in the accusative. |
| 3 | لِكُلِّ مَلِكٍ | li-kulli malikin | for every king | لِ makes كُلِّ genitive (ـِ). مَلِكٍ is genitive (ـٍ) because it completes كُلِّ. |
| 4 | حِمًى | ḥiman | a protected pasture | Accusative after إِنَّ. The ending is a long ā, so the case vowel does not show, and the tanwīn appears as ـً with no alif. This phrase comes after "for every king", which comes first. |
| 5 | حِمَى اللَّهِ | ḥimā llāhi | Allah's protected pasture | حِمَى is accusative after إِنَّ, with no vowel showing. اللَّهِ is genitive (ـِ) because it completes حِمَى. |
| 6 | مَحَارِمُهُ | maḥārimuhu | what He forbade | Nominative (ـُ): it is the second part of the statement. The attached ـهُ means "His". |
| 7 | فِي الْجَسَدِ | fī l-jasadi | in the body | فِي makes الْجَسَدِ genitive (ـِ). This phrase comes first, and the thing in the body follows. |
| 8 | مُضْغَةً | muḍghatan | a piece of flesh | Accusative (ـً) after إِنَّ, though it follows the phrase "in the body". |
| 9 | إِذَا صَلَحَتْ | idhā ṣalaḥat | when it is sound | إِذَا means "when". صَلَحَتْ is past tense, and the ـتْ ending marks "she", because the flesh is feminine. |
| 10 | صَلَحَ الْجَسَدُ كُلُّهُ | ṣalaḥa l-jasadu kulluhu | the whole body is sound | صَلَحَ is past tense, he. الْجَسَدُ is nominative (ـُ), the one who is sound. كُلُّهُ is nominative too, because it adds emphasis to الْجَسَدُ ("all of it"). |
| 11 | وَإِذَا فَسَدَتْ فَسَدَ الْجَسَدُ كُلُّهُ | wa-idhā fasadat fasada l-jasadu kulluhu | and when it is corrupt, the whole body is corrupt | The same pattern, with the opposite meaning. |
| 12 | وَهِيَ | wa-hiya | and it | هِيَ means "she" or "it". It is feminine because it points back to the piece of flesh. It is the subject. |
| 13 | الْقَلْبُ | al-qalbu | the heart | Nominative (ـُ): it is the second part of the statement, "it is the heart". |
</GrammarTable>
