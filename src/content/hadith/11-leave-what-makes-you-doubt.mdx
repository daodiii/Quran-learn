### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | دَعْ | daʿ | leave | A command to one person. It ends in a silent letter. The first letter of the verb has dropped, which is why it is only two letters. |
| 2 | مَا | mā | what | Means "that which". Its ending never changes. |
| 3 | يَرِيبُكَ | yarībuka | makes you doubt | Present tense, he or it, nominative (ـُ). The attached ـكَ means "you", the one who is made to doubt. |
| 4 | إِلَى | ilā | for, to | A preposition meaning "to": leave this and go to that. |
| 5 | مَا لَا يَرِيبُكَ | mā lā yarībuka | what does not make you doubt | مَا again means "that which". لَا denies the verb, which is the same verb as in row 3. |
</GrammarTable>
