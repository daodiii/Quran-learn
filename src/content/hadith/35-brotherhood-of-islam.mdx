## 1. What Not to Do

<ArabicExample
  arabic="لَا تَحَاسَدُوا، وَلَا تَنَاجَشُوا، وَلَا تَبَاغَضُوا، وَلَا تَدَابَرُوا، وَلَا يَبِعْ بَعْضُكُمْ عَلَى بَيْعِ بَعْضٍ، وَكُونُوا عِبَادَ اللَّهِ إِخْوَانًا"
  transliteration="lā taḥāsadū, wa-lā tanājashū, wa-lā tabāghaḍū, wa-lā tadābarū, wa-lā yabiʿ baʿḍukum ʿalā bayʿi baʿḍin, wa-kūnū ʿibāda llāhi ikhwānan"
  translation="Do not envy one another, do not bid up prices to deceive one another, do not hate one another, and do not turn your backs on one another. None of you should sell over the sale of another. Be servants of Allah, brothers."

### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | لَا تَحَاسَدُوا | lā taḥāsadū | do not envy one another | لَا here means "do not", and it makes the verb drop its final ن, leaving ـُوا ("you", plural). The long ā after the first letter is the pattern meaning "to each other". |
| 2 | وَلَا تَنَاجَشُوا | wa-lā tanājashū | and do not bid up prices to deceive | The same pattern. |
| 3 | وَلَا تَبَاغَضُوا | wa-lā tabāghaḍū | and do not hate one another | The same pattern. |
| 4 | وَلَا تَدَابَرُوا | wa-lā tadābarū | and do not turn your backs on one another | The same pattern. |
| 5 | وَلَا يَبِعْ | wa-lā yabiʿ | and let not sell | لَا means "do not". The verb ends in a silent letter, and the middle ي of يَبِيعُ has dropped. |
| 6 | بَعْضُكُمْ | baʿḍukum | some of you | Nominative (ـُ): it is who sells. The attached ـكُمْ means "you". |
| 7 | عَلَى بَيْعِ بَعْضٍ | ʿalā bayʿi baʿḍin | over the sale of another | عَلَى makes بَيْعِ genitive (ـِ), and بَعْضٍ is genitive (ـٍ) because it completes it. |
| 8 | وَكُونُوا | wa-kūnū | and be | A command to several people, from "to be". The ـُوا ending marks "you" (plural). |
| 9 | عِبَادَ اللَّهِ | ʿibāda llāhi | servants of Allah | Accusative (ـَ): it is the first thing "you be". اللَّهِ is genitive (ـِ) because it completes it. |
| 10 | إِخْوَانًا | ikhwānan | brothers | Accusative (ـًا): it is the second thing "you be". |
</GrammarTable>

### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | الْمُسْلِمُ أَخُو الْمُسْلِمِ | al-muslimu akhū l-muslimi | a Muslim is the brother of a Muslim | الْمُسْلِمُ is nominative (ـُ). أَخُو is nominative too, shown by a long ـو: أَخ is one of a few family words that show case with long vowels when they start a pair. الْمُسْلِمِ is genitive (ـِ) because it completes it. |
| 2 | لَا يَظْلِمُهُ | lā yaẓlimuhu | does not wrong him | Present tense, he, nominative (ـُ). The attached ـهُ means "him". |
| 3 | وَلَا يَخْذُلُهُ | wa-lā yakhdhuluhu | and does not abandon him | The same pattern. |
| 4 | وَلَا يَكْذِبُهُ | wa-lā yakdhibuhu | and does not lie to him | The same pattern. |
| 5 | وَلَا يَحْقِرُهُ | wa-lā yaḥqiruhu | and does not look down on him | The same pattern. |
| 6 | التَّقْوَى هَاهُنَا | at-taqwā hāhunā | piety is here | التَّقْوَى is nominative, but it ends in a long ā, so no vowel shows. هَاهُنَا means "right here", a word for place that never changes. |
| 7 | وَيُشِيرُ إِلَى صَدْرِهِ | wa-yushīru ilā ṣadrihi | and he pointed to his chest | Present tense, he, nominative. إِلَى makes صَدْرِ genitive (ـِ). The attached ـهِ means "his". |
| 8 | ثَلَاثَ مَرَّاتٍ | thalātha marrātin | three times | ثَلَاثَ is accusative (ـَ): it tells how many times. After a number from 3 to 10, the thing counted is plural and genitive, so مَرَّاتٍ ends in ـٍ. |
| 9 | بِحَسْبِ امْرِئٍ | bi-ḥasbi imriʾin | it is enough for a person | بِ is added before حَسْبِ ("enough"), which is how this phrase is made. امْرِئٍ is genitive (ـٍ) because it completes حَسْبِ. |
| 10 | مِنَ الشَّرِّ | mina sh-sharri | of evil | مِنْ takes a fatḥa before ال. It makes الشَّرِّ genitive (ـِ). |
| 11 | أَنْ يَحْقِرَ أَخَاهُ الْمُسْلِمَ | an yaḥqira akhāhu l-muslima | to look down on his Muslim brother | أَنْ makes the verb end in fatḥa. أَخَاهُ is accusative, shown by a long ـا. الْمُسْلِمَ describes it and takes the same case (ـَ). |
| 12 | كُلُّ الْمُسْلِمِ عَلَى الْمُسْلِمِ حَرَامٌ | kullu l-muslimi ʿalā l-muslimi ḥarāmun | every Muslim is forbidden to every Muslim | كُلُّ is nominative (ـُ), and الْمُسْلِمِ is genitive because it completes it. عَلَى makes the second الْمُسْلِمِ genitive. حَرَامٌ is nominative (ـٌ): the second part. |
| 13 | دَمُهُ وَمَالُهُ وَعِرْضُهُ | damuhu wa-māluhu wa-ʿirḍuhu | his blood, his wealth and his honor | All three are nominative (ـُ). They spell out what is forbidden. The attached ـهُ means "his". |
</GrammarTable>
