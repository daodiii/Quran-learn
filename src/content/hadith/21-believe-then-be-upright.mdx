### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | قُلْتُ | qultu | I said | Past tense with ـتُ ("I"). The middle letter of قَالَ drops before the silent ت, so it reads qul-. |
| 2 | يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ | yā rasūla llāhi | O Messenger of Allah | When the name being called is the first word of a pair ("messenger of…"), it ends in fatḥa. اللَّهِ is genitive (ـِ) because it completes رَسُولَ. |
| 3 | قُلْ | qul | say | A command to one person, ending in a silent letter. The middle و has dropped. |
| 4 | لِي | lī | to me | لِ plus ـي ("me"). |
| 5 | فِي الْإِسْلَامِ | fī l-islāmi | about Islam | فِي makes الْإِسْلَامِ genitive (ـِ). |
| 6 | قَوْلًا | qawlan | a statement | Accusative (ـًا): it is what he is asked to say. |
| 7 | لَا أَسْأَلُ | lā asʾalu | I will not ask | لَا denies. أَسْأَلُ is present tense, I, nominative (ـُ). The clause describes the statement. |
| 8 | عَنْهُ | ʿanhu | about it | عَنْ plus ـهُ ("it"), pointing to the statement. |
| 9 | أَحَدًا | aḥadan | anyone | Accusative (ـًا): it is who I ask. After a denial, أَحَد means "anyone". |
| 10 | غَيْرَكَ | ghayraka | other than you | Accusative (ـَ): it describes أَحَدًا, so it copies its case. The attached ـكَ means "you". |
| 11 | قَالَ | qāla | he said | Past tense, he. |
| 12 | قُلْ | qul | say | The same as row 3. |
| 13 | آمَنْتُ بِاللَّهِ | āmantu bi-llāhi | I believe in Allah | Past tense with ـتُ ("I"). بِ makes اللَّهِ genitive (ـِ). |
| 14 | ثُمَّ | thumma | then | Joins the next action to the first, with a gap between. |
| 15 | اسْتَقِمْ | istaqim | be upright | A command to one person, ending in a silent letter. The long vowel in the middle shortens to kasra. It means "to stay straight". |
</GrammarTable>
