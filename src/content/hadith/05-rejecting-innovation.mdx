## The Hadith

<ArabicExample
  arabic="مَنْ أَحْدَثَ فِي أَمْرِنَا هَذَا مَا لَيْسَ مِنْهُ فَهُوَ رَدٌّ"
  transliteration="man aḥdatha fī amrinā hādhā mā laysa minhu fa-huwa raddun"
  translation="Whoever introduces into this matter of ours something that is not part of it, it is rejected."
  reference="Hadith 5 · Narrated by Aisha"
/>

### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | مَنْ | man | whoever | Starts a condition: "if anyone…". It never changes its ending. |
| 2 | أَحْدَثَ | aḥdatha | introduced | Past tense, he, with a fixed fatḥa. The أَ at the start marks the pattern meaning "to bring something new into being". |
| 3 | فِي أَمْرِنَا | fī amrinā | in our matter | فِي makes أَمْرِ genitive (ـِ). The attached ـنَا means "our". "Our matter" means the religion. |
| 4 | هَذَا | hādhā | this | A pointing word. It describes أَمْرِنَا ("this matter"), and it never shows a case vowel. |
| 5 | مَا | mā | what, something | Means "that which": the thing that was introduced. It is what he introduced, and its ending never changes. |
| 6 | لَيْسَ | laysa | is not | A verb that means "is not", with a fixed fatḥa. It denies the statement that follows. |
| 7 | مِنْهُ | minhu | of it | مِنْ plus ـهُ ("it"), pointing back to the matter. "Not part of it". |
| 8 | فَهُوَ | fa-huwa | then it | فَ opens the result of the condition: "then…". هُوَ means "it" and is the subject. |
| 9 | رَدٌّ | raddun | rejected | Nominative (ـٌ): it is the second part of the statement. The word means "rejection", used to mean "something rejected". |
</GrammarTable>

## Muslim's Wording

<ArabicExample
  arabic="مَنْ عَمِلَ عَمَلًا لَيْسَ عَلَيْهِ أَمْرُنَا فَهُوَ رَدٌّ"
  transliteration="man ʿamila ʿamalan laysa ʿalayhi amrunā fa-huwa raddun"
  translation="Whoever does a deed that our affair does not support, it is rejected."
  reference="Hadith 5, in a narration of Muslim"
/>

### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | مَنْ عَمِلَ | man ʿamila | whoever does | The same condition word as before. عَمِلَ is past tense, he, with a fixed fatḥa. |
| 2 | عَمَلًا | ʿamalan | a deed | Accusative (ـًا): it is what he does. |
| 3 | لَيْسَ | laysa | is not | A verb meaning "is not", with a fixed fatḥa. |
| 4 | عَلَيْهِ | ʿalayhi | on it | عَلَى becomes عَلَيْ before ـهِ. It means "on the deed". This phrase comes first, and the thing that is not on the deed follows it. |
| 5 | أَمْرُنَا | amrunā | our affair | Nominative (ـُ): it is what "is not" on the deed. The attached ـنَا means "our". The whole clause describes the deed: "a deed that our affair is not on". |
| 6 | فَهُوَ رَدٌّ | fa-huwa raddun | then it is rejected | The result of the condition, built as in the first wording. |
</GrammarTable>
