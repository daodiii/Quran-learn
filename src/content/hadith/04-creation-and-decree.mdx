## The Whole Hadith

Below, the hadith is explained part by part.

## 1. The Stages in the Womb

<ArabicExample
  arabic="إِنَّ أَحَدَكُمْ يُجْمَعُ خَلْقُهُ فِي بَطْنِ أُمِّهِ أَرْبَعِينَ يَوْمًا نُطْفَةً، ثُمَّ يَكُونُ عَلَقَةً مِثْلَ ذَلِكَ، ثُمَّ يَكُونُ مُضْغَةً مِثْلَ ذَلِكَ"
  transliteration="inna aḥadakum yujmaʿu khalquhu fī baṭni ummihi arbaʿīna yawman nuṭfatan, thumma yakūnu ʿalaqatan mithla dhālika, thumma yakūnu muḍghatan mithla dhālik"
  translation="Each of you is put together in his mother's womb for forty days as a drop. Then he is a clot for the same length of time. Then he is a lump of flesh for the same length of time."
  reference="Hadith 4, part 1 · Narrated by Abdullah ibn Masud"
/>

### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | إِنَّ أَحَدَكُمْ | inna aḥadakum | indeed one of you | إِنَّ puts the next word in the accusative, so أَحَدَ ends in ـَ. The attached ـكُمْ means "you" (plural). |
| 2 | يُجْمَعُ | yujmaʿu | is gathered | Present tense in the passive: ḍamma on the first letter and fatḥa on the one before the last. It is nominative (ـُ). The one who gathers is not named. |
| 3 | خَلْقُهُ | khalquhu | his creation | Nominative (ـُ): with a passive verb, it takes the place of the doer. The attached ـهُ means "his". |
| 4 | فِي بَطْنِ أُمِّهِ | fī baṭni ummihi | in his mother's womb | فِي makes بَطْنِ genitive (ـِ). أُمِّ is genitive too, because it completes بَطْنِ. The attached ـهِ means "his". |
| 5 | أَرْبَعِينَ يَوْمًا | arbaʿīna yawman | forty days | أَرْبَعِينَ is accusative (ـِينَ) because it tells how long. After a number from 20 to 99, the thing counted is singular and accusative, so يَوْمًا ends in ـًا. |
| 6 | نُطْفَةً | nuṭfatan | as a drop | Accusative (ـًا): it says what state he is in. |
| 7 | ثُمَّ يَكُونُ | thumma yakūnu | then he becomes | ثُمَّ means "then". يَكُونُ is present tense, nominative (ـُ). It means "becomes". |
| 8 | عَلَقَةً | ʿalaqatan | a clot | Accusative (ـًا): it is what he becomes. |
| 9 | مِثْلَ ذَلِكَ | mithla dhālika | for the same length | مِثْلَ is accusative (ـَ) because it tells how long. ذَلِكَ means "that" and never shows a case vowel. |
| 10 | ثُمَّ يَكُونُ مُضْغَةً مِثْلَ ذَلِكَ | thumma yakūnu muḍghatan mithla dhālika | then he is a lump of flesh for the same length | The same pattern as rows 7–9. مُضْغَةً is accusative (ـًا). |
</GrammarTable>

## 2. The Angel and the Four Words

<ArabicExample
  arabic="ثُمَّ يُرْسَلُ إِلَيْهِ الْمَلَكُ فَيَنْفُخُ فِيهِ الرُّوحَ، وَيُؤْمَرُ بِأَرْبَعِ كَلِمَاتٍ: بِكَتْبِ رِزْقِهِ، وَأَجَلِهِ، وَعَمَلِهِ، وَشَقِيٌّ أَوْ سَعِيدٌ"
  transliteration="thumma yursalu ilayhi l-malaku fa-yanfukhu fīhi r-rūḥa, wa-yuʾmaru bi-arbaʿi kalimātin: bi-katbi rizqihi, wa-ajalihi, wa-ʿamalihi, wa-shaqiyyun aw saʿīdun"
  translation="Then the angel is sent to him and breathes the soul into him. He is commanded with four words: to write down his provision, his lifespan and his deeds, and whether he will be wretched or happy."
  reference="Hadith 4, part 2"
/>

### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | ثُمَّ يُرْسَلُ | thumma yursalu | then is sent | Present tense in the passive, nominative (ـُ). The one who sends is not named. |
| 2 | إِلَيْهِ | ilayhi | to him | إِلَى becomes إِلَيْ before ـهِ. |
| 3 | الْمَلَكُ | al-malaku | the angel | Nominative (ـُ): with a passive verb, it takes the place of the doer. |
| 4 | فَيَنْفُخُ | fa-yanfukhu | and he breathes | فَ means "and then". The verb is present tense, he, nominative (ـُ). |
| 5 | فِيهِ | fīhi | into him | فِي plus ـهِ ("him"). |
| 6 | الرُّوحَ | ar-rūḥa | the soul | Accusative (ـَ): it is what is breathed. |
| 7 | وَيُؤْمَرُ | wa-yuʾmaru | and he is commanded | Present tense in the passive, nominative (ـُ). The angel is the one commanded. |
| 8 | بِأَرْبَعِ كَلِمَاتٍ | bi-arbaʿi kalimātin | with four words | بِ makes أَرْبَعِ genitive (ـِ). After a number from 3 to 10, the thing counted is plural and genitive, so كَلِمَاتٍ ends in ـٍ. |
| 9 | بِكَتْبِ | bi-katbi | to write down | بِ makes كَتْبِ genitive (ـِ). The phrase explains what the four words are. |
| 10 | رِزْقِهِ وَأَجَلِهِ وَعَمَلِهِ | rizqihi wa-ajalihi wa-ʿamalihi | his provision, his lifespan and his deeds | Each is genitive (ـِ). The first completes كَتْبِ, and the other two are joined to it. The attached ـهِ means "his". |
| 11 | وَشَقِيٌّ أَوْ سَعِيدٌ | wa-shaqiyyun aw saʿīdun | and wretched or happy | Both are nominative (ـٌ). A statement is understood: "and [he will be] wretched or happy". |
</GrammarTable>

## 3. The Deeds of Paradise, Then the Fire

<ArabicExample
  arabic="فَوَاللَّهِ الَّذِي لَا إِلَهَ غَيْرُهُ إِنَّ أَحَدَكُمْ لَيَعْمَلُ بِعَمَلِ أَهْلِ الْجَنَّةِ حَتَّى مَا يَكُونُ بَيْنَهُ وَبَيْنَهَا إِلَّا ذِرَاعٌ، فَيَسْبِقُ عَلَيْهِ الْكِتَابُ فَيَعْمَلُ بِعَمَلِ أَهْلِ النَّارِ فَيَدْخُلُهَا"
  transliteration="fa-wa-llāhi lladhī lā ilāha ghayruhu inna aḥadakum la-yaʿmalu bi-ʿamali ahli l-jannati ḥattā mā yakūnu baynahu wa-baynahā illā dhirāʿun, fa-yasbiqu ʿalayhi l-kitābu fa-yaʿmalu bi-ʿamali ahli n-nāri fa-yadkhuluhā"
  translation="By Allah, other than whom there is no god, one of you may do the deeds of the people of Paradise until there is only an arm's length between him and it. Then what is written overtakes him, and he does the deeds of the people of the Fire, and he enters it."
  reference="Hadith 4, part 3"
/>

### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | فَوَاللَّهِ | fa-wa-llāhi | so by Allah | فَ means "so". وَ here means "by", as in an oath. It makes اللَّهِ genitive (ـِ). |
| 2 | الَّذِي | alladhī | who, the one who | A word meaning "the one who". It never changes its ending. Here it describes Allah: "by Allah, the one who…". |
| 3 | لَا إِلَهَ غَيْرُهُ | lā ilāha ghayruhu | there is no god other than Him | لَا denies the whole kind, so إِلَهَ stays on fatḥa with no tanwīn. غَيْرُهُ is nominative (ـُ): it stands for the missing "exists". The attached ـهُ means "Him". |
| 4 | إِنَّ أَحَدَكُمْ | inna aḥadakum | indeed one of you | إِنَّ makes أَحَدَ accusative (ـَ). |
| 5 | لَيَعْمَلُ | la-yaʿmalu | surely does | لَ adds strength: "surely". يَعْمَلُ is present tense, he, nominative (ـُ). |
| 6 | بِعَمَلِ أَهْلِ الْجَنَّةِ | bi-ʿamali ahli l-jannati | the deeds of the people of Paradise | بِ makes عَمَلِ genitive (ـِ). أَهْلِ is genitive because it completes عَمَلِ, and الْجَنَّةِ is genitive because it completes أَهْلِ. |
| 7 | حَتَّى مَا يَكُونُ | ḥattā mā yakūnu | until there is… | حَتَّى here starts a new statement: "until it comes to this". مَا is added for emphasis. يَكُونُ is nominative (ـُ). Some printings read يَكُونَ with a fatḥa. |
| 8 | بَيْنَهُ وَبَيْنَهَا | baynahu wa-baynahā | between him and it | بَيْنَ is a word for place, shown with fatḥa. The attached ـهُ means "him", and ـهَا means "it", feminine because it means Paradise. |
| 9 | إِلَّا ذِرَاعٌ | illā dhirāʿun | except an arm's length | إِلَّا means "except". ذِرَاعٌ is nominative (ـٌ). In a sentence with a denial, the word after إِلَّا takes the role of the word it picks out, here the thing that "is". |
| 10 | فَيَسْبِقُ | fa-yasbiqu | then it overtakes | فَ means "then". The verb is present tense, nominative (ـُ). |
| 11 | عَلَيْهِ | ʿalayhi | him | عَلَى becomes عَلَيْ before ـهِ. "Overtakes him". |
| 12 | الْكِتَابُ | al-kitābu | what is written | Nominative (ـُ): it is what does the overtaking. |
| 13 | فَيَعْمَلُ بِعَمَلِ أَهْلِ النَّارِ | fa-yaʿmalu bi-ʿamali ahli n-nāri | and he does the deeds of the people of the Fire | The same pattern as row 6. النَّارِ is genitive (ـِ) because it completes أَهْلِ. |
| 14 | فَيَدْخُلُهَا | fa-yadkhuluhā | and he enters it | The verb is present tense, nominative (ـُ). The attached ـهَا means "it", the Fire. |
</GrammarTable>
