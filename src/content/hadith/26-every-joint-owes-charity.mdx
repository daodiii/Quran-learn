### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | كُلُّ سُلَامَى | kullu sulāmā | every joint | كُلُّ is nominative (ـُ): it is what the statement is about. سُلَامَى is genitive because it completes كُلُّ, but it ends in a long ā, so no vowel shows. |
| 2 | مِنَ النَّاسِ | mina n-nāsi | of the people | مِنْ takes a fatḥa before ال. It makes النَّاسِ genitive (ـِ). |
| 3 | عَلَيْهِ | ʿalayhi | on it | عَلَى becomes عَلَيْ before ـهِ. |
| 4 | صَدَقَةٌ | ṣadaqatun | charity | Nominative (ـٌ): it is what is owed, put after the phrase "on it". |
| 5 | كُلَّ يَوْمٍ | kulla yawmin | every day | كُلَّ is accusative (ـَ) because it tells when. يَوْمٍ is genitive (ـٍ) because it completes كُلَّ. |
| 6 | تَطْلُعُ فِيهِ الشَّمْسُ | taṭluʿu fīhi sh-shamsu | on which the sun rises | تَطْلُعُ is present tense, she, nominative (ـُ). الشَّمْسُ is nominative (ـُ): it is what rises. The clause describes the day. فِيهِ means "in it". |
| 7 | تَعْدِلُ | taʿdilu | you judge fairly | Present tense, you, nominative (ـُ). |
| 8 | بَيْنَ اثْنَيْنِ | bayna ithnayni | between two | بَيْنَ is a word for place, shown with fatḥa. اثْنَيْنِ is genitive, and the ـَيْنِ ending shows a pair in the genitive. |
| 9 | صَدَقَةٌ | ṣadaqatun | charity | Nominative (ـٌ): the second part of the statement. |
| 10 | وَتُعِينُ الرَّجُلَ | wa-tuʿīnu r-rajula | and you help the man | Present tense, you, nominative (ـُ). الرَّجُلَ is accusative (ـَ): it is who you help. |
| 11 | فِي دَابَّتِهِ | fī dābbatihi | with his mount | فِي makes دَابَّةِ genitive (ـِ). The attached ـهِ means "his". |
| 12 | فَتَحْمِلُهُ عَلَيْهَا | fa-taḥmiluhu ʿalayhā | and you lift him onto it | فَ means "and then". The attached ـهُ means "him", and ـهَا means "it", feminine because the mount is. |
| 13 | أَوْ تَرْفَعُ لَهُ عَلَيْهَا مَتَاعَهُ | aw tarfaʿu lahu ʿalayhā matāʿahu | or you lift his luggage onto it | مَتَاعَهُ is accusative (ـَ): it is what you lift. The attached ـهُ means "his". |
| 14 | وَالْكَلِمَةُ الطَّيِّبَةُ صَدَقَةٌ | wa-l-kalimatu ṭ-ṭayyibatu ṣadaqatun | and a good word is charity | الْكَلِمَةُ is nominative (ـُ). الطَّيِّبَةُ describes it and takes the same case. صَدَقَةٌ is nominative (ـٌ). |
| 15 | وَبِكُلِّ خُطْوَةٍ | wa-bi-kulli khuṭwatin | and with every step | بِ makes كُلِّ genitive (ـِ), and خُطْوَةٍ is genitive because it completes it. |
| 16 | تَمْشِيهَا | tamshīhā | you walk it | Present tense, you. The attached ـهَا means "it", the step. The clause describes the step. |
| 17 | إِلَى الصَّلَاةِ | ilā ṣ-ṣalāti | to the prayer | إِلَى makes الصَّلَاةِ genitive (ـِ). |
| 18 | وَتُمِيطُ الْأَذَى | wa-tumīṭu l-adhā | and you remove the harm | الْأَذَى is accusative: it is what you remove, but it ends in a long ā, so no vowel shows. |
| 19 | عَنِ الطَّرِيقِ | ʿani ṭ-ṭarīqi | from the road | عَنْ takes a kasra before ال. It makes الطَّرِيقِ genitive (ـِ). |
</GrammarTable>
