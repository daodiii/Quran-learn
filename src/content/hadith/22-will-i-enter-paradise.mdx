### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | أَنَّ رَجُلًا | anna rajulan | that a man | أَنَّ means "that" and makes the word after it accusative, so رَجُلًا ends in ـًا. The tanwīn means the man is not named. |
| 2 | سَأَلَ | saʾala | asked | Past tense, he, with a fixed fatḥa. |
| 3 | رَسُولَ اللَّهِ | rasūla llāhi | the Messenger of Allah | رَسُولَ is accusative (ـَ): he is the one asked. اللَّهِ is genitive (ـِ) because it completes رَسُولَ. |
| 4 | فَقَالَ | fa-qāla | and he said | فَ means "and then". Past tense, he. |
| 5 | أَرَأَيْتَ | a-raʾayta | tell me | أَ starts a question. رَأَيْتَ is past tense with ـتَ ("you"). It literally means "have you seen", and it is used to mean "tell me". |
| 6 | إِذَا صَلَّيْتُ | idhā ṣallaytu | if I pray | إِذَا means "if" or "when". صَلَّيْتُ is past tense with ـتُ ("I"). |
| 7 | الْمَكْتُوبَاتِ | al-maktūbāti | the obligatory prayers | Accusative: it is what I pray. A feminine plural ends in ـَاتِ for the accusative, with a kasra. It means "the ones written as obligatory". |
| 8 | وَصُمْتُ | wa-ṣumtu | and I fast | Past tense with ـتُ ("I"). The long vowel of the verb shortens before the silent ت. |
| 9 | رَمَضَانَ | Ramaḍāna | Ramadan | Accusative (ـَ), with no tanwīn, because it is a name ending in ـان. |
| 10 | وَأَحْلَلْتُ | wa-aḥlaltu | and I treat as lawful | Past tense with ـتُ ("I"). The verb's two ل are written apart because the silent ت follows. The أَ at the start marks the pattern meaning "to declare lawful". |
| 11 | الْحَلَالَ | al-ḥalāla | the lawful | Accusative (ـَ): it is what I declare lawful. |
| 12 | وَحَرَّمْتُ الْحَرَامَ | wa-ḥarramtu l-ḥarāma | and I treat the unlawful as unlawful | The same pattern: past tense with ـتُ ("I"). The doubled ر marks the pattern meaning "to declare forbidden". الْحَرَامَ is accusative (ـَ). |
| 13 | وَلَمْ أَزِدْ | wa-lam azid | and I do not add | لَمْ makes the verb end in a silent letter. The middle ي of the verb drops, so أَزِيدُ becomes أَزِدْ. |
| 14 | عَلَى ذَلِكَ | ʿalā dhālika | to that | عَلَى takes a genitive, but ذَلِكَ never shows a case vowel. |
| 15 | شَيْئًا | shayʾan | anything | Accusative (ـًا): it is what I add. |
| 16 | أَأَدْخُلُ | a-adkhulu | will I enter | The first أَ starts a question, and the second means "I". Present tense, I, nominative (ـُ). |
| 17 | الْجَنَّةَ | al-jannata | Paradise | Accusative (ـَ): it is where I enter. |
| 18 | قَالَ نَعَمْ | qāla naʿam | he said: yes | نَعَمْ means "yes" and ends in a silent letter that never changes. |
</GrammarTable>
