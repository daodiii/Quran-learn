Below, the hadith is explained part by part.

## 1. Relieving, Easing and Covering

<ArabicExample
  arabic="مَنْ نَفَّسَ عَنْ مُؤْمِنٍ كُرْبَةً مِنْ كُرَبِ الدُّنْيَا نَفَّسَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ كُرْبَةً مِنْ كُرَبِ يَوْمِ الْقِيَامَةِ، وَمَنْ يَسَّرَ عَلَى مُعْسِرٍ يَسَّرَ اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ فِي الدُّنْيَا وَالْآخِرَةِ، وَمَنْ سَتَرَ مُسْلِمًا سَتَرَهُ اللَّهُ فِي الدُّنْيَا وَالْآخِرَةِ"
  transliteration="man naffasa ʿan muʾminin kurbatan min kurabi d-dunyā naffasa llāhu ʿanhu kurbatan min kurabi yawmi l-qiyāmati, wa-man yassara ʿalā muʿsirin yassara llāhu ʿalayhi fī d-dunyā wa-l-ākhirati, wa-man satara muslimān satarahu llāhu fī d-dunyā wa-l-ākhirati"
  translation="Whoever relieves a believer of a hardship from the hardships of this world, Allah will relieve them of a hardship from the hardships of the Day of Resurrection. Whoever eases the situation of someone in difficulty, Allah will ease it for them in this world and the next. Whoever covers a Muslim's faults, Allah will cover theirs in this world and the next."
  reference="Hadith 36, part 1 · Narrated by Abu Hurayra"
/>

### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | مَنْ نَفَّسَ | man naffasa | whoever relieves | مَنْ starts a condition. نَفَّسَ is past tense, he, with a fixed fatḥa. The doubled ف marks the pattern meaning "to relieve". |
| 2 | عَنْ مُؤْمِنٍ | ʿan muʾminin | of a believer | عَنْ makes مُؤْمِنٍ genitive (ـٍ). |
| 3 | كُرْبَةً | kurbatan | a hardship | Accusative (ـًا): it is what he relieves. |
| 4 | مِنْ كُرَبِ الدُّنْيَا | min kurabi d-dunyā | from the hardships of this world | مِنْ makes كُرَبِ genitive (ـِ). الدُّنْيَا is genitive because it completes it, but it ends in a long ā, so no vowel shows. |
| 5 | نَفَّسَ اللَّهُ | naffasa llāhu | Allah will relieve | The result of the condition. Past tense, he. اللَّهُ is nominative (ـُ): He is who relieves. |
| 6 | عَنْهُ | ʿanhu | of him | عَنْ plus ـهُ ("him"). |
| 7 | كُرْبَةً مِنْ كُرَبِ يَوْمِ الْقِيَامَةِ | kurbatan min kurabi yawmi l-qiyāmati | a hardship from the hardships of the Day of Resurrection | The same pattern as rows 3 and 4. Each word completes the one before it and is genitive (ـِ). |
| 8 | وَمَنْ يَسَّرَ عَلَى مُعْسِرٍ | wa-man yassara ʿalā muʿsirin | and whoever eases things for one in difficulty | يَسَّرَ is past tense, he, with a doubled س. عَلَى makes مُعْسِرٍ genitive (ـٍ). |
| 9 | يَسَّرَ اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ | yassara llāhu ʿalayhi | Allah will ease things for him | The result. عَلَى becomes عَلَيْ before ـهِ. |
| 10 | فِي الدُّنْيَا وَالْآخِرَةِ | fī d-dunyā wa-l-ākhirati | in this world and the next | فِي makes both words genitive, but الدُّنْيَا ends in a long ā, so no vowel shows. الْآخِرَةِ shows ـِ. |
| 11 | وَمَنْ سَتَرَ مُسْلِمًا | wa-man satara muslimān | and whoever covers a Muslim | سَتَرَ is past tense, he. مُسْلِمًا is accusative (ـًا): he is who is covered. |
| 12 | سَتَرَهُ اللَّهُ | satarahu llāhu | Allah will cover him | The result. The attached ـهُ means "him". |
</GrammarTable>

## 2. Helping and Seeking Knowledge

<ArabicExample
  arabic="وَاللَّهُ فِي عَوْنِ الْعَبْدِ مَا كَانَ الْعَبْدُ فِي عَوْنِ أَخِيهِ، وَمَنْ سَلَكَ طَرِيقًا يَلْتَمِسُ فِيهِ عِلْمًا سَهَّلَ اللَّهُ لَهُ بِهِ طَرِيقًا إِلَى الْجَنَّةِ"
  transliteration="wa-llāhu fī ʿawni l-ʿabdi mā kāna l-ʿabdu fī ʿawni akhīhi, wa-man salaka ṭarīqan yaltamisu fīhi ʿilman sahhala llāhu lahu bihi ṭarīqan ilā l-jannati"
  translation="Allah is helping a servant as long as that servant is helping their brother. Whoever takes a path in which they seek knowledge, Allah will make easy for them, because of it, a path to Paradise."
  reference="Hadith 36, part 2"
/>

### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | وَاللَّهُ فِي عَوْنِ الْعَبْدِ | wa-llāhu fī ʿawni l-ʿabdi | and Allah is in the help of the servant | اللَّهُ is nominative (ـُ): what the statement is about. فِي makes عَوْنِ genitive (ـِ), and الْعَبْدِ is genitive because it completes it. The phrase is the second part of the statement. |
| 2 | مَا كَانَ الْعَبْدُ | mā kāna l-ʿabdu | as long as the servant is | Here مَا means "as long as". كَانَ is past tense, he. الْعَبْدُ is nominative (ـُ). |
| 3 | فِي عَوْنِ أَخِيهِ | fī ʿawni akhīhi | in the help of his brother | أَخِيهِ is genitive. أَخ is one of a few family words that show their case with a long vowel when they take a pronoun: the genitive is ـِي. |
| 4 | وَمَنْ سَلَكَ طَرِيقًا | wa-man salaka ṭarīqan | and whoever takes a path | سَلَكَ is past tense, he. طَرِيقًا is accusative (ـًا): it is what he takes. |
| 5 | يَلْتَمِسُ فِيهِ عِلْمًا | yaltamisu fīhi ʿilman | seeking knowledge in it | Present tense, he, nominative (ـُ). The clause describes the path. عِلْمًا is accusative (ـًا): it is what he seeks. |
| 6 | سَهَّلَ اللَّهُ لَهُ | sahhala llāhu lahu | Allah makes easy for him | The result. The doubled ه marks the pattern meaning "to make easy". لِ takes a fatḥa before ـهُ. |
| 7 | بِهِ | bihi | because of it | بِ plus ـهِ ("it"), pointing back to seeking knowledge. |
| 8 | طَرِيقًا إِلَى الْجَنَّةِ | ṭarīqan ilā l-jannati | a path to Paradise | طَرِيقًا is accusative (ـًا): it is what He makes easy. إِلَى makes الْجَنَّةِ genitive (ـِ). |
</GrammarTable>

## 3. Gathering for the Book of Allah

<ArabicExample
  arabic="وَمَا اجْتَمَعَ قَوْمٌ فِي بَيْتٍ مِنْ بُيُوتِ اللَّهِ يَتْلُونَ كِتَابَ اللَّهِ وَيَتَدَارَسُونَهُ بَيْنَهُمْ إِلَّا نَزَلَتْ عَلَيْهِمُ السَّكِينَةُ، وَغَشِيَتْهُمُ الرَّحْمَةُ، وَحَفَّتْهُمُ الْمَلَائِكَةُ، وَذَكَرَهُمُ اللَّهُ فِيمَنْ عِنْدَهُ، وَمَنْ بَطَّأَ بِهِ عَمَلُهُ لَمْ يُسْرِعْ بِهِ نَسَبُهُ"
  transliteration="wa-mā jtamaʿa qawmun fī baytin min buyūti llāhi yatlūna kitāba llāhi wa-yatadārasūnahu baynahum illā nazalat ʿalayhimu s-sakīnatu, wa-ghashiyat-humu r-raḥmatu, wa-ḥaffat-humu l-malāʾikatu, wa-dhakarahumu llāhu fī-man ʿindahu, wa-man baṭṭaʾa bihi ʿamaluhu lam yusriʿ bihi nasabuhu"
  translation="No group gathers in one of the houses of Allah, reciting the Book of Allah and studying it among themselves, except that tranquility descends on them, mercy covers them, the angels surround them, and Allah mentions them among those with Him. And whoever is slowed down by their deeds will not be sped up by their lineage."
  reference="Hadith 36, part 3"
/>

### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | وَمَا اجْتَمَعَ قَوْمٌ | wa-mā jtamaʿa qawmun | and no group gathers | Here مَا means "not". اجْتَمَعَ is past tense, he. قَوْمٌ is nominative (ـٌ): they are who gather. |
| 2 | فِي بَيْتٍ مِنْ بُيُوتِ اللَّهِ | fī baytin min buyūti llāhi | in a house of the houses of Allah | فِي makes بَيْتٍ genitive (ـٍ). مِنْ makes بُيُوتِ genitive (ـِ), and اللَّهِ is genitive because it completes it. |
| 3 | يَتْلُونَ كِتَابَ اللَّهِ | yatlūna kitāba llāhi | reciting the Book of Allah | Present tense, they (ـُونَ). It describes the group. كِتَابَ is accusative (ـَ): it is what they recite. |
| 4 | وَيَتَدَارَسُونَهُ بَيْنَهُمْ | wa-yatadārasūnahu baynahum | and studying it among themselves | The long ā marks the pattern meaning "with each other". The attached ـهُ means "it". بَيْنَ is a word for place, shown with fatḥa. |
| 5 | إِلَّا نَزَلَتْ | illā nazalat | except that descended | إِلَّا means "except". نَزَلَتْ is past tense, she, because what descends is feminine. |
| 6 | عَلَيْهِمُ السَّكِينَةُ | ʿalayhimu s-sakīnatu | on them tranquility | عَلَيْهِمْ takes a ḍamma before ال, so it reads ʿalayhimu. السَّكِينَةُ is nominative (ـُ): it is what descends. |
| 7 | وَغَشِيَتْهُمُ الرَّحْمَةُ | wa-ghashiyat-humu r-raḥmatu | and mercy covers them | Past tense, she. The attached ـهُمُ means "them". الرَّحْمَةُ is nominative (ـُ). |
| 8 | وَحَفَّتْهُمُ الْمَلَائِكَةُ | wa-ḥaffat-humu l-malāʾikatu | and the angels surround them | The same pattern. الْمَلَائِكَةُ is nominative (ـُ). |
| 9 | وَذَكَرَهُمُ اللَّهُ | wa-dhakarahumu llāhu | and Allah mentions them | Past tense, he. اللَّهُ is nominative (ـُ). |
| 10 | فِيمَنْ عِنْدَهُ | fī-man ʿindahu | among those with Him | فِي plus مَنْ ("those who"). عِنْدَ is accusative (ـَ), a word for place. The attached ـهُ means "Him". |
| 11 | وَمَنْ بَطَّأَ بِهِ عَمَلُهُ | wa-man baṭṭaʾa bihi ʿamaluhu | and whoever is slowed by his deeds | بَطَّأَ is past tense, he, with a doubled ط, and means "to slow down". عَمَلُهُ is nominative (ـُ): it is what slows him. |
| 12 | لَمْ يُسْرِعْ بِهِ نَسَبُهُ | lam yusriʿ bihi nasabuhu | his lineage will not speed him up | لَمْ makes the verb end in a silent letter. نَسَبُهُ is nominative (ـُ): it is what would speed him. |
</GrammarTable>
