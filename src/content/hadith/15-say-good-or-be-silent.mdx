### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | مَنْ | man | whoever | Starts a condition: "if anyone…". Its ending never changes. |
| 2 | كَانَ | kāna | is | Past tense of "to be", he, with a fixed fatḥa. Joined to a present verb it describes a lasting state: "whoever is someone who believes". |
| 3 | يُؤْمِنُ | yuʾminu | believes | Present tense, he, nominative (ـُ). It completes كَانَ. |
| 4 | بِاللَّهِ | bi-llāhi | in Allah | بِ makes اللَّهِ genitive (ـِ). |
| 5 | وَالْيَوْمِ الْآخِرِ | wa-l-yawmi l-ākhiri | and the Last Day | الْيَوْمِ is genitive (ـِ) because وَ joins it to اللَّهِ. الْآخِرِ describes it and takes the same case. |
| 6 | فَلْيَقُلْ | fa-l-yaqul | then let him say | فَ opens the result of the condition. لِ in front of a present verb means "let him", and after فَ it loses its vowel. The verb ends in a silent letter, and its middle و drops, so يَقُولُ becomes يَقُلْ. |
| 7 | خَيْرًا | khayran | something good | Accusative (ـًا): it is what he says. |
| 8 | أَوْ | aw | or | Gives a choice between two things. |
| 9 | لِيَصْمُتْ | li-yaṣmut | let him keep silent | لِ plus a present verb means "let him". The verb ends in a silent letter. |
| 10 | فَلْيُكْرِمْ | fa-l-yukrim | then let him honor | The same pattern as row 6, from the verb "to honor". It ends in a silent letter. |
| 11 | جَارَهُ | jārahu | his neighbor | Accusative (ـَ): it is who he honors. The attached ـهُ means "his". |
| 12 | ضَيْفَهُ | ḍayfahu | his guest | Accusative (ـَ), in the same way. |
</GrammarTable>
