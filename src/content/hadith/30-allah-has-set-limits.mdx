### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | إِنَّ اللَّهَ تَعَالَى | inna llāha taʿālā | indeed Allah, the Most High | إِنَّ makes اللَّهَ accusative (ـَ). تَعَالَى is a phrase of praise that ends in a long ā. |
| 2 | فَرَضَ | faraḍa | has laid down | Past tense, he, with a fixed fatḥa. |
| 3 | فَرَائِضَ | farāʾiḍa | obligations | Accusative: it is what He laid down. It ends in ـَ with no tanwīn, because words of this plural pattern take no tanwīn. |
| 4 | فَلَا تُضَيِّعُوهَا | fa-lā tuḍayyiʿūhā | so do not neglect them | فَ means "so". لَا here means "do not", and it makes the verb drop its final ن, leaving ـُوا. The doubled ي marks the pattern meaning "to cause to be lost". The attached ـهَا means "them". |
| 5 | وَحَدَّ حُدُودًا | wa-ḥadda ḥudūdan | and set limits | حَدَّ is past tense, he, with a doubled د. حُدُودًا is accusative (ـًا): it is what He set. |
| 6 | فَلَا تَعْتَدُوهَا | fa-lā taʿtadūhā | so do not overstep them | The same pattern as row 4. The attached ـهَا means "them". |
| 7 | وَحَرَّمَ أَشْيَاءَ | wa-ḥarrama ashyāʾa | and forbade things | Past tense, he. أَشْيَاءَ is accusative (ـَ) with no tanwīn. |
| 8 | فَلَا تَنْتَهِكُوهَا | fa-lā tantahikūhā | so do not violate them | The same pattern as row 4. |
| 9 | وَسَكَتَ عَنْ أَشْيَاءَ | wa-sakata ʿan ashyāʾa | and stayed silent about things | سَكَتَ is past tense, he. عَنْ makes أَشْيَاءَ genitive, but it takes no tanwīn, so it shows fatḥa. |
| 10 | رَحْمَةً لَكُمْ | raḥmatan lakum | out of mercy for you | رَحْمَةً is accusative (ـًا): it says why. لِ takes a fatḥa before ـكُمْ. |
| 11 | غَيْرَ نِسْيَانٍ | ghayra nisyānin | not out of forgetfulness | غَيْرَ is accusative (ـَ): it describes how He stayed silent. نِسْيَانٍ is genitive (ـٍ) because it completes غَيْرَ. |
| 12 | فَلَا تَبْحَثُوا عَنْهَا | fa-lā tabḥathū ʿanhā | so do not search for them | The same pattern as row 4. عَنْ plus ـهَا ("them"). |
</GrammarTable>
