### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | مَنْ رَأَى | man raʾā | whoever sees | مَنْ starts a condition. رَأَى is past tense, he, and ends in a long ā. |
| 2 | مِنْكُمْ | minkum | among you | مِنْ plus ـكُمْ ("you"). |
| 3 | مُنْكَرًا | munkaran | something wrong | Accusative (ـًا): it is what he sees. |
| 4 | فَلْيُغَيِّرْهُ | fa-l-yughayyirhu | then let him change it | فَ opens the result. لِ in front of a present verb means "let him", and after فَ it loses its vowel. The verb ends in a silent letter. The doubled ي marks the pattern meaning "to change". The attached ـهُ means "it". |
| 5 | بِيَدِهِ | bi-yadihi | with his hand | بِ makes يَدِ genitive (ـِ). The attached ـهِ means "his". |
| 6 | فَإِنْ لَمْ يَسْتَطِعْ | fa-in lam yastaṭiʿ | and if he cannot | إِنْ means "if". لَمْ makes the verb end in a silent letter, and the long vowel in the middle of يَسْتَطِيعُ shortens to a kasra. |
| 7 | فَبِلِسَانِهِ | fa-bi-lisānihi | then with his tongue | فَ means "then". بِ makes لِسَانِ genitive (ـِ). The verb "let him change it" is understood. |
| 8 | فَبِقَلْبِهِ | fa-bi-qalbihi | then with his heart | The same pattern. |
| 9 | وَذَلِكَ | wa-dhālika | and that | A pointing word. It is the subject, and it never shows a case vowel. |
| 10 | أَضْعَفُ الْإِيمَانِ | aḍʿafu l-īmāni | the weakest of faith | أَضْعَفُ is nominative (ـُ): the second part of the statement. It takes no tanwīn, because it is on the "most…" pattern. الْإِيمَانِ is genitive (ـِ) because it completes it. |
</GrammarTable>
