Below, the hadith is explained part by part.

## 1. Guard Allah

<ArabicExample
  arabic="كُنْتُ خَلْفَ النَّبِيِّ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ يَوْمًا، فَقَالَ لِي: يَا غُلَامُ، إِنِّي أُعَلِّمُكَ كَلِمَاتٍ: احْفَظِ اللَّهَ يَحْفَظْكَ، احْفَظِ اللَّهَ تَجِدْهُ تُجَاهَكَ"
  transliteration="kuntu khalfa n-nabiyyi ṣallā llāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam yawman, fa-qāla lī: yā ghulāmu, innī uʿallimuka kalimātin: iḥfaẓi llāha yaḥfaẓka, iḥfaẓi llāha tajidhu tujāhaka"
  translation="One day I was riding behind the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, and he said to me: “Young man, I will teach you some words. Guard Allah and He will guard you. Guard Allah and you will find Him in front of you.”"
  reference="Hadith 19, part 1 · Narrated by Abdullah ibn Abbas"
/>

### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | كُنْتُ | kuntu | I was | Past tense of "to be" with ـتُ ("I"). The long vowel of كَانَ drops before the silent ت. |
| 2 | خَلْفَ النَّبِيِّ | khalfa n-nabiyyi | behind the Prophet | خَلْفَ is a word for place, shown with fatḥa. النَّبِيِّ is genitive (ـِ) because it completes it. |
| 3 | يَوْمًا | yawman | one day | Accusative (ـًا): it tells when. |
| 4 | فَقَالَ لِي | fa-qāla lī | and he said to me | فَ means "and then". قَالَ is past tense, he. لِ plus ـي means "to me". |
| 5 | يَا غُلَامُ | yā ghulāmu | O boy | A single name being called ends in ḍamma and takes no tanwīn. |
| 6 | إِنِّي | innī | indeed I | إِنَّ plus ـي ("I"). The attached ـي takes the place of the word that follows إِنَّ in the accusative. |
| 7 | أُعَلِّمُكَ | uʿallimuka | I will teach you | Present tense, I, nominative (ـُ). The doubled ل marks the pattern meaning "to teach". The attached ـكَ means "you". |
| 8 | كَلِمَاتٍ | kalimātin | some words | Accusative: it is what I will teach. A feminine plural ends in ـَاتٍ in the accusative, with a kasra. |
| 9 | احْفَظِ | iḥfaẓi | guard | A command to one person, ending in a silent letter that takes a kasra before the ال that follows. |
| 10 | اللَّهَ | Allāha | Allah | Accusative (ـَ): it is who you guard. |
| 11 | يَحْفَظْكَ | yaḥfaẓka | He will guard you | Present tense, he, ending in a silent letter because it is the result of a command: "guard Allah, and He will guard you". The attached ـكَ means "you". |
| 12 | تَجِدْهُ | tajidhu | you will find Him | Present tense, you, ending in a silent letter for the same reason. The attached ـهُ means "Him". |
| 13 | تُجَاهَكَ | tujāhaka | in front of you | Accusative (ـَ): a word for place. The attached ـكَ means "you". |
</GrammarTable>

## 2. Asking and Seeking Help

<ArabicExample
  arabic="إِذَا سَأَلْتَ فَاسْأَلِ اللَّهَ، وَإِذَا اسْتَعَنْتَ فَاسْتَعِنْ بِاللَّهِ"
  transliteration="idhā saʾalta fa-sʾali llāha, wa-idhā staʿanta fa-staʿin bi-llāhi"
  translation="When you ask, ask Allah. When you seek help, seek help from Allah."
  reference="Hadith 19, part 2"
/>

### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | إِذَا سَأَلْتَ | idhā saʾalta | when you ask | إِذَا means "when". سَأَلْتَ is past tense with ـتَ ("you", masculine, singular). |
| 2 | فَاسْأَلِ | fa-sʾali | then ask | فَ opens the result. A command to one person, ending in a silent letter that takes a kasra before ال. |
| 3 | اللَّهَ | Allāha | Allah | Accusative (ـَ): it is who you ask. |
| 4 | وَإِذَا اسْتَعَنْتَ | wa-idhā staʿanta | and when you seek help | The same pattern. The long vowel in the middle of the verb shortens before the silent ت. |
| 5 | فَاسْتَعِنْ | fa-staʿin | then seek help | A command to one person, ending in a silent letter. The long vowel in the middle shortens to kasra. |
| 6 | بِاللَّهِ | bi-llāhi | from Allah | بِ makes اللَّهِ genitive (ـِ). |
</GrammarTable>

## 3. What Is Written

<ArabicExample
  arabic="وَاعْلَمْ أَنَّ الْأُمَّةَ لَوِ اجْتَمَعَتْ عَلَى أَنْ يَنْفَعُوكَ بِشَيْءٍ لَمْ يَنْفَعُوكَ إِلَّا بِشَيْءٍ قَدْ كَتَبَهُ اللَّهُ لَكَ، وَإِنِ اجْتَمَعُوا عَلَى أَنْ يَضُرُّوكَ بِشَيْءٍ لَمْ يَضُرُّوكَ إِلَّا بِشَيْءٍ قَدْ كَتَبَهُ اللَّهُ عَلَيْكَ، رُفِعَتِ الْأَقْلَامُ وَجَفَّتِ الصُّحُفُ"
  transliteration="wa-ʿlam anna l-ummata lawi jtamaʿat ʿalā an yanfaʿūka bi-shayʾin lam yanfaʿūka illā bi-shayʾin qad katabahu llāhu laka, wa-ini jtamaʿū ʿalā an yaḍurrūka bi-shayʾin lam yaḍurrūka illā bi-shayʾin qad katabahu llāhu ʿalayka, rufiʿati l-aqlāmu wa-jaffati ṣ-ṣuḥufu"
  translation="Know that if the whole nation gathered to benefit you with something, they would benefit you only with something Allah has already written for you. And if they gathered to harm you with something, they would harm you only with something Allah has already written against you. The pens have been lifted and the pages have dried."
  reference="Hadith 19, part 3"
/>

### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | وَاعْلَمْ | wa-ʿlam | and know | A command to one person, ending in a silent letter. |
| 2 | أَنَّ الْأُمَّةَ | anna l-ummata | that the nation | أَنَّ means "that" and makes الْأُمَّةَ accusative (ـَ). |
| 3 | لَوِ اجْتَمَعَتْ | lawi jtamaʿat | if it gathered | لَوْ means "if", for something that is not expected. It takes a kasra before the joining hamza. اجْتَمَعَتْ is past tense, she: the ـتْ ending refers to the nation, which is feminine. |
| 4 | عَلَى أَنْ يَنْفَعُوكَ | ʿalā an yanfaʿūka | to benefit you | أَنْ makes the verb drop its final ن, leaving ـُوا ("they"). The attached ـكَ means "you". |
| 5 | بِشَيْءٍ | bi-shayʾin | with something | بِ makes شَيْءٍ genitive (ـٍ). |
| 6 | لَمْ يَنْفَعُوكَ | lam yanfaʿūka | they would not benefit you | لَمْ makes the verb drop its final ن, leaving ـُوا ("they"). |
| 7 | إِلَّا بِشَيْءٍ | illā bi-shayʾin | except with something | إِلَّا means "except". |
| 8 | قَدْ كَتَبَهُ اللَّهُ | qad katabahu llāhu | Allah has already written it | قَدْ means "has already". كَتَبَ is past tense, he. The attached ـهُ means "it". اللَّهُ is nominative (ـُ): it is who wrote. The clause describes "something". |
| 9 | لَكَ | laka | for you | لِ takes a fatḥa before an attached pronoun. |
| 10 | وَإِنِ اجْتَمَعُوا | wa-ini jtamaʿū | and if they gathered | إِنْ means "if" and takes a kasra before the joining hamza. اجْتَمَعُوا is past tense with ـُوا ("they"). |
| 11 | عَلَى أَنْ يَضُرُّوكَ | ʿalā an yaḍurrūka | to harm you | The same pattern as row 4. The verb's root has two ر, which are written together as one doubled letter. |
| 12 | لَمْ يَضُرُّوكَ | lam yaḍurrūka | they would not harm you | The same pattern as row 6. |
| 13 | عَلَيْكَ | ʿalayka | against you | عَلَى becomes عَلَيْ before ـكَ. |
| 14 | رُفِعَتِ | rufiʿati | have been lifted | Past tense in the passive: ḍamma on the first letter and kasra on the one before the last. The ـتْ ending is feminine, and it takes a kasra before ال. |
| 15 | الْأَقْلَامُ | al-aqlāmu | the pens | Nominative (ـُ): with a passive verb, it takes the place of the doer. |
| 16 | وَجَفَّتِ | wa-jaffati | and have dried | Past tense, she, with the doubled ف. It takes a kasra before ال. |
| 17 | الصُّحُفُ | aṣ-ṣuḥufu | the pages | Nominative (ـُ): it is what dried. |
</GrammarTable>

## 4. Another Narration

### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | احْفَظِ اللَّهَ تَجِدْهُ أَمَامَكَ | iḥfaẓi llāha tajidhu amāmaka | guard Allah and you will find Him before you | The same as part 1, rows 9, 10 and 12. أَمَامَكَ is accusative (ـَ), a word for place, with ـكَ ("you") attached. |
| 2 | تَعَرَّفْ | taʿarraf | get to know | A command to one person, ending in a silent letter. The doubled letter marks the pattern meaning "to make yourself known". |
| 3 | إِلَى اللَّهِ | ilā llāhi | to Allah | إِلَى makes اللَّهِ genitive (ـِ). |
| 4 | فِي الرَّخَاءِ | fī r-rakhāʾi | in times of ease | فِي makes الرَّخَاءِ genitive (ـِ). |
| 5 | يَعْرِفْكَ | yaʿrifka | He will know you | Present tense, he, ending in a silent letter because it is the result of a command. The attached ـكَ means "you". |
| 6 | فِي الشِّدَّةِ | fī sh-shiddati | in hardship | فِي makes الشِّدَّةِ genitive (ـِ). |
| 7 | وَاعْلَمْ أَنَّ مَا أَخْطَأَكَ | wa-ʿlam anna mā akhṭaʾaka | and know that what missed you | أَنَّ means "that". مَا means "that which". أَخْطَأَ is past tense, he. The attached ـكَ means "you", who was missed. |
| 8 | لَمْ يَكُنْ لِيُصِيبَكَ | lam yakun li-yuṣībaka | was never going to reach you | لَمْ makes يَكُنْ end in a silent letter, and its middle و drops. لِ in front of a verb that ends in fatḥa means "in order to", here "to reach you". |
| 9 | وَمَا أَصَابَكَ لَمْ يَكُنْ لِيُخْطِئَكَ | wa-mā aṣābaka lam yakun li-yukhṭiʾaka | and what reached you was never going to miss you | The same pattern as rows 7 and 8, with the two verbs swapped. |
| 10 | النَّصْرَ | an-naṣra | victory | Accusative (ـَ): it follows أَنَّ. |
| 11 | مَعَ الصَّبْرِ | maʿa ṣ-ṣabri | with patience | مَعَ is a word for place, shown with fatḥa. الصَّبْرِ is genitive (ـِ) because it completes مَعَ. |
| 12 | وَأَنَّ الْفَرَجَ مَعَ الْكَرْبِ | wa-anna l-faraja maʿa l-karbi | and that relief is with distress | The same pattern: الْفَرَجَ is accusative (ـَ) after أَنَّ, and الْكَرْبِ is genitive (ـِ). |
| 13 | وَأَنَّ مَعَ الْعُسْرِ يُسْرًا | wa-anna maʿa l-ʿusri yusran | and that with hardship comes ease | The phrase "with hardship" comes first, and the thing that comes with it follows. يُسْرًا is accusative (ـًا) because it follows أَنَّ. |
</GrammarTable>
