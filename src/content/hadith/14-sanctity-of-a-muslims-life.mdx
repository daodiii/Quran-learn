### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | لَا يَحِلُّ | lā yaḥillu | is not permitted | لَا denies. يَحِلُّ is present tense, nominative (ـُ). The two ل of the root are written together as one doubled letter. |
| 2 | دَمُ | damu | the blood of | Nominative (ـُ): it is what is not permitted. It is the first word of a pair ("the blood of…"), so it takes no tanwīn. |
| 3 | امْرِئٍ | imri'in | a person | Genitive (ـٍ) because it completes دَمُ. The tanwīn means "any person". The word changes the vowel on its ر as well as on its end, and here both are kasra. |
| 4 | مُسْلِمٍ | muslimin | Muslim | Genitive (ـٍ): it describes امْرِئٍ, so it copies its case and its tanwīn. |
| 5 | إِلَّا | illā | except | Makes an exception. |
| 6 | بِإِحْدَى | bi-iḥdā | in one of | بِ makes إِحْدَى genitive, but it ends in a long ā, so no vowel shows. It is the feminine "one", because what is counted is understood as "cases". |
| 7 | ثَلَاثٍ | thalāthin | three | Genitive (ـٍ) because it completes إِحْدَى. It has no ـة ending because what is counted is understood as feminine. |
| 8 | الثَّيِّبُ | ath-thayyibu | the previously married | Nominative (ـُ): it starts the list of the three. Each item is a new statement: "they are…". |
| 9 | الزَّانِي | az-zānī | who commits adultery | Nominative: it describes الثَّيِّبُ. It ends in a long ī, so no vowel shows. |
| 10 | وَالنَّفْسُ بِالنَّفْسِ | wa-n-nafsu bi-n-nafsi | and a life for a life | النَّفْسُ is nominative (ـُ): the second item. بِ makes النَّفْسِ genitive (ـِ): "[taken] for a life". |
| 11 | وَالتَّارِكُ | wa-t-tāriku | and the one who leaves | Nominative (ـُ): the third item. It means "one who leaves". |
| 12 | لِدِينِهِ | li-dīnihi | his religion | لِ makes دِينِ genitive (ـِ). The attached ـهِ means "his". |
| 13 | الْمُفَارِقُ | al-mufāriqu | the one who separates | Nominative (ـُ): it describes التَّارِكُ. |
| 14 | لِلْجَمَاعَةِ | li-l-jamāʿati | from the community | لِ makes الْجَمَاعَةِ genitive (ـِ). The alif of ال drops after لِ, so the two ل join. |
</GrammarTable>
