### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | لَا يُؤْمِنُ أَحَدُكُمْ | lā yuʾminu aḥadukum | none of you believes | لَا denies the verb. يُؤْمِنُ is present tense, he, nominative (ـُ). أَحَدُكُمْ is nominative (ـُ): he is who believes. The attached ـكُمْ means "you" (plural). |
| 2 | حَتَّى يَكُونَ | ḥattā yakūna | until it becomes | حَتَّى makes the present verb end in fatḥa. يَكُونَ means "becomes". |
| 3 | هَوَاهُ | hawāhu | his desire | Nominative: it is what becomes. It ends in a long ā, so no vowel shows. The attached ـهُ means "his". |
| 4 | تَبَعًا | tabaʿan | following | Accusative (ـًا): it is the second part of what "becomes". |
| 5 | لِمَا جِئْتُ بِهِ | li-mā jiʾtu bihi | to what I have brought | لِ plus مَا ("that which"). جِئْتُ is past tense with ـتُ ("I"): the long vowel of the verb shortens before the silent ت. بِهِ points back to مَا. |
</GrammarTable>
