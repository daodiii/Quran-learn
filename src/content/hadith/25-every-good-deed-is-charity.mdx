### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | أَنَّ نَاسًا | anna nāsan | that some people | أَنَّ means "that" and makes the word after it accusative, so نَاسًا ends in ـًا. |
| 2 | مِنْ أَصْحَابِ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ | min aṣḥābi rasūli llāhi | of the Companions of the Messenger of Allah | مِنْ makes أَصْحَابِ genitive (ـِ). رَسُولِ and اللَّهِ are genitive too, each completing the one before. |
| 3 | قَالُوا | qālū | they said | Past tense with ـُوا ("they"). |
| 4 | لِلنَّبِيِّ | li-n-nabiyyi | to the Prophet | لِ makes النَّبِيِّ genitive (ـِ). |
| 5 | يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ | yā rasūla llāhi | O Messenger of Allah | A called name that is the first word of a pair ends in fatḥa. اللَّهِ is genitive (ـِ). |
| 6 | ذَهَبَ | dhahaba | have gone off | Past tense, he, with a fixed fatḥa. |
| 7 | أَهْلُ الدُّثُورِ | ahlu d-duthūri | the people of wealth | أَهْلُ is nominative (ـُ): they are who went off. الدُّثُورِ is genitive (ـِ) because it completes أَهْلُ. |
| 8 | بِالْأُجُورِ | bi-l-ujūri | with the rewards | بِ makes الْأُجُورِ genitive (ـِ). |
| 9 | يُصَلُّونَ | yuṣallūna | they pray | Present tense, they. The ـُونَ ending marks "they" and shows the verb is nominative. |
| 10 | كَمَا نُصَلِّي | ka-mā nuṣallī | as we pray | كَمَا means "as". نُصَلِّي is present tense, we. The ـِي ending is part of the verb. |
| 11 | وَيَصُومُونَ كَمَا نَصُومُ | wa-yaṣūmūna ka-mā naṣūmu | and they fast as we fast | The same pattern. |
| 12 | وَيَتَصَدَّقُونَ | wa-yataṣaddaqūna | and they give charity | Present tense, they. The doubled د marks the pattern meaning "to give charity". |
| 13 | بِفُضُولِ أَمْوَالِهِمْ | bi-fuḍūli amwālihim | from their surplus wealth | بِ makes فُضُولِ genitive (ـِ). أَمْوَالِ is genitive because it completes it. The attached ـهِمْ means "their". |
</GrammarTable>

### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | أَوَلَيْسَ | a-wa-laysa | is it not so | أَ starts a question, وَ means "and", and لَيْسَ means "is not". Together they ask a question that expects the answer "yes". |
| 2 | قَدْ جَعَلَ اللَّهُ | qad jaʿala llāhu | Allah has made | قَدْ means "has indeed". جَعَلَ is past tense, he. اللَّهُ is nominative (ـُ): it is who made. |
| 3 | لَكُمْ | lakum | for you | لِ takes a fatḥa before ـكُمْ. |
| 4 | مَا تَصَّدَّقُونَ | mā taṣṣaddaqūna | what you give as charity | مَا means "that which". The verb is present tense, you (plural). The first ت has merged into the ص, which is why the ص is doubled. |
| 5 | إِنَّ بِكُلِّ تَسْبِيحَةٍ | inna bi-kulli tasbīḥatin | indeed in every "Glory be" | بِ makes كُلِّ genitive (ـِ), and تَسْبِيحَةٍ is genitive because it completes it. This phrase comes first, and what is in it follows. |
| 6 | صَدَقَةً | ṣadaqatan | charity | Accusative (ـًا) because it follows إِنَّ, though it comes after the phrase. |
| 7 | وَكُلِّ تَكْبِيرَةٍ صَدَقَةً | wa-kulli takbīratin ṣadaqatan | and in every "Allah is the greatest", charity | The same pattern, joined by وَ, so كُلِّ stays genitive and صَدَقَةً stays accusative. |
| 8 | وَكُلِّ تَحْمِيدَةٍ ... وَكُلِّ تَهْلِيلَةٍ | wa-kulli taḥmīdatin ... wa-kulli tahlīlatin | and every "Praise be…" and every "There is no god…" | The same pattern again, twice. |
| 9 | وَأَمْرٌ بِالْمَعْرُوفِ صَدَقَةٌ | wa-amrun bi-l-maʿrūfi ṣadaqatun | and enjoining what is right is charity | A new statement. أَمْرٌ and صَدَقَةٌ are both nominative (ـٌ). بِ makes الْمَعْرُوفِ genitive (ـِ). |
| 10 | وَنَهْيٌ عَنْ مُنْكَرٍ صَدَقَةٌ | wa-nahyun ʿan munkarin ṣadaqatun | and forbidding what is wrong is charity | The same pattern. عَنْ makes مُنْكَرٍ genitive (ـٍ). |
| 11 | وَفِي بُضْعِ أَحَدِكُمْ صَدَقَةٌ | wa-fī buḍʿi aḥadikum ṣadaqatun | and in the intimacy of each of you is charity | فِي makes بُضْعِ genitive (ـِ), and أَحَدِ is genitive because it completes it. صَدَقَةٌ is nominative (ـٌ): it is what is in it, put after the phrase. |
</GrammarTable>

### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | أَيَأْتِي | a-yaʾtī | does he come to | أَ starts a question. يَأْتِي is present tense, he. The ـِي ending is part of the verb. |
| 2 | أَحَدُنَا | aḥadunā | one of us | Nominative (ـُ): it is who comes to. The attached ـنَا means "us". |
| 3 | شَهْوَتَهُ | shahwatahu | his desire | Accusative (ـَ): it is what he comes to. The attached ـهُ means "his". |
| 4 | وَيَكُونُ لَهُ | wa-yakūnu lahu | and there is for him | يَكُونُ is present tense of "to be". لَهُ is لِ plus ـهُ, with a fatḥa on the لِ. |
| 5 | فِيهَا | fīhā | in it | فِي plus ـهَا ("it"), meaning the desire, a feminine word. |
| 6 | أَجْرٌ | ajrun | a reward | Nominative (ـٌ): it is what "there is". It comes last, after the phrase "for him in it". |
| 7 | أَرَأَيْتُمْ | a-raʾaytum | do you see | أَ starts a question. رَأَيْتُمْ is past tense with ـتُمْ ("you", plural). It is used to mean "tell me" or "consider". |
| 8 | لَوْ وَضَعَهَا | law waḍaʿahā | if he put it | لَوْ means "if". وَضَعَ is past tense, he. The attached ـهَا means "it". |
| 9 | فِي حَرَامٍ | fī ḥarāmin | in what is forbidden | فِي makes حَرَامٍ genitive (ـٍ). |
| 10 | أَكَانَ | a-kāna | would there be | أَ starts a question. كَانَ is past tense of "to be". |
| 11 | عَلَيْهِ وِزْرٌ | ʿalayhi wizrun | a sin on him | عَلَى becomes عَلَيْ before ـهِ. وِزْرٌ is nominative (ـٌ): it is what "there is", put after the phrase. |
| 12 | فَكَذَلِكَ | fa-ka-dhālika | so likewise | فَ means "so". كَذَلِكَ is كَ ("like") plus ذَلِكَ ("that"). |
| 13 | إِذَا وَضَعَهَا فِي الْحَلَالِ | idhā waḍaʿahā fī l-ḥalāli | when he puts it in the lawful | إِذَا means "when". فِي makes الْحَلَالِ genitive (ـِ). |
| 14 | كَانَ لَهُ أَجْرٌ | kāna lahu ajrun | he has a reward | The same as rows 4 to 6: أَجْرٌ is nominative (ـٌ). |
</GrammarTable>
