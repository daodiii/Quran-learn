### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | الطُّهُورُ | aṭ-ṭuhūru | purity | Nominative (ـُ): it is what the statement is about. The ل of ال merges into the ط, so the ط is doubled. |
| 2 | شَطْرُ الْإِيمَانِ | shaṭru l-īmāni | half of faith | شَطْرُ is nominative (ـُ): the second part of the statement, and the first word of a pair, so it has no tanwīn. الْإِيمَانِ is genitive (ـِ) because it completes شَطْرُ. |
| 3 | وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ | wa-l-ḥamdu li-llāhi | and "praise be to Allah" | وَ adds a new statement. الْحَمْدُ is nominative (ـُ): what the sentence is about. لِلَّهِ is genitive (ـِ) after لِ. |
| 4 | تَمْلَأُ | tamlaʾu | fills | Present tense, she, nominative (ـُ). The تَ marks "she", because the phrase الْحَمْدُ is treated as feminine here. |
| 5 | الْمِيزَانَ | al-mīzāna | the scale | Accusative (ـَ): it is what gets filled. |
| 6 | وَسُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ | wa-subḥāna llāhi | and "glory be to Allah" | سُبْحَانَ is accusative (ـَ), as a phrase of praise. اللَّهِ is genitive (ـِ) because it completes it. |
| 7 | وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ | wa-l-ḥamdu li-llāhi | and "praise be to Allah" | The same as row 3. |
| 8 | تَمْلَآنِ | tamlāni | (the two) fill | Present tense, they two (feminine). The ـانِ ending marks "the two", because the two phrases together are the doer. |
| 9 | أَوْ تَمْلَأُ | aw tamlaʾu | or it fills | أَوْ gives a choice. The verb is the same as in row 4, said of each phrase alone. |
| 10 | مَا بَيْنَ | mā bayna | what is between | مَا means "that which". بَيْنَ is a word for place, shown with fatḥa. |
| 11 | السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضِ | as-samāwāti wa-l-arḍi | the heavens and the earth | Both are genitive (ـِ). السَّمَاوَاتِ completes بَيْنَ, and الْأَرْضِ is joined to it by وَ. |
| 12 | وَالصَّلَاةُ نُورٌ | wa-ṣ-ṣalātu nūrun | and prayer is light | الصَّلَاةُ is nominative (ـُ): what the statement is about. نُورٌ is nominative (ـٌ): the second part. |
| 13 | وَالصَّدَقَةُ بُرْهَانٌ | wa-ṣ-ṣadaqatu burhānun | and charity is proof | The same pattern. |
| 14 | وَالصَّبْرُ ضِيَاءٌ | wa-ṣ-ṣabru ḍiyāʾun | and patience is brightness | The same pattern. |
| 15 | وَالْقُرْآنُ حُجَّةٌ | wa-l-qurʾānu ḥujjatun | and the Quran is an argument | الْقُرْآنُ is nominative (ـُ), and حُجَّةٌ is nominative (ـٌ). |
| 16 | لَكَ أَوْ عَلَيْكَ | laka aw ʿalayka | for you or against you | لِ takes a fatḥa before ـكَ ("you"). عَلَى becomes عَلَيْ before ـكَ. Both describe حُجَّةٌ. |
| 17 | كُلُّ النَّاسِ | kullu n-nāsi | everyone | كُلُّ is nominative (ـُ): what the statement is about. النَّاسِ is genitive (ـِ) because it completes كُلُّ. |
| 18 | يَغْدُو | yaghdū | goes out in the morning | Present tense, he, nominative. The ending is a long ū that is part of the verb, so no vowel shows. |
| 19 | فَبَائِعٌ | fa-bāʾiʿun | and one who sells | فَ means "and then". بَائِعٌ means "one who sells" and is nominative (ـٌ): it is the first part of a statement, "one is a seller of himself". |
| 20 | نَفْسَهُ | nafsahu | himself | Accusative (ـَ): it is what is sold. The attached ـهُ means "his". |
| 21 | فَمُعْتِقُهَا | fa-muʿtiquhā | and so frees it | فَ means "and so". مُعْتِقُ is nominative (ـُ): "one who frees". The attached ـهَا means "it" (the self, a feminine word). |
| 22 | أَوْ مُوبِقُهَا | aw mūbiquhā | or ruins it | The same pattern, with أَوْ ("or") giving the other choice. |
</GrammarTable>
