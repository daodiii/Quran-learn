### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | جَاءَ رَجُلٌ | jāʾa rajulun | a man came | جَاءَ is past tense, he. رَجُلٌ is nominative (ـٌ): he is who came. |
| 2 | إِلَى النَّبِيِّ | ilā n-nabiyyi | to the Prophet | إِلَى makes النَّبِيِّ genitive (ـِ). |
| 3 | فَقَالَ | fa-qāla | and he said | فَ means "and then". Past tense, he. |
| 4 | دُلَّنِي | dullanī | show me | A command to one person, from a verb with a doubled ل. A doubled verb ends in fatḥa in the command. The ـنِي is a protecting ن before ـي ("me"). |
| 5 | عَلَى عَمَلٍ | ʿalā ʿamalin | to a deed | عَلَى makes عَمَلٍ genitive (ـٍ). |
| 6 | إِذَا عَمِلْتُهُ | idhā ʿamiltuhu | if I do it | إِذَا means "if" or "when". عَمِلْتُهُ is past tense with ـتُ ("I") and ـهُ ("it"). |
| 7 | أَحَبَّنِيَ اللَّهُ | aḥabbaniya llāhu | Allah will love me | أَحَبَّ is past tense, he, here with the meaning of a promise. The attached ـنِي means "me", and its ي takes a fatḥa before the joining alif of اللَّهُ. اللَّهُ is nominative (ـُ): He is who loves. |
| 8 | وَأَحَبَّنِيَ النَّاسُ | wa-aḥabbaniya n-nāsu | and people will love me | The same pattern. النَّاسُ is nominative (ـُ). |
| 9 | ازْهَدْ | izhad | renounce | A command to one person, ending in a silent letter. |
| 10 | فِي الدُّنْيَا | fī d-dunyā | in this world | فِي makes الدُّنْيَا genitive, but it ends in a long ā, so no vowel shows. |
| 11 | يُحِبَّكَ اللَّهُ | yuḥibbaka llāhu | Allah will love you | Present tense, he, ending in fatḥa on the doubled ب. This is how the result of a command is made: "renounce, and He will love you". The attached ـكَ means "you". |
| 12 | وَازْهَدْ فِيمَا عِنْدَ النَّاسِ | wa-zhad fīmā ʿinda n-nāsi | and renounce what people have | فِي plus مَا ("that which"), written together. عِنْدَ is accusative (ـَ), a word for place. النَّاسِ is genitive (ـِ) because it completes عِنْدَ. |
| 13 | يُحِبَّكَ النَّاسُ | yuḥibbaka n-nāsu | people will love you | The same as row 11. النَّاسُ is nominative (ـُ). |
</GrammarTable>
