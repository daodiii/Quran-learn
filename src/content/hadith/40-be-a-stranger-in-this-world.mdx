## 1. The Prophet's Words

  transliteration="akhadha rasūlu llāhi ṣallā llāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam bi-mankibī fa-qāla: kun fī d-dunyā ka-annaka gharībun aw ʿābiru sabīlin"
  translation="The Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, took hold of my shoulder and said: “Be in this world as though you were a stranger or a traveler passing through.”"

### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | أَخَذَ | akhadha | took | Past tense, he, with a fixed fatḥa. |
| 2 | رَسُولُ اللَّهِ | rasūlu llāhi | the Messenger of Allah | رَسُولُ is nominative (ـُ): he is who took hold. اللَّهِ is genitive (ـِ) because it completes it. |
| 3 | بِمَنْكِبِي | bi-mankibī | of my shoulder | بِ makes مَنْكِبِ genitive. The attached ـي means "my", so a kasra shows. |
| 4 | فَقَالَ | fa-qāla | and he said | فَ means "and then". Past tense, he. |
| 5 | كُنْ | kun | be | A command to one person, ending in a silent letter. The long vowel in the middle of كَانَ has dropped. |
| 6 | فِي الدُّنْيَا | fī d-dunyā | in this world | فِي makes الدُّنْيَا genitive, but it ends in a long ā, so no vowel shows. |
| 7 | كَأَنَّكَ | ka-annaka | as though you | Three parts: كَ ("like"), أَنَّ ("that") and ـكَ ("you"). |
| 8 | غَرِيبٌ | gharībun | a stranger | Nominative (ـٌ): it is the second part of the statement that أَنَّ opens. |
| 9 | أَوْ عَابِرُ سَبِيلٍ | aw ʿābiru sabīlin | or a passer-by | أَوْ gives a choice. عَابِرُ is nominative (ـُ): joined to غَرِيبٌ. سَبِيلٍ is genitive (ـٍ) because it completes عَابِرُ. |
</GrammarTable>

### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | وَكَانَ ابْنُ عُمَرَ يَقُولُ | wa-kāna bnu ʿUmara yaqūlu | and Ibn Umar used to say | كَانَ with a present verb means "used to". ابْنُ is nominative (ـُ). عُمَرَ is genitive but takes no tanwīn, because it is a name, so it shows fatḥa. |
| 2 | إِذَا أَمْسَيْتَ | idhā amsayta | when you reach the evening | إِذَا means "when". أَمْسَيْتَ is past tense with ـتَ ("you"). |
| 3 | فَلَا تَنْتَظِرِ | fa-lā tantaẓiri | then do not wait | لَا here means "do not". The verb ends in a silent letter, which takes a kasra before ال. |
| 4 | الصَّبَاحَ | aṣ-ṣabāḥa | the morning | Accusative (ـَ): it is what you wait for. |
| 5 | وَإِذَا أَصْبَحْتَ فَلَا تَنْتَظِرِ الْمَسَاءَ | wa-idhā aṣbaḥta fa-lā tantaẓiri l-masāʾa | and when you reach the morning, do not wait for the evening | The same pattern. الْمَسَاءَ is accusative (ـَ). |
| 6 | وَخُذْ | wa-khudh | and take | A command to one person, ending in a silent letter. The first letter of the verb has dropped. |
| 7 | مِنْ صِحَّتِكَ لِمَرَضِكَ | min ṣiḥḥatika li-maraḍika | from your health for your illness | مِنْ makes صِحَّةِ genitive (ـِ). لِ means "for", and makes مَرَضِ genitive. The attached ـكَ means "your". |
| 8 | وَمِنْ حَيَاتِكَ لِمَوْتِكَ | wa-min ḥayātika li-mawtika | and from your life for your death | The same pattern. |
</GrammarTable>
