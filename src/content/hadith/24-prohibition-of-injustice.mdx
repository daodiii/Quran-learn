Below, the hadith is explained part by part.

## 1. Injustice Is Forbidden

<ArabicExample
  arabic="يَا عِبَادِي إِنِّي حَرَّمْتُ الظُّلْمَ عَلَى نَفْسِي، وَجَعَلْتُهُ بَيْنَكُمْ مُحَرَّمًا، فَلَا تَظَالَمُوا"
  transliteration="yā ʿibādī innī ḥarramtu ẓ-ẓulma ʿalā nafsī, wa-jaʿaltuhu baynakum muḥarraman, fa-lā tazālamū"
  translation="My servants, I have forbidden injustice for Myself and have made it forbidden among you, so do not wrong one another."
  reference="Hadith 24, part 1 · Narrated by Abu Dharr"
/>

### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | يَا عِبَادِي | yā ʿibādī | O my servants | يَا calls them. The attached ـي means "my", and the letter before it takes a kasra. |
| 2 | إِنِّي | innī | indeed I | إِنَّ plus ـي ("I"). |
| 3 | حَرَّمْتُ | ḥarramtu | I forbade | Past tense with ـتُ ("I"). The doubled ر marks the pattern meaning "to declare forbidden". |
| 4 | الظُّلْمَ | aẓ-ẓulma | injustice | Accusative (ـَ): it is what He forbade. |
| 5 | عَلَى نَفْسِي | ʿalā nafsī | for Myself | عَلَى makes نَفْسِ genitive (ـِ). The attached ـي means "my", so "on My self". |
| 6 | وَجَعَلْتُهُ | wa-jaʿaltuhu | and I made it | Past tense with ـتُ ("I"). The attached ـهُ means "it". |
| 7 | بَيْنَكُمْ | baynakum | among you | بَيْنَ is a word for place, shown with fatḥa. The attached ـكُمْ means "you". |
| 8 | مُحَرَّمًا | muḥarraman | forbidden | Accusative (ـًا): it is what "it" was made. |
| 9 | فَلَا تَظَالَمُوا | fa-lā tazālamū | so do not wrong one another | فَ means "so". لَا here means "do not", and it makes the verb drop its final ن, leaving ـُوا. The pattern with a long ā after the first letter means doing something to each other. |
</GrammarTable>

My servants, all of you are hungry except the one I feed, so ask Me for food and I will feed you. ### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | كُلُّكُمْ | kullukum | all of you | Nominative (ـُ): it is what the statement is about. The attached ـكُمْ means "you". |
| 2 | ضَالٌّ | ḍāllun | lost | Nominative (ـٌ): the second part. The ل is doubled. |
| 3 | إِلَّا مَنْ هَدَيْتُهُ | illā man hadaytuhu | except the one I guide | إِلَّا means "except". مَنْ means "the one whom". هَدَيْتُهُ is past tense with ـتُ ("I") and ـهُ ("him"). |
| 4 | فَاسْتَهْدُونِي | fa-stahdūnī | so ask Me for guidance | فَ means "so". A command to several people, from a verb meaning "to ask for guidance". Its ن dropped, leaving ـُوا, and a protecting ن comes before the attached ـي ("me"). |
| 5 | أَهْدِكُمْ | ahdikum | I will guide you | Present tense, I, ending in a silent letter because it is the result of a command: "ask, and I will guide". The attached ـكُمْ means "you". |
| 6 | جَائِعٌ | jāʾiʿun | hungry | Nominative (ـٌ): the second part. |
| 7 | أَطْعَمْتُهُ | aṭʿamtuhu | I fed him | Past tense with ـتُ ("I") and ـهُ ("him"). |
| 8 | فَاسْتَطْعِمُونِي أُطْعِمْكُمْ | fa-staṭʿimūnī uṭʿimkum | so ask Me for food and I will feed you | The same pattern as rows 4 and 5. |
| 9 | عَارٍ | ʿārin | unclothed | Nominative, but its final letter has dropped, so only the tanwīn shows, written as ـٍ. |
| 10 | كَسَوْتُهُ | kasawtuhu | I clothed him | Past tense with ـتُ ("I") and ـهُ ("him"). |
| 11 | فَاسْتَكْسُونِي أَكْسُكُمْ | fa-staksūnī aksukum | so ask Me for clothing and I will clothe you | The same pattern as rows 4 and 5. In أَكْسُكُمْ the final و of the verb has dropped, leaving ḍamma. |
</GrammarTable>

## 3. Forgiveness and Not Harming Allah

<ArabicExample
  arabic="يَا عِبَادِي إِنَّكُمْ تُخْطِئُونَ بِاللَّيْلِ وَالنَّهَارِ وَأَنَا أَغْفِرُ الذُّنُوبَ جَمِيعًا، فَاسْتَغْفِرُونِي أَغْفِرْ لَكُمْ. يَا عِبَادِي إِنَّكُمْ لَنْ تَبْلُغُوا ضَرِّي فَتَضُرُّونِي، وَلَنْ تَبْلُغُوا نَفْعِي فَتَنْفَعُونِي"
  transliteration="yā ʿibādī innakum tukhṭiʾūna bi-l-layli wa-n-nahāri wa-anā aghfiru dh-dhunūba jamīʿan, fa-staghfirūnī aghfir lakum. yā ʿibādī innakum lan tablughū ḍarrī fa-taḍurrūnī, wa-lan tablughū nafʿī fa-tanfaʿūnī"
  translation="My servants, you sin by night and by day, and I forgive all sins, so ask Me for forgiveness and I will forgive you. My servants, you will never reach the point of harming Me so that you could harm Me, and you will never reach the point of benefiting Me so that you could benefit Me."
  reference="Hadith 24, part 3"
/>

### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | إِنَّكُمْ | innakum | indeed you | إِنَّ plus ـكُمْ ("you", plural). |
| 2 | تُخْطِئُونَ | tukhṭiʾūna | you sin | Present tense, you (plural). The ـُونَ ending marks "you" and shows the verb is nominative. |
| 3 | بِاللَّيْلِ وَالنَّهَارِ | bi-l-layli wa-n-nahāri | by night and by day | بِ makes اللَّيْلِ genitive (ـِ). النَّهَارِ is joined to it by وَ, so it is genitive too. |
| 4 | وَأَنَا أَغْفِرُ | wa-anā aghfiru | and I forgive | أَنَا is a separate pronoun meaning "I", and it never changes. أَغْفِرُ is present tense, I, nominative (ـُ). |
| 5 | الذُّنُوبَ جَمِيعًا | adh-dhunūba jamīʿan | all sins | الذُّنُوبَ is accusative (ـَ): it is what He forgives. جَمِيعًا is accusative (ـًا) and says how: "all together". |
| 6 | فَاسْتَغْفِرُونِي أَغْفِرْ لَكُمْ | fa-staghfirūnī aghfir lakum | so ask Me for forgiveness and I will forgive you | A command to several people, with ـُوا and a protecting ن before ـي ("me"). أَغْفِرْ ends in a silent letter because it is the result of the command. لِ takes a fatḥa before ـكُمْ. |
| 7 | لَنْ تَبْلُغُوا | lan tablughū | you will never reach | لَنْ means "never" about the future. It makes the verb drop its final ن, leaving ـُوا. |
| 8 | ضَرِّي | ḍarrī | harming Me | Accusative (ـَ in form) but it carries the attached ـي ("me"), so a kasra shows. It is what they would reach. |
| 9 | فَتَضُرُّونِي | fa-taḍurrūnī | so that you harm Me | Present tense, you (plural), with ـُونَ. The ن stays because the clause stands as a new statement. A protecting ن comes before ـي ("me"). |
| 10 | وَلَنْ تَبْلُغُوا نَفْعِي فَتَنْفَعُونِي | wa-lan tablughū nafʿī fa-tanfaʿūnī | and you will never reach benefiting Me so that you benefit Me | The same pattern as rows 7 to 9. |
</GrammarTable>

## 4. Piety and Wickedness Change Nothing

<ArabicExample
  arabic="يَا عِبَادِي لَوْ أَنَّ أَوَّلَكُمْ وَآخِرَكُمْ وَإِنْسَكُمْ وَجِنَّكُمْ كَانُوا عَلَى أَتْقَى قَلْبِ رَجُلٍ وَاحِدٍ مِنْكُمْ مَا زَادَ ذَلِكَ فِي مُلْكِي شَيْئًا. يَا عِبَادِي لَوْ أَنَّ أَوَّلَكُمْ وَآخِرَكُمْ وَإِنْسَكُمْ وَجِنَّكُمْ كَانُوا عَلَى أَفْجَرِ قَلْبِ رَجُلٍ وَاحِدٍ مِنْكُمْ مَا نَقَصَ ذَلِكَ مِنْ مُلْكِي شَيْئًا"
  transliteration="yā ʿibādī law anna awwalakum wa-ākhirakum wa-insakum wa-jinnakum kānū ʿalā atqā qalbi rajulin wāḥidin minkum mā zāda dhālika fī mulkī shayʾan. yā ʿibādī law anna awwalakum wa-ākhirakum wa-insakum wa-jinnakum kānū ʿalā afjari qalbi rajulin wāḥidin minkum mā naqaṣa dhālika min mulkī shayʾan"
  translation="My servants, if the first of you and the last of you, the humans among you and the jinn among you, were all as devout as the most devout heart of one of you, that would add nothing to My kingdom. My servants, if the first of you and the last of you, the humans among you and the jinn among you, were all as wicked as the most wicked heart of one of you, that would take nothing from My kingdom."
  reference="Hadith 24, part 4"
/>

### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | لَوْ أَنَّ | law anna | if | لَوْ means "if", for something not expected. أَنَّ means "that" and makes the words after it accusative. |
| 2 | أَوَّلَكُمْ وَآخِرَكُمْ | awwalakum wa-ākhirakum | the first of you and the last of you | Both are accusative (ـَ) after أَنَّ, joined by وَ. The attached ـكُمْ means "you". |
| 3 | وَإِنْسَكُمْ وَجِنَّكُمْ | wa-insakum wa-jinnakum | and your humans and your jinn | Both are accusative (ـَ), joined by وَ, with ـكُمْ ("you"). |
| 4 | كَانُوا | kānū | they were | Past tense of "to be", with ـُوا ("they"). It completes the sentence that أَنَّ opens. |
| 5 | عَلَى أَتْقَى قَلْبِ | ʿalā atqā qalbi | as the most devout heart | عَلَى makes أَتْقَى genitive, but it ends in a long ā, so no vowel shows. قَلْبِ is genitive (ـِ) because it completes أَتْقَى. |
| 6 | رَجُلٍ وَاحِدٍ مِنْكُمْ | rajulin wāḥidin minkum | of one man among you | رَجُلٍ is genitive (ـٍ) because it completes قَلْبِ. وَاحِدٍ describes it and copies its case. مِنْكُمْ means "from among you". |
| 7 | مَا زَادَ | mā zāda | would not add | Here مَا means "not". زَادَ is past tense, he. This is the answer to "if". |
| 8 | ذَلِكَ | dhālika | that | Nominative in position: it is what would add. It never shows a vowel. |
| 9 | فِي مُلْكِي | fī mulkī | to My kingdom | فِي makes مُلْكِ genitive (ـِ). The attached ـي means "my". |
| 10 | شَيْئًا | shayʾan | anything | Accusative (ـًا): it is what would be added. |
| 11 | أَفْجَرِ | afjari | the most wicked | Words of this pattern take no tanwīn, but when they begin a pair like this they show a normal kasra. |
| 12 | مَا نَقَصَ ذَلِكَ مِنْ مُلْكِي شَيْئًا | mā naqaṣa dhālika min mulkī shayʾan | that would take nothing from My kingdom | The same as row 7, with the opposite verb. مِنْ makes مُلْكِي genitive. |
</GrammarTable>

## 5. The Needle in the Sea

<ArabicExample
  arabic="يَا عِبَادِي لَوْ أَنَّ أَوَّلَكُمْ وَآخِرَكُمْ وَإِنْسَكُمْ وَجِنَّكُمْ قَامُوا فِي صَعِيدٍ وَاحِدٍ فَسَأَلُونِي، فَأَعْطَيْتُ كُلَّ وَاحِدٍ مَسْأَلَتَهُ، مَا نَقَصَ ذَلِكَ مِمَّا عِنْدِي إِلَّا كَمَا يَنْقُصُ الْمِخْيَطُ إِذَا أُدْخِلَ الْبَحْرَ"
  transliteration="yā ʿibādī law anna awwalakum wa-ākhirakum wa-insakum wa-jinnakum qāmū fī ṣaʿīdin wāḥidin fa-saʾalūnī, fa-aʿṭaytu kulla wāḥidin masʾalatahu, mā naqaṣa dhālika mimmā ʿindī illā ka-mā yanquṣu l-mikhyaṭu idhā udkhila l-baḥra"
  translation="My servants, if the first of you and the last of you, the humans among you and the jinn among you, stood on one plain and asked Me, and I gave everyone what they asked, that would reduce what I have only as much as a needle reduces the sea when it is dipped into it."
  reference="Hadith 24, part 5"
/>

### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | قَامُوا | qāmū | they stood | Past tense with ـُوا ("they"). |
| 2 | فِي صَعِيدٍ وَاحِدٍ | fī ṣaʿīdin wāḥidin | on one plain | فِي makes صَعِيدٍ genitive (ـٍ). وَاحِدٍ describes it and copies its case. |
| 3 | فَسَأَلُونِي | fa-saʾalūnī | and asked Me | فَ means "and then". Past tense with ـُوا ("they") and a protecting ن before ـي ("me"). |
| 4 | فَأَعْطَيْتُ | fa-aʿṭaytu | and I gave | Past tense with ـتُ ("I"). |
| 5 | كُلَّ وَاحِدٍ مَسْأَلَتَهُ | kulla wāḥidin masʾalatahu | everyone what they asked | The verb takes two objects. كُلَّ is accusative (ـَ): who was given. وَاحِدٍ is genitive because it completes كُلَّ. مَسْأَلَتَهُ is accusative (ـَ): what was given. The attached ـهُ means "his". |
| 6 | مَا نَقَصَ ذَلِكَ | mā naqaṣa dhālika | that would not reduce | Here مَا means "not". نَقَصَ is past tense, he. |
| 7 | مِمَّا عِنْدِي | mimmā ʿindī | what I have | مِنْ plus مَا ("that which"), written together. عِنْدِي is عِنْدَ ("with") plus ـي ("me"), so a kasra shows. |
| 8 | إِلَّا كَمَا | illā ka-mā | except as | إِلَّا means "except". كَمَا is كَ ("like") plus مَا ("what"). |
| 9 | يَنْقُصُ | yanquṣu | reduces | Present tense, he, nominative (ـُ). |
| 10 | الْمِخْيَطُ | al-mikhyaṭu | the needle | Nominative (ـُ): it is what reduces. |
| 11 | إِذَا أُدْخِلَ | idhā udkhila | when it is put in | Past tense in the passive: ḍamma on the first letter and kasra on the one before the last. The one who puts it in is not named. |
| 12 | الْبَحْرَ | al-baḥra | the sea | Accusative (ـَ): it is where the needle is put. |
</GrammarTable>

## 6. Only Your Deeds

<ArabicExample
  arabic="يَا عِبَادِي إِنَّمَا هِيَ أَعْمَالُكُمْ أُحْصِيهَا لَكُمْ ثُمَّ أُوَفِّيكُمْ إِيَّاهَا، فَمَنْ وَجَدَ خَيْرًا فَلْيَحْمَدِ اللَّهَ، وَمَنْ وَجَدَ غَيْرَ ذَلِكَ فَلَا يَلُومَنَّ إِلَّا نَفْسَهُ"
  transliteration="yā ʿibādī innamā hiya aʿmālukum uḥṣīhā lakum thumma uwaffīkum iyyāhā, fa-man wajada khayran fa-l-yaḥmadi llāha, wa-man wajada ghayra dhālika fa-lā yalūmanna illā nafsahu"
  translation="My servants, it is only your deeds that I record for you and then repay you in full. Whoever finds good, let them praise Allah, and whoever finds other than that, let them blame no one but themselves."
  reference="Hadith 24, part 6"
/>

### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | إِنَّمَا هِيَ | innamā hiya | it is only | إِنَّمَا means "only". هِيَ means "it" and is feminine, because it points to the deeds, a plural of things. |
| 2 | أَعْمَالُكُمْ | aʿmālukum | your deeds | Nominative (ـُ): it is the second part of the statement. The attached ـكُمْ means "your". |
| 3 | أُحْصِيهَا | uḥṣīhā | I record them | Present tense, I, nominative. The attached ـهَا means "them" (feminine). The clause describes the deeds. |
| 4 | لَكُمْ | lakum | for you | لِ takes a fatḥa before ـكُمْ. |
| 5 | ثُمَّ أُوَفِّيكُمْ | thumma uwaffīkum | then I repay you in full | The doubled ف marks the pattern meaning "to pay in full". The attached ـكُمْ is the first object: "you". |
| 6 | إِيَّاهَا | iyyāhā | them | A separate word used for "them" when the verb already has one attached pronoun. It is the second object. |
| 7 | فَمَنْ وَجَدَ | fa-man wajada | so whoever finds | فَ means "so". مَنْ starts a condition. وَجَدَ is past tense, he. |
| 8 | خَيْرًا | khayran | good | Accusative (ـًا): it is what he finds. |
| 9 | فَلْيَحْمَدِ | fa-l-yaḥmadi | then let him praise | فَ opens the result. لِ plus a present verb means "let him", and it loses its vowel after فَ. The verb ends in a silent letter that takes a kasra before ال. |
| 10 | اللَّهَ | Allāha | Allah | Accusative (ـَ): it is who he praises. |
| 11 | وَمَنْ وَجَدَ غَيْرَ ذَلِكَ | wa-man wajada ghayra dhālika | and whoever finds other than that | غَيْرَ is accusative (ـَ): it is what he finds. ذَلِكَ never shows a vowel. |
| 12 | فَلَا يَلُومَنَّ | fa-lā yalūmanna | then let him not blame | لَا here means "do not". The ـنَّ ending strengthens the command, and it makes the verb end in fatḥa. |
| 13 | إِلَّا نَفْسَهُ | illā nafsahu | except himself | Accusative (ـَ): it is who he may blame. The attached ـهُ means "his". |
</GrammarTable>
