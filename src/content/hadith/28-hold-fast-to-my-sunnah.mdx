## 1. The Sermon

  transliteration="waʿaẓanā rasūlu llāhi ṣallā llāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam mawʿiẓatan wajilat minhā l-qulūbu wa-tharafat minhā l-ʿuyūnu, fa-qulnā: yā rasūla llāhi, ka-annahā mawʿiẓatu muwaddiʿin fa-awṣinā"
  translation="The Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, gave us a sermon that made our hearts tremble and our eyes fill with tears. We said: “Messenger of Allah, it is like a farewell sermon, so advise us.”"
  reference="Hadith 28, part 1 · Narrated by al-Irbad ibn Sariya"

### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | وَعَظَنَا | waʿaẓanā | he gave us a sermon | Past tense, he. The attached ـنَا means "us". |
| 2 | رَسُولُ اللَّهِ | rasūlu llāhi | the Messenger of Allah | رَسُولُ is nominative (ـُ): he is who gave the sermon. اللَّهِ is genitive (ـِ) because it completes it. |
| 3 | مَوْعِظَةً | mawʿiẓatan | a sermon | Accusative (ـًا): it is what he gave. |
| 4 | وَجِلَتْ | wajilat | trembled | Past tense, she. The ـتْ ending is feminine, because it is said of the hearts, a plural of things. |
| 5 | مِنْهَا | minhā | because of it | مِنْ plus ـهَا ("it"), meaning the sermon, a feminine word. |
| 6 | الْقُلُوبُ | al-qulūbu | the hearts | Nominative (ـُ): they are what trembled. |
| 7 | وَذَرَفَتْ | wa-tharafat | and shed tears | Past tense, she, said of the eyes. |
| 8 | الْعُيُونُ | al-ʿuyūnu | the eyes | Nominative (ـُ): they are what shed tears. |
| 9 | فَقُلْنَا | fa-qulnā | so we said | Past tense with ـنَا ("we"). The middle letter of قَالَ drops before the silent ن. |
| 10 | كَأَنَّهَا | ka-annahā | as if it | Three parts: كَ ("like"), أَنَّ ("that") and ـهَا ("it"). |
| 11 | مَوْعِظَةُ مُوَدِّعٍ | mawʿiẓatu muwaddiʿin | a sermon of one who bids farewell | مَوْعِظَةُ is nominative (ـُ): the second part. مُوَدِّعٍ is genitive (ـٍ) because it completes it. |
| 12 | فَأَوْصِنَا | fa-awṣinā | so advise us | A command to one person. The attached ـنَا means "us". |
</GrammarTable>

## 2. The Advice

<ArabicExample
  arabic="قَالَ: أُوصِيكُمْ بِتَقْوَى اللَّهِ عَزَّ وَجَلَّ، وَالسَّمْعِ وَالطَّاعَةِ، وَإِنْ تَأَمَّرَ عَلَيْكُمْ عَبْدٌ، فَإِنَّهُ مَنْ يَعِشْ مِنْكُمْ فَسَيَرَى اخْتِلَافًا كَثِيرًا"
  transliteration="qāla: uwṣīkum bi-taqwā llāhi ʿazza wa-jalla, wa-s-samʿi wa-ṭ-ṭāʿati, wa-in taʾammara ʿalaykum ʿabdun, fa-innahu man yaʿish minkum fa-sa-yarā ikhtilāfan kathīran"
  translation="He said: “I advise you to be mindful of Allah, mighty and majestic, and to listen and obey, even if a slave is placed in command over you. Whoever among you lives will see much disagreement.”"
  reference="Hadith 28, part 2"
/>

### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | أُوصِيكُمْ | uwṣīkum | I advise you | Present tense, I, nominative. The attached ـكُمْ means "you" (plural). |
| 2 | بِتَقْوَى اللَّهِ | bi-taqwā llāhi | to be mindful of Allah | بِ makes تَقْوَى genitive, but it ends in a long ā, so no vowel shows. اللَّهِ is genitive (ـِ) because it completes it. |
| 3 | عَزَّ وَجَلَّ | ʿazza wa-jalla | mighty and majestic | Two past-tense verbs used as a phrase of praise. |
| 4 | وَالسَّمْعِ وَالطَّاعَةِ | wa-s-samʿi wa-ṭ-ṭāʿati | and listening and obeying | Both are genitive (ـِ), joined by وَ to تَقْوَى, so they take its case. |
| 5 | وَإِنْ تَأَمَّرَ | wa-in taʾammara | even if is placed in command | وَ here means "even". إِنْ means "if". The verb is past tense, he, and means "to take command". |
| 6 | عَلَيْكُمْ عَبْدٌ | ʿalaykum ʿabdun | over you a slave | عَلَى becomes عَلَيْ before ـكُمْ. عَبْدٌ is nominative (ـٌ): he is who takes command. |
| 7 | فَإِنَّهُ | fa-innahu | for indeed | فَ means "for". إِنَّ plus ـهُ ("the matter"). |
| 8 | مَنْ يَعِشْ | man yaʿish | whoever lives | مَنْ starts a condition. يَعِشْ ends in a silent letter because of it, and the middle ي of the verb has dropped, so يَعِيشُ becomes يَعِشْ. |
| 9 | مِنْكُمْ | minkum | among you | مِنْ plus ـكُمْ ("you"). |
| 10 | فَسَيَرَى | fa-sa-yarā | then he will see | فَ opens the result. سَـ marks the near future. The verb ends in a long ā. |
| 11 | اخْتِلَافًا كَثِيرًا | ikhtilāfan kathīran | much disagreement | اخْتِلَافًا is accusative (ـًا): it is what he will see. كَثِيرًا describes it and takes the same case. |
</GrammarTable>

## 3. Hold Fast and Beware

<ArabicExample
  arabic="فَعَلَيْكُمْ بِسُنَّتِي وَسُنَّةِ الْخُلَفَاءِ الرَّاشِدِينَ الْمَهْدِيِّينَ، عَضُّوا عَلَيْهَا بِالنَّوَاجِذِ، وَإِيَّاكُمْ وَمُحْدَثَاتِ الْأُمُورِ، فَإِنَّ كُلَّ بِدْعَةٍ ضَلَالَةٌ، وَكُلَّ ضَلَالَةٍ فِي النَّارِ"
  transliteration="fa-ʿalaykum bi-sunnatī wa-sunnati l-khulafāʾi r-rāshidīna l-mahdiyyīna, ʿaḍḍū ʿalayhā bi-n-nawājidhi, wa-iyyākum wa-muḥdathāti l-umūri, fa-inna kulla bidʿatin ḍalālatun, wa-kulla ḍalālatin fī n-nāri"
  translation="So hold to my way and the way of the rightly guided, guided successors. Bite onto it with your back teeth. And beware of newly invented matters, for every innovation is a misguidance, and every misguidance is in the Fire."
  reference="Hadith 28, part 3"
/>

### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | فَعَلَيْكُمْ | fa-ʿalaykum | so hold to | عَلَيْكُمْ works here as a command: "you must hold to". It is followed by بِ. |
| 2 | بِسُنَّتِي | bi-sunnatī | my way | بِ makes سُنَّةِ genitive. The attached ـي means "my", so a kasra shows. |
| 3 | وَسُنَّةِ الْخُلَفَاءِ | wa-sunnati l-khulafāʾi | and the way of the successors | Genitive (ـِ): joined by وَ to سُنَّتِي. الْخُلَفَاءِ is genitive because it completes it. |
| 4 | الرَّاشِدِينَ الْمَهْدِيِّينَ | ar-rāshidīna l-mahdiyyīna | the rightly guided, the guided | Both describe الْخُلَفَاءِ and take its genitive case. A masculine plural ends in ـِينَ for the genitive. |
| 5 | عَضُّوا | ʿaḍḍū | bite | A command to several people. The ض is doubled, and the ـُوا ending marks "you" (plural). |
| 6 | عَلَيْهَا بِالنَّوَاجِذِ | ʿalayhā bi-n-nawājidhi | onto it with the back teeth | The attached ـهَا means "it", meaning the way, a feminine word. بِ makes النَّوَاجِذِ genitive (ـِ). |
| 7 | وَإِيَّاكُمْ | wa-iyyākum | and beware | A word of warning. إِيَّا with ـكُمْ ("you") means "keep away from". |
| 8 | وَمُحْدَثَاتِ الْأُمُورِ | wa-muḥdathāti l-umūri | newly invented matters | مُحْدَثَاتِ is accusative: it is what they are warned about. A feminine plural ends in ـَاتِ with a kasra. الْأُمُورِ is genitive (ـِ) because it completes it. |
| 9 | فَإِنَّ كُلَّ بِدْعَةٍ ضَلَالَةٌ | fa-inna kulla bidʿatin ḍalālatun | for every innovation is a misguidance | إِنَّ makes كُلَّ accusative (ـَ). بِدْعَةٍ is genitive because it completes it. ضَلَالَةٌ is nominative (ـٌ). |
| 10 | وَكُلَّ ضَلَالَةٍ فِي النَّارِ | wa-kulla ḍalālatin fī n-nāri | and every misguidance is in the Fire | كُلَّ is accusative (ـَ), joined to the first كُلَّ. فِي makes النَّارِ genitive (ـِ). |
</GrammarTable>
