### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | إِنَّ | inna | indeed | The word that opens the statement. What follows the whole phrase "among what people received" is the saying itself. |
| 2 | مِمَّا | mimmā | among what | مِنْ plus مَا ("that which"), written together. The ن of مِنْ merges into the م of مَا, so the م is doubled. |
| 3 | أَدْرَكَ | adraka | reached, received | Past tense, he, with a fixed fatḥa. The أَ at the start marks the pattern meaning "to catch up with something". |
| 4 | النَّاسُ | an-nāsu | the people | Nominative (ـُ): it is who received. |
| 5 | مِنْ كَلَامِ | min kalāmi | from the words of | مِنْ makes كَلَامِ genitive (ـِ). |
| 6 | النُّبُوَّةِ | an-nubuwwati | prophecy | Genitive (ـِ) because it completes كَلَامِ. |
| 7 | الْأُولَى | al-ūlā | the first | It describes النُّبُوَّةِ, so it takes the same case: genitive. It ends in a long ā, so no vowel shows. |
| 8 | إِذَا | idhā | if, when | Starts a condition. |
| 9 | لَمْ تَسْتَحِ | lam tastaḥi | you have no shame | لَمْ turns the present into a past denial and makes the verb end in a silent letter. The verb's last letter drops, so it ends in kasra. |
| 10 | فَاصْنَعْ | fa-ṣnaʿ | then do | فَ opens the result. A command to one person, ending in a silent letter. |
| 11 | مَا شِئْتَ | mā shiʾta | what you wish | مَا means "that which". شِئْتَ is past tense with ـتَ ("you"). |
</GrammarTable>
