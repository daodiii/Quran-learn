### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | لَا | lā | no | Denies the whole kind: "no harm at all". This is why the next word has no tanwīn. |
| 2 | ضَرَرَ | ḍarara | harm | Fixed on fatḥa with no tanwīn, because لَا is denying the whole kind. |
| 3 | وَلَا ضِرَارَ | wa-lā ḍirāra | and no harming in return | وَ adds a second denial. ضِرَارَ is fixed on fatḥa with no tanwīn in the same way. The long ā after the first letter is the pattern that means doing something back to someone. |
</GrammarTable>
