## 1. The Question and the Pillars

### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | قُلْتُ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ | qultu yā rasūla llāhi | I said: O Messenger of Allah | Past tense with ـتُ ("I"). A called name that is the first word of a pair ends in fatḥa. اللَّهِ is genitive (ـِ). |
| 2 | أَخْبِرْنِي | akhbirnī | tell me | A command to one person. The ـنِ is a protecting ن before ـي ("me"). |
| 3 | بِعَمَلٍ | bi-ʿamalin | of a deed | بِ makes عَمَلٍ genitive (ـٍ). The tanwīn means "some deed". |
| 4 | يُدْخِلُنِي | yudkhilunī | that will admit me | Present tense, he, nominative (ـُ). It describes the deed. A protecting ن comes before ـي ("me"). |
| 5 | الْجَنَّةَ | al-jannata | Paradise | Accusative (ـَ): it is where I am admitted. |
| 6 | وَيُبَاعِدُنِي | wa-yubāʿidunī | and keeps me far | Present tense, he, nominative. The long ā marks the pattern that means putting distance between two things. |
| 7 | عَنِ النَّارِ | ʿani n-nāri | from the Fire | عَنْ takes a kasra before ال. It makes النَّارِ genitive (ـِ). |
| 8 | لَقَدْ سَأَلْتَ | la-qad saʾalta | you have indeed asked | لَ means "indeed". قَدْ means "has". سَأَلْتَ is past tense with ـتَ ("you"). |
| 9 | عَنْ عَظِيمٍ | ʿan ʿaẓīmin | about something great | عَنْ makes عَظِيمٍ genitive (ـٍ). |
| 10 | وَإِنَّهُ لَيَسِيرٌ | wa-innahu la-yasīrun | and it is easy | إِنَّ plus ـهُ ("it"). لَ adds strength before the second part. يَسِيرٌ is nominative (ـٌ). |
| 11 | عَلَى مَنْ يَسَّرَهُ اللَّهُ | ʿalā man yassarahu llāhu | for the one for whom Allah makes it easy | مَنْ means "the one whom". يَسَّرَ is past tense, he, with a doubled س. The attached ـهُ means "it". اللَّهُ is nominative (ـُ): it is who makes it easy. |
| 12 | عَلَيْهِ | ʿalayhi | for him | عَلَى becomes عَلَيْ before ـهِ. |
| 13 | تَعْبُدُ اللَّهَ | taʿbudu llāha | you worship Allah | Present tense, you, nominative (ـُ). اللَّهَ is accusative (ـَ). This and the rows that follow explain what the deed is. |
| 14 | لَا تُشْرِكْ بِهِ شَيْئًا | lā tushrik bihi shayʾan | associating nothing with Him | لَا here means "do not", and it makes the verb end in a silent letter. شَيْئًا is accusative (ـًا). |
| 15 | وَتُقِيمُ الصَّلَاةَ | wa-tuqīmu ṣ-ṣalāta | and you establish the prayer | Present tense, you, nominative. الصَّلَاةَ is accusative (ـَ). |
| 16 | وَتُؤْتِي الزَّكَاةَ | wa-tuʾtī z-zakāta | and you give the zakat | Present tense, you. The ـِي ending is part of the verb. الزَّكَاةَ is accusative (ـَ). |
| 17 | وَتَصُومُ رَمَضَانَ | wa-taṣūmu Ramaḍāna | and you fast Ramadan | رَمَضَانَ is accusative (ـَ), with no tanwīn, because it is a name ending in ـان. |
| 18 | وَتَحُجُّ الْبَيْتَ | wa-taḥujju l-bayta | and you make pilgrimage to the House | The ج is doubled because the verb's two root letters are the same. الْبَيْتَ is accusative (ـَ). |
</GrammarTable>

### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | أَلَا أَدُلُّكَ | a-lā adulluka | shall I not show you | أَ starts a question and لَا means "not". أَدُلُّ is present tense, I, nominative (ـُ), with a doubled ل. The attached ـكَ means "you". |
| 2 | عَلَى أَبْوَابِ الْخَيْرِ | ʿalā abwābi l-khayri | to the doors of goodness | عَلَى makes أَبْوَابِ genitive (ـِ). الْخَيْرِ is genitive because it completes it. |
| 3 | الصَّوْمُ جُنَّةٌ | aṣ-ṣawmu junnatun | fasting is a shield | Both are nominative: الصَّوْمُ is what the statement is about, and جُنَّةٌ is the second part. |
| 4 | وَالصَّدَقَةُ تُطْفِئُ | wa-ṣ-ṣadaqatu tuṭfiʾu | and charity extinguishes | الصَّدَقَةُ is nominative (ـُ). تُطْفِئُ is present tense, she, nominative. |
| 5 | الْخَطِيئَةَ | al-khaṭīʾata | sin | Accusative (ـَ): it is what is extinguished. |
| 6 | كَمَا يُطْفِئُ الْمَاءُ النَّارَ | ka-mā yuṭfiʾu l-māʾu n-nāra | as water extinguishes fire | كَمَا means "as". يُطْفِئُ is present tense, he. الْمَاءُ is nominative (ـُ): it is what extinguishes. النَّارَ is accusative (ـَ): it is what is extinguished. |
| 7 | وَصَلَاةُ الرَّجُلِ | wa-ṣalātu r-rajuli | and a person's prayer | صَلَاةُ is nominative (ـُ): it is one more door. الرَّجُلِ is genitive (ـِ) because it completes it. |
| 8 | فِي جَوْفِ اللَّيْلِ | fī jawfi l-layli | in the depth of the night | فِي makes جَوْفِ genitive (ـِ). اللَّيْلِ is genitive because it completes it. |
</GrammarTable>

### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | ثُمَّ تَلَا | thumma talā | then he recited | ثُمَّ means "then". تَلَا is past tense, he, and ends in a long ā. |
| 2 | تَتَجَافَى | tatajāfā | part, keep away | Present tense, she, because the sides are feminine. The ending is a long ā, so no vowel shows. |
| 3 | جُنُوبُهُمْ | junūbuhum | their sides | Nominative (ـُ): they are what keep away. The attached ـهُمْ means "their". |
| 4 | عَنِ الْمَضَاجِعِ | ʿani l-maḍājiʿi | from the beds | عَنْ takes a kasra before ال. It makes الْمَضَاجِعِ genitive (ـِ). |
| 5 | حَتَّى بَلَغَ | ḥattā balagha | until he reached | حَتَّى means "until". بَلَغَ is past tense, he. |
| 6 | يَعْمَلُونَ | yaʿmalūna | they used to do | The word he reached, quoted from the verse. Present tense, they, and the ـُونَ ending marks "they". |
</GrammarTable>

| 1 | بِمَلَاكِ ذَلِكَ كُلِّهِ | bi-malāki dhālika kullihi | of what holds all of that together | بِ makes مَلَاكِ genitive (ـِ). ذَلِكَ is genitive in position, and it never shows a vowel. كُلِّهِ is genitive (ـِ) and adds emphasis to ذَلِكَ. |
| 2 | فَأَخَذَ بِلِسَانِهِ | fa-akhadha bi-lisānihi | so he took hold of his tongue | فَ means "and then". أَخَذَ is past tense, he. بِ makes لِسَانِ genitive (ـِ). The attached ـهِ means "his". |
| 3 | وَقَالَ كُفَّ | wa-qāla kuffa | and said: restrain | كُفَّ is a command to one person. The ف is doubled, and a doubled verb like this ends in fatḥa in the command. |
| 4 | عَلَيْكَ | ʿalayka | on yourself | عَلَى becomes عَلَيْ before ـكَ. Here it means "for your own sake". |
| 5 | هَذَا | hādhā | this | A pointing word. It is what is to be restrained, and it never shows a case vowel. |
</GrammarTable>

### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | يَا نَبِيَّ اللَّهِ | yā nabiyya llāhi | O Prophet of Allah | A called name that is the first word of a pair ends in fatḥa. اللَّهِ is genitive (ـِ). |
| 2 | وَإِنَّا لَمُؤَاخَذُونَ | wa-innā la-muʾākhadhūna | and indeed we will be held to account | إِنَّ plus نَا ("we"). لَ adds strength. مُؤَاخَذُونَ is nominative: the ـُونَ ending marks a masculine plural. It means "ones who are taken to account". |
| 3 | بِمَا نَتَكَلَّمُ بِهِ | bi-mā natakallamu bihi | for what we say | بِ plus مَا ("that which"). نَتَكَلَّمُ is present tense, we, nominative (ـُ). بِهِ points back to مَا. |
| 4 | ثَكِلَتْكَ أُمُّكَ | thakilatka ummuka | may your mother lose you | A set phrase of surprise. ثَكِلَتْ is past tense, she, with the sense of a wish. The attached ـكَ means "you". أُمُّ is nominative (ـُ): she is who loses. |
| 5 | يَا مُعَاذُ | yā Muʿādhu | O Muadh | A single name being called ends in ḍamma with no tanwīn. |
| 6 | وَهَلْ | wa-hal | and is there | هَلْ starts a yes-or-no question. |
| 7 | يَكُبُّ النَّاسَ | yakubbu n-nāsa | throws people | Present tense, he, nominative, with a doubled ب. النَّاسَ is accusative (ـَ): they are who are thrown. |
| 8 | فِي النَّارِ عَلَى وُجُوهِهِمْ | fī n-nāri ʿalā wujūhihim | into the Fire on their faces | فِي makes النَّارِ genitive (ـِ). عَلَى makes وُجُوهِ genitive. The attached ـهِمْ means "their". |
| 9 | أَوْ قَالَ | aw qāla | or he said | أَوْ gives a choice between two wordings. Past tense, he. |
| 10 | عَلَى مَنَاخِرِهِمْ | ʿalā manākhirihim | on their noses | عَلَى makes مَنَاخِرِ genitive. This word normally takes no tanwīn, and here the attached ـهِمْ gives it a normal kasra. |
| 11 | إِلَّا حَصَائِدُ أَلْسِنَتِهِمْ | illā ḥaṣāʾidu alsinatihim | other than the harvests of their tongues | إِلَّا means "except". حَصَائِدُ is nominative (ـُ): it is what throws them. أَلْسِنَةِ is genitive (ـِ) because it completes it. |
</GrammarTable>
