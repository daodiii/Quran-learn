### Word by Word

<GrammarTable>
| # | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning | Why it is written this way |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|----------------------------|
| 1 | الدِّينُ | ad-dīnu | the religion | Nominative (ـُ): it is the thing being defined. The ل of الـ merges into the د, which is why the د is doubled. |
| 2 | النَّصِيحَةُ | an-naṣīḥatu | sincere advice | Nominative (ـُ): it is the second part of the statement. The ل of الـ merges into the ن, which is why the ن is doubled. It means wanting what is best for someone, with sincerity. |
| 3 | قُلْنَا | qulnā | we said | Past tense with ـنَا ("we"). The middle letter of قَالَ drops before the silent ن, so it reads qul-. |
| 4 | لِمَنْ | li-man | for whom | لِ ("for") plus مَنْ ("who"), a question word. |
| 5 | قَالَ | qāla | he said | Past tense, he. |
| 6 | لِلَّهِ | li-llāhi | for Allah | لِ makes اللَّهِ genitive (ـِ). The alif of ال drops after لِ. |
| 7 | وَلِكِتَابِهِ | wa-li-kitābihi | and for His Book | وَ adds an item. لِ makes كِتَابِ genitive (ـِ). The attached ـهِ means "His". |
| 8 | وَلِرَسُولِهِ | wa-li-rasūlihi | and for His Messenger | Built the same way: genitive (ـِ), with ـهِ "His". |
| 9 | وَلِأَئِمَّةِ الْمُسْلِمِينَ | wa-li-a'immati l-muslimīna | and for the leaders of the Muslims | أَئِمَّةِ is genitive (ـِ) after لِ, and it takes no tanwīn. الْمُسْلِمِينَ is genitive too, because it completes أَئِمَّةِ. A masculine plural ends in ـِينَ for the genitive. |
| 10 | وَعَامَّتِهِمْ | wa-ʿāmmatihim | and their common people | Genitive (ـِ): it is joined to أَئِمَّةِ, so it takes the same case. The attached ـهِمْ means "their", meaning the Muslims'. |
</GrammarTable>
