This list holds the 200 most frequent words in the Quran: the 40 most common small words (particles, pronouns and the like), the 80 most common verbs and the 80 most common nouns and adjectives. About **62% of all the words in the Quran** (48,000 of 77,429) are built on one of these 200, so they are your best vocabulary investment.

Every number on this page was counted from the [Quranic Arabic Corpus](https://corpus.quran.com) (version 0.4). A count covers every form of the word: *qāla* includes *yaqūlu*, *qul* and *qālū*.

<h2 id="how-to-use-this-list">How to Use This List</h2>

**Study Strategy:**

1. **Start with particles (1-40)** — These function words appear in nearly every verse and unlock sentence structure understanding
2. **Progress to verbs (41-120)** — Action words that convey the Quran's core messages
3. **Build noun vocabulary (121-200)** — Concrete and abstract concepts central to Quranic themes

**Learning Tips:**

- Focus on recognition first (reading comprehension), then production (writing/speaking)
- Connect each word to its root pattern to accelerate learning related terms
- Look any word up in [Word Lookup](/resources/word-lookup/) to see its root, form and grammar
- Practice with [level quizzes](/learn/) that reinforce high-frequency vocabulary

**Frequency Data:**

- The number in the Freq. column is how many times the word occurs in the Quran, counted from the corpus
- Each verb form of a root is counted on its own. أَنْزَلَ (Form IV) and نَزَّلَ (Form II) both mean "send down" and have separate counts
- Higher frequency = greater comprehension impact per word learned

<Callout type="tip" title="Power Law Effect">
Learning these 200 words lets you recognise about six words in every ten of the Quran. The 40 small words in Part 1 alone are the stem of about 30% of all Quranic words, and they appear in nearly every verse.
</Callout>
