## What the learner can do at the end

1. Read the twelve joined pronouns and say which are the k family ("you") and the h family ("he, she, they").
2. Say what job the same ending does on a noun (muḍāf ilayh, genitive in place), on a preposition (the noun after it, genitive in place), on a verb (the object, accusative in place) and after inna (ism inna, accusative in place).
3. Tell a doer ending from an object ending on a past verb (the vowel before -nā), and read a verb with two joined pronouns: doer first, object last.
4. Choose -ī (noun, most prepositions) or -nī (verb, and min/ʿan with the doubled nūn), and name the protecting nūn.
5. Spell hum/him, hu/hi after a kasra or a yāʾ with sukūn (link to L2.07).
6. Go through four verses word by word (7:23, 3:8, 2:3, 26:78).

## The lesson, step by step

Five steps, 30 blocks (the overview is not counted). 15 `Exercise`, 12 `Check`, 4 `Parse`.

**Step 1. One set of endings** (6 blocks)
- `overview`: Formula and Start (goal, 45 minutes, before, six terms).
- `see`: 7:23 (part), -nā three times. Check 1.
- `what`: attached versus separate pronoun. Check 2.
- `chart`: Conjugation table, كِتَاب with twelve pronouns.
- `first-book`: al-Ajurrumiyyah, chapter on the object: the twelve joined pronouns and the twelve separate ones.
- `families`: the k family and the h family. Check 3.

**Step 2. One set of endings, three places** (7 blocks)
- `table`: the twelve endings on a noun, بِ and ضَرَبَ.
- `cases`: table of job and case in place for each place. Check 4.
- `noun`: 2:163 (start). Check 5.
- `noun-changes`: tanwīn goes, ة becomes ت, the noun keeps its case, the five nouns, dual and plural lose the nūn, the hidden ending before ـِي.
- `prep`: 1:7 (part) and the table of six prepositions with -hu, -hum, -ī. Note on hā and mīm.
- `hum-him`: the spelling rule from L2.07. Check 6.
- `verb`: 7:23 (part), the object.

**Step 3. Me, inna, and the doer** (4 blocks)
- `me`: -ī and -nī, the protecting nūn. Check 7.
- `inna`: 3:9 (part). Check 8.
- `doer-object`: ظَلَمْنَا and ظَلَمَنَا. Check 9.
- `order`: Conjugation table of ضَرَبَ with six doers and -hu. Check 10.

**Step 4. Five mistakes, four verses word by word** (9 blocks)
- Mistakes 1 to 5: `mistake-order` (doer and object), `mistake-noun` (al- and tanwīn; Check 11), `mistake-me`, `mistake-separate` (Check 12), `mistake-split`.
- `parse-1` 7:23, `parse-2` 3:8, `parse-3` 2:3, `parse-4` 26:78.

**Step 5. Practice** (4 blocks)
- `practice-1`: Exercises 1 to 5.
- `practice-2`: Exercises 6 to 9.
- `practice-3`: Exercises 10 to 15, the verses earlier lessons pointed to.
- `done`: Formula, summary, "Next", Sources.

## Errors fixed from the old lesson

1. Examples 1 to 3 (An-Nas 114:1, 114:2, 114:3) contain no attached pronoun. The old text says so ("this is not an attached pronoun", "this verse establishes the base form"). Dropped.
2. The introduction says "رَبِّكَ (rabbika) 'your Lord' in later verses" of An-Nas. The surah has no رَبِّكَ. Dropped.
3. Exercise 3 invents a verse ("Verse 7 (example): مِنْ شَرِّ ٱلْوَسْوَاسِ ٱلْخَنَّاسِ (implied: ... فِي صُدُورِهِمْ)"), a "modified example" رَبِّهِمْ, and labels 114:5 as showing attached pronouns although it has none. Replaced by a real verse (3:9).
4. "ـنِي on verbs and prepositions" (note 1 and mistake 3): prepositions take plain ـِي (لِي, بِي) except مِنْ and عَنْ (مِنِّي, عَنِّي) and إِلَىٰ / عَلَىٰ (إِلَيَّ, عَلَيَّ). Corrected in block `me`.
5. "The extra نِ prevents vowel collision" is unsourced. Replaced by the term نُون الْوِقَايَة, with al-Jadwal's words for it on 26:78 ("for protection") and a plain explanation of the name.
6. Exercise 4 says هُوَ is "emphasis in a verbal sentence". In هُوَ يُحِبُّهَا, هُوَ starts the sentence: it is the mubtadaʾ of a nominal sentence whose khabar is a verbal sentence (L2.01). Corrected.
7. The related-lessons list has "L4.08 Pronoun Agreement" pointing to the subject-pronouns lesson. Dropped; links are renumbered to the new Level 3 order.
8. Table row "3rd f sg: her / her" and similar glosses were loose. The new tables give one plain English gloss per row, and "men" versus "women" in the plural rows.
9. The old chart lists the 12 forms without saying that the first-person singular has a second shape in the same row. The new table has "(verb: ـنِي)" in the Ending cell.
10. The old lesson never said how -nā on a past verb can be the doer or the object. Added (block `doer-object`); this was the one place a learner of Level 2 would stumble (7:23, 108:1).
