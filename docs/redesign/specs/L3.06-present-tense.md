# Content spec: L3.06 The Present Tense

Written 2026-10-28. This says what the rewritten L3.06 teaches, with which verses, and how each verse is parsed. The lesson is `src/content/lessons/level-3/06-present-tense.mdx` (order 6; it was L3.04 before the 2026-10-28 reorder, old text on `main` as `04-present-tense.mdx`). It follows the definition of a complete lesson (end of `docs/redesign/CONTENT-INVENTORY.md`).

## What the learner can do at the end

1. Say that a present-tense verb starts with one of أ ن ي ت, and which persons each letter stands for.
2. Split a present-tense verb into prefix, stem and ending, and say who does it from the prefix and the ending.
3. Read the whole table of 13 forms on a real Form I verb (عَلِمَ, root ع ل م), and see the two lookalike pairs (she and you, one man; they two, women and you two).
4. Say why the verb ends in -u (the plain form, marfūʿ: no little word before it changes it), and that a verb does not take a case the way a noun does.
5. Know the five verbs (-āni, -ūna, -īna): the nūn is an ending, it stays in the plain form, and it drops when a little word changes the verb (preview of L3.07).
6. Know that the -na form (they, women; you all, women) is built (مبني): the nūn is the doer and nothing changes it.
7. Recognise hollow (يَقُولُ, يَكُونُ) and defective (يَدْعُو, يَهْدِي, يَرْمُونَ) present-tense verbs, know their share of the Quran's verbs, and know where Level 4 teaches them.
8. Go through four verses word by word.

## The lesson, step by step

5 steps, 32 blocks (the overview block is not counted in "n of m"). Each block has its own example beside the text.

**Step 1. Prefix and ending (hero), 5 blocks**
1. `overview`: Formula (prefix plus stem plus ending), `Start` with five terms.
2. `see`: 2:77, two verbs of one root, -ūna and -u. ✓ 1
3. `time`: 13:42 and 26:49. Present, repeated action, future; سَـ and سَوْفَ. ✓ 2
4. `prefixes`: the four letters (tiles of the four persons), al-Ajurrumiyyah quote with أنيت. ✓ 3
5. `parts`: prefix, stem, ending, on يَعْلَمُونَ; past has only endings (L3.05); an attached object like -ka is not the doer (L3.02). ✓ 4

**Step 2. The whole table, 7 blocks**
6. `whole`: `Conjugation` of يَعْلَمُ (13 rows); the vowel after the middle letter depends on the verb (يَنْصُرُ, يَضْرِبُ); why 13 rows and not 14.
7. `same-shape`: تَـ twice, 13:42 and 5:116 (she, you, I). ✓ 5
8. `past-present`: 5:116 past and present of the same person; the table of what moved. ✓ 6
9. `i-we-you`: 7:62, 36:76 (أَـ, نَـ, تَـ + -ūna); أَعْبُدُ (109:2) named in text. ✓ 7
10. `dual`: 5:75, -āni. ✓ 8
11. `one-woman`: 11:73, -īna. ✓ 9
12. `many-women`: 12:48, -na, the nūn is the doer. ✓ 10

**Step 3. The ending, 5 blocks**
13. `book`: al-Ajurrumiyyah: ḍamma for a verb with nothing attached, the nūn as a sign, the five verbs, "raised until a naṣb or jazm word comes in".
14. `plain-u`: 2:77, أَنَّ changes the noun, not the verb; a verb does not take a case the way a noun does (L2.04's promise); the "plain form" (marfūʿ). ✓ 11
15. `nun-stays`: the five verbs (tiles); the nūn is the ending, the و ا ي is the doer; examples 2:77, 5:75, 11:73; the nūn stays because no little word changes the verb (L2.04, L2.05 promise). ✓ 12
16. `nun-drops`: 3:92, preview of L3.07: لَن and حَتَّىٰ drop the nūn; the -u of a verb without nūn becomes -a or sukūn (L3.07). Note: 112:3 (L1.11 promise). ✓ 13
17. `na-built`: 2:232, the -na form stays the same after أَن; comparison table. ✓ 14

**Step 4. Verbs that change shape (required block), 4 blocks**
18. `two-kinds`: tiles (hollow, defective, regular); table with the shares (26.7%, 18.0%); the prefixes and endings stay, the middle or last letter changes.
19. `hollow`: 2:117, يَقُولُ and يَكُونُ; four prefixes on ق و ل (6:50, 50:30); قَالَ and كَانَ are the 7th and 10th most common words. ✓ 15
20. `defective`: 10:25 (hidden -u, al-Jadwal says so for this verse) and 24:4 (يَرْمُونَ). ✓ 16
21. `learn-more`: L4.11, L4.12, L4.13 with links.

**Step 5. Mistakes, word by word, practice, 11 blocks**
22. `mistake-prefix`: تَـ means three things; a word that starts with أ ن ي ت is not always present tense (نَصَرَ, أَخَذَ, تَابَ). (old mistake 1 + new)
23. `mistake-ending`: -ūna / -na, -āni / -ūna, -īna / -ūna. (old mistakes 3 and 6)
24. `mistake-form`: an ending in -a, a sukūn or a dropped nūn means a little word changed the verb; present is not only "now"; the middle vowel varies and other verb forms use the same letters. (old mistakes 2, 4, 5, new vowel point)
25. to 28. `parse-1` to `parse-4`: 2:77, 5:116, 10:25, 12:48.
29. to 31. `practice-1` (exercises 1 to 3), `practice-2` (4 to 7), `practice-3` (8 to 10).
32. `done`: summary, next lessons, `Sources`.

## Errors fixed from the old lesson

1. 10:25: the old lesson said يَدْعُو has "damma on final waw". The ḍamma is hidden (al-Jadwal: الضمة المقدرة على الواو).
2. The example "يُؤْمِنُونَ بِٱللَّهِ وَٱلْيَوْمِ ٱلْآخِرِ" was cited as 2:62. 2:62 has مَنْ ءَامَنَ بِٱللَّهِ وَٱلْيَوْمِ ٱلْءَاخِرِ (a past verb). Replaced by 2:77.
3. Exercise 4 cited "Luqman 31:26" for يَعْلَمُ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَاوَاتِ وَٱلْأَرْضِ. 31:26 reads لِلَّهِ مَا فِى ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضِ. Replaced by 49:16.
4. "Form I pattern is يَفْعُلُ for most verbs" (unsourced). Form I has three present vowels (يَعْلَمُ, يَنْصُرُ, يَضْرِبُ); the lesson says the vowel depends on the verb.
5. The table listed 13 rows under a heading that said 14. The new lesson says why (the grammar books count "you two" for men and for women).
6. Table note "When a suffix with its own vowel follows, the final root letter takes fatḥa": not true before -ūna (ḍamma) or -īna (kasra). Removed.
7. Exercise 4 item 3 (لَحَافِظُونَ, a participle) is not about the present tense. Moved out; L3.19 teaches participles.
8. Three typos in the old text: "yuنْزِلُ" with an Arabic letter inside the transliteration, and "yadhhhabūna" and "yadhhhabna" with an extra h.
9. Related lessons used old numbers or wrong titles: "L3.06 Imperative" (now L3.08), "L3.05 Subjunctive and Jussive" (now L3.07), "L3.10 Weak Verbs" pointing to L4.11, "L4.03 Participles" pointing to L3.19, and "L4.10 Conditional" pointing to L4.03. The new lesson links by the new numbers.
10. The old lesson called the present-tense ending "indicative mood" and never mentioned the five verbs, the nūn, or the built -na form. The al-Ajurrumiyyah account is now the base.
