# Content spec: L3.17 Verbs with a Long ā (Form III)

Written 2026-10-28. Follows the shape of `L2.01-nominal-sentence.md`. Lesson file: `src/content/lessons/level-3/17-verb-form-iii.mdx` (old file on `main`: `13-verb-form-iii.mdx`). Source for every parse: *al-Jadwal* (Maḥmūd Ṣāfī). The lesson moved from L3.13 to L3.17 because Form III has 334 verb words (Quran Learn's count from the Quranic Arabic Corpus), fewer than Forms I, IV, II, VIII, V and X.

## What the learner can do at the end

1. Spot Form III by the long ā after the first root letter (small alif or full alif).
2. Build the past, present and command of a real verb (جَاهَدَ, يُجَاهِدُ, جَاهِدْ) and say where the ḍamma goes on the prefix.
3. See the nūn drop after لَا and حَتَّىٰ, as in Form I.
4. Say how the passive changes the ā (to و).
5. Say what Form III adds in the verses shown (a second party: with, against, towards), and that some verbs have none.
6. Tell Form III from look-alikes (قَالَ, كَانَ, ءَامَنَ, ٱلْقَٰعِدُونَ) by finding the root.
7. Preview the participle (مُفَاعِل), the two verbal nouns (فِعَال, مُفَاعَلَة) and Form VI (تَ + Form III).
8. Go through four verses word by word.

## Errors fixed from the old lesson

1. The three "meanings" (reciprocal, attempted, competitive) were taught as a fixed list of three, with 12 example verbs (كَاتَبَ, سَالَمَ, سَابَقَ, نَافَسَ, صَارَعَ, غَالَبَ, حَاكَمَ ...) that are not Quran verbs; the old exercise even admits سَابَقَ and عَالَمَ are unused. The new lesson uses only verbs in verses and shows a pattern, not three fixed meanings. *Checked* (the Corpus has no such forms among the 334).
2. "Mistake 2" called قَاتِلٌ the participle that looks like Form III. قَاتِلٌ is the Form I participle; the Form III participle is مُقَاتِل. *Certain.*
3. "Mistake 4" said Form III often needs a preposition (مَعَ, ضِدَّ). The Quran takes a direct object (قَٰتِلُوهُمْ) and has no ضِدَّ construction with it. *Checked* in the verses.
4. Example 2 said يُخَادِعُونَ means "try to deceive" against Form I "actually deceive". The lesson does not use this claim; it shows the two verbs next to each other and who the object is. *Needs a source* for the "attempt" reading.
5. The present pattern was written يُفَاعِلٌُ (a tanwīn and a ḍamma together) and the command pattern فَاعِلٌُوا. Fixed: يُفَاعِلُ, جَاهِدُوا۟.
6. Example 4 labelled عَاهَدتُّمْ's pattern "فَاعَلَ (second person plural)", mixing pattern and person. Fixed in `past`.
7. Old explanation of the term مُفَاعَلَة ("the form is called this because its verbal noun is this") has no source and was dropped. *Needs a source.*
8. Wrong links: L3.01, L3.02 and L3.03 named by their old numbers, "L3.14 Verb Form IV", "L3.16 Verb Form VI", "L3.17 Verb Forms VII & VIII". Replaced by today's numbers.
