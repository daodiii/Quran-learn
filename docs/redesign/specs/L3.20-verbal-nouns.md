# Content spec: L3.20 Verbal nouns (ٱلْمَصْدَر)

Written 2026-10-28. File: `src/content/lessons/level-3/20-verbal-nouns.mdx`. Old lesson: `main:src/content/lessons/level-3/20-verbal-nouns.mdx`.

## What the learner can do at the end

1. Say what a verbal noun (maṣdar) is: the name of an action, a noun with no tense and no doer, taking al-, tanwīn and a case.
2. Tell a verbal noun from a doer word and a done-to word of the same root.
3. Know that Form I verbal nouns come in many heard patterns (سَمَاعِيّ), and that one verb can have several.
4. Name the regular pattern of a verbal noun from forms II to X (قِيَاسِيّ), and know that a few derived ones are heard.
5. Read أَنْ plus a verb as one verbal noun (المصدر المؤوّل).
6. Go through four verses word by word, and recognise a verb's own verbal noun as a preview of the absolute object (L4.07).

## The lesson, step by step (5 steps, 29 blocks)

**Step 1, The action name (4):** overview with Start; `see` (1:2, Check 1); `family` (ح م د, Check 2); `noun` (case, al-, tanwīn, idafah; Check 3).

**Step 2, Form I (5):** `heard` (Check 4); `patterns` (table, Corpus frequencies, Check 5); `many` (تَوْب/تَوْبَة, جَهَالَة/جَهْل, مَيْل, الرُّشْد/الرَّشَد; Check 6); `mim` (مصدر ميمي; Check 7); `names` (رَبّ, وِفَاق, ٱلْكِتَٰب).

**Step 3, Forms II to X (5):** `two-kinds` (4:65; Check 8); `table` (nine rows, Checks 9 and 10); `form-x` (9:114); `form-iv` (3:19; Check 11); `form-iii` (2:137; Check 12).

**Step 4, In a sentence (4):** `jobs` (16:90; Check 13); `an` (2:237; Check 14); `keeps-work` (2:217; Check 15); `preview` (33:33; Check 16).

**Step 5, Mistakes and practice (11):** four mistakes (doer/done-to, guessing a Form I pattern, shape alone, exceptions in II to X; Check 17); four Parse panels (1:2, 3:19, 2:237, 4:65); two practice blocks (7 exercises); summary and Sources.

## Exercises (7; old lesson had 4)

1. Action, doer or done-to (ح م د, 4 words). 2. Form and pattern of five verbal nouns. 3.
