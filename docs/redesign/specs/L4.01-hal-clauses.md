# Content spec: L4.01 Hal Clauses

Written 2026-10-28. This is the old L4.01 "Hal Clauses (Circumstantial Expressions)", same number. It follows the definition of a complete lesson (end of `docs/redesign/CONTENT-INVENTORY.md`). The lesson is `src/content/lessons/level-4/01-hal-clauses.mdx`. The old lesson had no `titleArabic`; the new one has اَلْحَال.

## What the learner can do at the end

1. Say what a ḥāl is (the state of the owner while the action happens) and find it in a verse.
2. Find the ṣāḥib al-ḥāl (the owner): the doer or the object.
3. Give the rules of al-Ajurrumiyyah: a noun, accusative, indefinite, after a complete sentence, definite owner.
4. Tell the three kinds: one word, a whole sentence (verb, nominal with wāw al-ḥāl, past verb with qad), a phrase (shibh jumla).
5. Say that a ḥāl may name a lasting state (4:28, 2:91), and use the real test instead of "temporary".
6. Tell a ḥāl from an adjective, a tamyīz (pointer to L4.02), a time word, a place phrase and a new sentence.
7. Go through four verses word by word.

## The lesson, step by step

5 steps, 26 blocks.

**Step 1: What a ḥāl is** (hero; 5 blocks)
1. `overview`: Start, formula "action plus state".
2. `see`: 28:21 (start). Check 1.
3. `name`: al-Ajurrumiyyah's definition and example جَاءَ زَيْدٌ رَاكِبًا. Check 2.
4. `owner`: 2:119 (part), the owner is the doer or the object. Check 3.
5. `conditions`: the four rules (noun, accusative, indefinite, after a complete sentence) and the definite owner; the "take it out" test. Check 4.

**Step 2: Three kinds** (5 blocks)
6. `one-word`: 39:73 (end) and a made-up sentence; -īna; note on كُسَالَىٰ. Check 5.
7. `verb-sentence`: 12:16; no wāw; root and pattern of يَبْكُونَ; second ḥāl in 28:21. Check 6.
8. `nominal`: 4:43 (part) and a made-up sentence; wāw al-ḥāl. Check 7.
9. `past-sentence`: 5:61 (part); wāw + qad + past verb. Check 8.
10. `phrase`: 28:79 (part); the swap test. Check 9.

**Step 3: Look-alikes** (5 blocks)
11. `fixed`: 2:91 (part); 4:28 in the text; the lasting state. Check 10.
12. `naat`: made-up pairs; 12:2. Check 11.
13. `tamyiz`: pointer to L4.02; 28:78, 4:69. Check 12.
14. `not-hal`: 12:63 (part), made-up place phrase; time word, place phrase, answer of lammā, new sentence (12:19). Check 13.
15. `earlier`: the table of 18 rows (20 verses) from earlier lessons.

**Step 4: Mistakes and parsing** (8 blocks)
16. to 19. `mistake-definite`, `mistake-ending`, `mistake-waw`, `mistake-temporary`.
20. to 23. `parse-1` to `parse-4`: 28:21 (5 cards), 4:43 (7), 5:61 (7), 28:79 (4): 23 cards.

**Step 5: Practice** (3 blocks)
24. `practice-1`: exercises 1 to 6. 25. `practice-2`: exercises 7 to 12. 26. `done`: summary, next, links, Sources.
