# Content spec: L1.11 Checkpoint, Surah Al-Ikhlas

Written 2026-10-28. Lesson file: `src/content/lessons/level-1/11-checkpoint-al-ikhlas.mdx`. Every parse follows al-Jadwal (Maḥmūd Ṣāfī); al-Durr al-Maṣūn for disputes. Level 1 depth: type, definiteness, gender, number, case and mark, with the Level 1 lesson that taught each point and the Level 2 or 3 lesson that teaches it in full.

## What the learner can do at the end

1. Read all four verses aloud, with the marks (sukūn, shadda, tanween, small wāw, alif waṣla, sun letter) and the blend of the nūn of يَكُن into the lām of لَّهُۥ.
2. Go through all 15 written words (18 pieces) and say type, definite or indefinite, gender, number, case and the mark that shows it.
3. Say that verses 1 and 2 are nominal sentences, verse 3 is verbs and little words only, and verse 4 is a kāna sentence (a preview of L2.11).
4. Give both readings of هُوَ in 112:1 and say what stays the same.
5. Say why كُفُوًا ends in -an, and give the second view on the khabar of يَكُن.
6. Tell a case from a job (أَحَدٌ twice, -un, two jobs), and know that a pronoun has a case by place.
7. Use the same tools on a fresh verse (2:163, start).

## Checked against al-Jadwal and al-Durr (2026-10-28)

Read with `jadwal.mjs 112:1 112:2 112:3 112:4` (the page is the whole surah) and `--book aldur-almasoon` for 112:1, 112:2, 112:4 (112:3 has no entry). Also `jadwal.mjs 109:1 17:111 2:247 2:163`.

Al-Jadwal names: ٱللَّهُ as mubtadaʾ thānin; أَحَدٌ as its khabar; footnotes for the other reading (ٱللَّهُ khabar of هُوَ, أَحَدٌ second khabar); the places of the sentences in all four verses (including the second and third khabar of هُوَ, with the alternative "new sentence"); لَّهُۥ as tied to the khabar of يَكُن, which is كُفُوًا; the forms of ٱلصَّمَدُ and كُفُوًا; the point on the word order of verse 4. It does not list a role for each single word of verses 3 and 4. Those follow its parse of the same pattern in 17:111 (لم حرف نفي وجزم; يكن مضارع ناقص مجزوم; شريك اسم كان مرفوع; له متعلق بخبر كان; يتخذ مضارع مجزوم والفاعل هو; الواو عاطفة) and in 109:1/17:111 for the command (فعل أمر ... والفاعل أنت).

Al-Durr al-Maṣūn read: **112:1** two ways to understand هُوَ (it points to what the listeners asked about; or it is ḍamīr al-shaʾn explained by the sentence after it), with the other parses of ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ (Allah mubtadaʾ and aḥad khabar; Allah the first khabar and aḥad the second; Allah a badal; aḥad the khabar of a left-out هُوَ); the hamza of أَحَد from wāw (the usual view) or original (Abū al-Baqāʾ); Thaʿlab's wāḥid against aḥad and the answer aḥad wa-ʿishrūn. **112:2** ٱلصَّمَدُ is faʿal with the meaning of mafʿūl, "the master who is turned to in needs"; a weak view that it is a ṣifa. **112:4** the two parses of كُفُوًا (khabar yakun with aḥad as ism and لَّهُۥ tied to it; or لَّهُۥ khabar and كُفُوًا a ḥāl from aḥad, by Makkī and Abū al-Baqāʾ), Abū Ḥayyān's rejection of the second, and al-Samīn's reply. The lesson follows the first view, which is al-Jadwal's.

Count: 4 main verses: 2 agree word by word (112:1, 112:4), 2 agree at sentence level only (112:2, 112:3), 0 disagree, 0 replaced. Exercise verse: 1 agrees word by word (2:163).

## Errors fixed from the old lesson

1. Verse 4 was typed أَحَدٌ; the Tanzil text is أَحَدٌۢ, with the small ۢ above the tanween.
2. Exercise 1 counted "approximately 6" nouns but listed 7, "approximately 5" particles, and added to 15. The pieces are 8 nouns (including two pronouns), 4 verbs and 6 little words: 18 pieces in 15 written words (Corpus tags, counted in full).
3. The root of أَحَدٌ was given as و-ح-د with no note. The Corpus lists أ-ح-د; al-Durr gives the wāw view as the usual one and records the other.
4. "The Quran chose أَحَدٌ over وَاحِدٌ to show..." was stated as fact. It is now Thaʿlab's distinction and the reply, both from al-Durr, and al-Jadwal's note on the meaning of the two names.
5. هُوَ in 112:1 was given no analysis. The lesson now gives al-Jadwal's reading and the second one (ḍamīr al-shaʾn) from al-Durr.
6. 112:4: the old table gave no role for كُفُوًا or لَّهُۥ beyond "accusative". Now: khabar of يَكُن, لَّهُۥ tied to it, أَحَدٌۢ the ism, and the second view named.
7. Exercise 3 said ٱللَّهُ is "the subject" in both verses. It is the mubtadaʾ.
8. The Gender column said the surah speaks of Allah "using masculine forms, as you would expect". This is grammatical gender only; the lesson says "masculine, singular" as grammar and does not say more.
9. The transliteration of verse 4 (*wa-lam yakun lahū*) missed the blend written in the Tanzil text (no sukūn on the nūn, shadda on the lām): *yakul-lahū*.
10. ٱلصَّمَدُ and أَحَدٌ were labelled "Noun/Adjective". The Corpus tags both as nouns; al-Jadwal calls ٱلصَّمَدُ a describing word of the pattern faʿal. The lesson says noun and gives the pattern.
11. Exercise 2 said "two words have tanween" and listed أَحَدٌ for verses 1 and 4 together; there are three tanween marks on two words.
12. Em dashes in the English.

## What is not checked

- Al-Jadwal's online text does not list each word's role for verses 3 and 4. Those roles rest on its parse of the same pattern in 17:111 (read) and 109:1, and on the standard formulas. The passive formula for يُولَدْ (نائب الفاعل ضمير مستتر) was not read for any verse.
- The blend *yakul-lahū* is read from the Tanzil marks (no sukūn on the nūn, shadda on the lām). It is a reading rule from tajwīd; none of the allowed sources was read for it, and nothing in the grammar depends on it.
- The meaning of the small ۢ is described only as "a reading sign"; the lesson gives no rule for it.
- The English meanings and the Arabic i'rab lines have not been read by an Arabic speaker.
- Links to L2.01 to L2.12 and L3.xx use their file names; L2.xx and L3.xx contents were not re-read for this lesson, except L2.01 and L2.12 for layout.
