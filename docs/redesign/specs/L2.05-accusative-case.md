# Content spec: L2.05 The Accusative Case

Written 2026-10-27. Lesson file: `src/content/lessons/level-2/05-accusative-case.mdx`. Follows the model spec `L2.01-nominal-sentence.md` and the definition of a complete lesson (end of `CONTENT-INVENTORY.md`).

## What the learner can do at the end

1. Say that the accusative (naṣb) is the case of the object and of a few other jobs, and find an object by asking "what? whom?".
2. Name the mark on each kind of word: -a / -an, -ayni (dual), -īna (sound masculine plural), a kasra (sound feminine plural), an alif (five nouns), a hidden fatḥa, and the pronoun that shows nothing.
3. Name the jobs that make a word accusative: object, ism inna, khabar kāna, time word, place word. Recognise ḥāl, tamyīz, mafʿūl muṭlaq and mafʿūl li-ajlih as previews.
4. Tell accusative from genitive when the mark is the same (-īna, -ayni, a kasra), and tell a verb's own -a from a case ending.
5. Go through a short verse word by word: role, case, mark, reason.

## The lesson, step by step

5 steps, 32 blocks (5, 9, 7, 8, 3). 20 quick checks, 9 exercises, 4 word-by-word verses.

**Step 1. The receiver case** (hero): overview (`overview`); `see` 3:181 doer -u / receiver -a; `receiver` 2:43, naṣb, mafʿūl bihi, "what? whom?", English him/me; `order` 2:87 fronted object; `pronoun` 2:21 -kum object, "in the place of".

**Step 2. The marks**: `five-signs` al-Ajurrumiyyah quote (five signs), which are for nouns; `an` 17:11 and 4:96 (alif and tāʾ marbūṭa); `dual` 14:33; `masc-plural` 4:165; `same-look` 3:76 vs 2:43 (accusative vs genitive); `fem-plural` 2:87 (kasra; مَرْيَمَ warning); `hidden-fatha` 4:164; `five-nouns` 12:16; `marks-table` summary.

**Step 3. The jobs**: `job-map` list with lesson for each; `inna` 14:34; `kana` 14:34 beside 17:11 (same word, two endings); `when` 17:1; `where` 17:1; `preview-1` ḥāl and mafʿūl li-ajlih; `preview-2` tamyīz and mafʿūl muṭlaq.

**Step 4. Mistakes and word by word**: four mistakes (`mistake-a` every -a; `mistake-b` kasra; `mistake-c` looking only for -a; `mistake-d` khabar of inna), then `parse-1` 3:76, `parse-2` 14:33, `parse-3` 12:16, `parse-4` 4:164.

**Step 5. Practice**: `practice-1` (exercises 1 to 5), `practice-2` (6 to 9), `done`.

## Errors fixed from the old lesson

1. "Time: يَوْمًا" was listed as a ḥāl in the body but as a ẓarf zamān in exercise 4. A time word is a ẓarf, not a ḥāl. Fixed: time and place words have their own blocks.
2. In the exercise on رَأَيْتُ, "hidden subject أَنَا". The doer is the -tu on the verb, not a hidden pronoun. Fixed in exercise 1.
3. "Ḥāl is always accusative and indefinite" with only one example. Dropped the absolute claim; ḥāl is a preview.
4. Exercise 3 translated كَانَ as "is". Fixed ("has always been", as in L2.01).
5. Exercise 4 and the made-up sentence جَاءَ رَسُولُ ٱللَّهِ يَوْمًا were not labelled made-up. Fixed.
6. Exercise 2 quoted ٱلْإِنسَانُ in a made-up form and the verse in a different spelling. The verse is now quoted exactly (14:34).
7. The ka in وَدَّعَكَ was called "accusative" with no explanation. Now explained as "in the place of" the accusative.
8. The dual was written "yaa + nun (ـَيْنِ)". It is yāʾ then the final nūn with kasra, or -ayni. Rewritten.
