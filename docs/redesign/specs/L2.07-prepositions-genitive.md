# Content spec: L2.07 Prepositions and the Noun After Them

Written 2026-10-27. Lesson file: `src/content/lessons/level-2/07-prepositions-genitive.mdx`. Every parse follows al-Jadwal (Maḥmūd Ṣāfī); al-Durr al-Maṣūn for disputes.

## What the learner can do at the end

1. Name the prepositions in al-Ajurrumiyyah's list (makhfūḍāt chapter) and say the main meanings of the eight common ones and the oath letters.
2. Say that the noun after a preposition (ism majrūr) is genitive, and show the mark, also when it is hidden (هُدًى) or only "in place" (a joined pronoun).
3. Read a joined pronoun after a preposition: bihim, ʿalayhim, lahu, ilayhi; know why hum becomes him, ilā/ʿalā become ilay-/ʿalay-, and li- becomes la- (except "me").
4. Say what a preposition phrase is tied to (mutaʿalliq): a verb, a verb-like word, or a left-out word; recognise an extra bi- that is tied to nothing.
5. Go through four verses word by word.

## Errors fixed from the old lesson

1. "The alif drops with li, bi and ka" then shows بِٱلْحَقِّ with the alif: contradiction. Only li loses the alif of ال in writing.
2. Sun letters: it says the nūn of min/ʿan assimilates. The lām of ال assimilates; min keeps its nūn.
3. Example 2 (16:65) quoted وَأَنْزَلْنَا مِنَ ٱلسَّمَاءِ مَاءً: not in the verse (it has وَٱللَّهُ أَنزَلَ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءً).
4. Example 1 (22:64) quoted وَٱلْأَرْضِ وَمَا فِيْهَا: not in the verse (it has وَمَا فِى ٱلْأَرْضِ).
5. Example 8 (2:74) quoted فَقُلُوبُهُمْ كَٱلْحِجَارَةِ: the verse has فَهِىَ كَٱلْحِجَارَةِ; the English also changed the meaning.
6. Example 5 (2:284) dropped the second مَا in وَمَا فِى ٱلْأَرْضِ.
7. Example 7 began with 49:11 under the wrong heading and a drafting remark ("let me provide") was left in the text.
8. Example 6 (12:17) treated the bi- of بِمُؤْمِنٍ as an ordinary predicate preposition; the bāʾ is extra (al-Jadwal).
9. Exercise 3 said كُلِّ is genitive "for two reasons". A word has one case; the idafah does not add a second.
10. Exercise 4 wrote بِٱسْمِ with an alif and explained a "hamzat wasl"; the Tanzil text writes بِسْمِ.
11. "This rule applies 100% of the time with no exceptions" did not say that many nouns show no visible genitive (hidden, in place of).
12. Exercise 2 quoted 2:64 as an example of "mercy from Allah" with مِنَ ٱللَّهِ; that verse has no مِنَ ٱللَّهِ.
13. The old list "eight prepositions" was given as the whole list; al-Ajurrumiyyah has more (rubba, the oath letters, mudh, mundhu).
