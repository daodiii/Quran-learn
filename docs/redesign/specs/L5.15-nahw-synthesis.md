## What the learner can do at the end

1. Find any point of syntax from Levels 1 to 4 on one of four maps (sentences, cases and moods, little words, objects with the ḥāl and the tamyīz) and name the lesson that teaches it.
2. Tell, for each row, whether the lesson calls it a rule, a usual pattern, a tendency or a teaching summary.
3. Take a whole verse apart with the five moves of L5.01 and give the lesson behind each point.
4. Tell the ten accusative jobs apart by their questions.
5. Say where the old L5.15 was wrong (condition words, حَتَّىٰ, case marks, the exception in لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ, the ism of the lā that denies the kind).
