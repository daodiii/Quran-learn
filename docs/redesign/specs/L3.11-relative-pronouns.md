# Content spec: L3.11 Relative Pronouns (who, which, what)

Written 2026-10-28. File: `src/content/lessons/level-3/11-relative-pronouns.mdx` (old lesson on `main`, same path). Every parse follows al-Jadwal (Maḥmūd Ṣāfī); al-Durr al-Maṣūn is cited once (the dual, 4:16). Text is the Tanzil text from the repo's Corpus file.

## What the learner can do at the end

1. Say what a joining noun (*ism mawṣūl*) is, and find the ṣila that finishes it.
2. Find the returning pronoun (*ʿāʾid*) in a ṣila: visible, hidden in the verb, or left out.
3. Choose the form that fits the noun before it: ٱلَّذِى, ٱلَّتِى, ٱلَّذِينَ, the dual (ٱلَّذَانِ / ٱلَّذَيْنِ), the women's ٱلَّٰتِى and ٱلَّٰٓـِٔى.
4. Say why the word never changes its shape (built, *mabnī*) and still has a case "in the place of" (*fī maḥall*) from its job.
5. Say that the ṣila has no place in the iʿrāb (*lā maḥalla lahā*), and tell it from a khabar sentence, which has one.
6. Name the three kinds of ṣila: verbal sentence, nominal sentence, phrase that works like a sentence (*shibh jumla*).
7. Tell the relative مَنْ and مَا from the question, condition, negative and verbal-noun-particle uses (relative use taught, others named; conditions in L4.03 and L4.04).

## The lesson, step by step

5 steps, 30 blocks (6, 5, 5, 5, 9). Quick checks are numbered 1 to 21.

**Step 1: The joining noun** (hero): 6 blocks
1. `overview`: formula, goal, 50 minutes, 8 terms. Before: L3.01, L3.02, L2.01, L2.04 to L2.06, L2.09.
2. `see`: 107:1. Check 1.
3. `alone`: ٱلَّذِى alone says nothing; *ism mawṣūl*, *ṣila*, root و ص ل; the formula. Check 2.
4. `noun`: it is a noun (agrees with L1.04, L1.06, L2.02, L2.09, L2.10; Corpus tag REL; Note on al-Jadwal's name). Check 3.
5. `returning`: 8:2; the *ʿāʾid*; matches the joining noun; ties back to L3.02 and L2.02. Check 4.
6. `hidden`: the hidden "he" in 107:1; the three shapes of the returning pronoun (attached pronoun, doer letter, hidden doer). Check 5.

**Step 2: The forms** : 5 blocks
7. `table`: the family in a `Conjugation` table; the Quran Learn count (1,446); Note on the spelling and the absent forms. Check 6.

## Errors fixed from the old lesson

1. "All forms begin with ٱلّ, the definite article merged with the relative pronoun." This contradicts L1.04 and L1.06 and the Corpus (no article is split off). The lesson says the ٱل belongs to the word.
2. Example 3 (65:11) called مَن a relative pronoun. Al-Jadwal calls it a condition word (the verbs are jussive and there is an answer). Replaced by 27:65 (relative) and 4:123 (condition).
3. Example 4 (2:4) said "no explicit pronoun links back because the passive verb implicitly refers to مَا". The hidden stand-in doer of the passive is the returning pronoun.
4. Example 5 (21:91) called the ـتْ of أَحْصَنَتْ a "tāʾ marbūṭa". It is the silent feminine tāʾ (see CONTENT-INVENTORY, L3.03 item 2). The lesson says only that the ending shows a feminine doer.
5. "The relative clause must be a complete sentence", with ٱلرَّجُلُ ٱلَّذِي فِي ٱلْبَيْتِ marked wrong. A preposition phrase or place word can be the ṣila (al-Jadwal 2:284, 27:65).
6. "مَنْ is for people, مَا for things" as a rule with a "WRONG" example. Al-Jadwal reads مَن for animals (24:45) and مَا for women (4:3); it is a guide.
7. "مَنْ يَعْمَلْ" given as a general relative ("whoever does"). With a jussive verb and an answer it is a condition word.
8. Example 1 gave ٱلَّذِينَ "Case: Genitive (in iḍāfah)" as if it declined. It is built and "in the place of" the genitive.
9. Made-up phrases (ٱلرَّجُلُ ٱلَّذِي كَتَبَ, ٱلْمَرْأَةُ ٱلَّتِي قَرَأَتْ, and others) were not labelled. They are now.
10. The table listed ٱللَّتَانِ and ٱللَّوَاتِي without saying the Quran has neither (the Corpus has no feminine dual and no ٱللَّوَاتِي). The lesson says so, with the Quran Learn count.
11. "The returning pronoun is required" ("Arabic often requires"): it is usual, and can be left out (36:52).
12. Related-lesson links used the old numbers (L3.08 Subject Pronouns is now L3.01). The new text uses today's numbers.
13. "L4.03 Conditional Sentences: advanced relative clause usage" (old link text). L4.03 teaches conditions, not relative clauses.
