## What the learner can do at the end

1. Say what a pattern is in this lesson (a shape that returns) and use four questions to find and check one (identify, catalog, analyse, compare). The four questions are Quran Learn's teaching order.
2. Read the shape `(وَ)إِذَا + noun + feminine past verb` in 81, 82 and 84 (18 verses), say why each verb ends in `-at`, tell a passive verb from a Form VII or Form VIII verb by its shape, and say what al-Jadwal calls the noun (the doer of a left-out verb).
3. Say that these clauses wait for an answer: `عَلِمَتْ نَفْسٌ` in 81:14 and 82:5, and left out in 84:1 and 80:33.
4. Read `وَمَآ أَدْرَىٰكَ مَا` + a noun in seven surahs of Juz 'Amma, with al-Jadwal's parts (two question words, one verb with two objects).
5. Count and compare the endings of verses (81, 82, 84, 99) without saying more than the counts show.
6. Say what the counts do not show (the patterns are not only in Juz 'Amma: 77:8 to 77:11, 69:3, 74:27, 77:14).
