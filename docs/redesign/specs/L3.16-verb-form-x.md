# Content spec: L3.16 Form X (ٱسْتَفْعَلَ)

Written 2026-10-28. Lesson file: `src/content/lessons/level-3/16-verb-form-x.mdx`. Old lesson: `git show main:src/content/lessons/level-3/18-verb-forms-ix-x.mdx` (Form X parts only; the Form IX parts move to L3.18). Same shape as the model spec (L2.01).

## What the learner can do at the end

1. Spot a Form X verb (ٱسْتَ in front, sukūn on the first root letter) and say what the Corpus tag (X) means.
2. Make and read the past, present and command of a Form X verb (ٱسْتَغْفَرَ, يَسْتَغْفِرُ, ٱسْتَغْفِرْ), and the passive (ٱسْتُفْعِلَ).
3. Say when the joining alif is said and when it is silent.
4. Guess a likely meaning from the root (asking for, counting as, being or acting), and know that some verbs are learned whole.
5. Peel ٱسْتَ, find the root, and recognise weak and doubled roots.
6. Tell Form X from look-alikes that start with ٱسْتَ (Form VIII: ٱسْتَوَىٰ, ٱسْتَبَقَ).
7. Recognise the doer word (مُسْتَفْعِل), passive word and verbal noun (ٱسْتِفْعَال) as previews of L3.19 and L3.20.
8. Go through four verses word by word.

## Errors fixed from the old lesson

1. Example 3 (12:25) called ٱسْتَبَقَا Form X. The Corpus tags it (VIII), root س ب ق.
2. Table row ٱسْتَوَىٰ as Form X "coming into a state", root "و-ي-ي". The Corpus tags it (VIII), root س و ي; al-Jadwal calls it five-letter.
3. Roots in the seeking table: ٱسْتَعَاذَ "ع-ذ-ب" (the root is ع و ذ) and ٱسْتَجَابَ "ج-ب-ب" (the root is ج و ب).
4. Rows for verbs that are not in the Quran (ٱسْتَحْسَنَ, ٱسْتَبْعَدَ, ٱسْتَيْقَظَ, and exercise verbs ٱسْتَعْلَمَ, ٱسْتَكْثَرَ as quoted). Not used.

6. Example 6 headed ٱسْتَقِيمُوا۟ but quoting ٱسْتَقَٰمُوا۟ (a past verb), and its Arabic of 2:45 omitted the opening وَ.
