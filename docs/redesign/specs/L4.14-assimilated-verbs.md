# Content spec: L4.14 Assimilated Verbs (al-Mithal)

Written 2026-10-28. It follows the definition of a complete lesson (end of `docs/redesign/CONTENT-INVENTORY.md`). The lesson is `src/content/lessons/level-4/14-assimilated-verbs.mdx`. It replaces the old lesson of the same name (file name, `title`, `level` and `order` are kept; `titleArabic` is new because the old file had none).

## What the learner can do at the end

1. Say which verbs are assimilated (first root letter و, rarely ي) and find the root of a present verb that shows only two letters (يَعِدُ is و ع د, not ع د د).
2. Say why the و falls in the Form I present (it stands between يَ and a kasra, al-Jadwal) and write all 13 present forms of يَجِدُ.
3. Tell the kasra verbs (يَجِدُ) from the fatḥa verbs (يَهَبُ, يَضَعُ), and know يَذَرُ has no past in the Quran.
4. Make the command (عِدْ, قِفْ, هَبْ) and know it takes no helping alif.
5. Read the double change of وَقَى (يَقِي, قِ, قِنَا, قُوا).
6. Say where the و stays: the past (13 forms of وَجَدَ), the passive (وُعِدَ, يُوعَدُ), Forms II to VI and X.
7. Name the two verbs that keep the و in the present (وَجِلَ, وَدَّ) and say that a ي at the start stays (يَيْـَٔسُ).
8. Read the other changes: the verbal noun without و (سِنَة, دِيَة, سَعَة, and the teaching forms عِدَة, صِلَة), Form VIII (و becomes ت: ٱتَّسَقَ, ٱتَّقَىٰ), and nouns (و becomes ي after a kasra: مِيثَاق).
9. Go through four verses word by word (17:64, 13:35, 2:201, 15:53).

### Sentence verses (roles shown)

## The lesson, step by step

5 steps, 27 blocks, 15 `Check`s, 11 `Exercise`s, 4 `Parse` panels (9, 6, 5 and 9 cards = 29), 13 `Sentence`s, 2 `Conjugation` tables, 14 `Tiles`.

**Step 1: The idea** (hero; 4 blocks)
1. `overview`: Formula "starting form يَوْعِدُ, the و falls, يَعِدُ"; Start (goal, 90 minutes, five terms).
2. `see`: 93:6 and 93:7 side by side; the two و in وَوَجَدَكَ. Check 1.
3. `name`: what an assimilated verb is; table of nine roots with Corpus counts; the count note. Check 2.
4. `drop`: يَوْصِلُ becomes يَصِلُ (4:90); al-Jadwal's reason (66:6, 6:145, 17:64, 2:268); sound next to assimilated table; the pattern يَعِلُ and عِلْ. Check 3.

**Step 2: The present and the command** (4 blocks)
5. `forms`: Conjugation of يَجِدُ, 13 forms; Corpus person counts. Check 4.
6. `vowel`: 42:49; table of 10 verbs, kasra and fatḥa, with Corpus counts; throat letter (2:247); يَذَرُ has no past (2:234). Check 5.
7. `command`: three moves from L3.08; 37:24; table of six commands; 44 Corpus commands. Check 6.
8. `qi`: وَقَى, يَقِي, قِ, قِنَا, قُوا (66:6). Check 7.

**Step 3: When it stays** (5 blocks)
9. `past`: 27:23; Conjugation of the past of وَجَدَ (13 forms); Corpus forms; the shadda in وَجَدتُّ. Check 8.
10. `other`: 6:145 (أَجِدُ and أُوحِىَ); table of Forms II, III, IV, V, VI, X (stays) and VIII (changes). Check 9.
11. `passive`: وَعَدَ, وُعِدَ, يَعِدُ, يُوعَدُ; 51:22; 49 passive words show the و. Check 10.
12. `keep`: وَجِلَ and وَدَّ; 2:96; the 244, 232, 12 count. Check 11.
13. `ya`: ي-initial verbs; 12:87; يَسَرَ and يَسَّرَ; يُوقِنُونَ. Check 12.

**Step 4: Other changes** (11 blocks)
14. `masdar`: verbal noun without و; 2:247. Check 13.
15. `viii`: و becomes ت; 84:18; 168 of 963. Check 14.
16. `ya-nouns`: و after kasra becomes ي (مِيثَاق, مِيعَاد, مِيزَان, مِيرَاث); مَوْعِد keeps it. Check 15.
17. to 20. `mistake-write`, `mistake-root`, `mistake-helper`, `mistake-all`.
21. to 24. `parse-1` to `parse-4`: 17:64, 13:35, 2:201, 15:53.

**Step 5: Practice** (3 blocks)
25. `practice-1`: exercises 1 to 6. 26. `practice-2`: exercises 7 to 11. 27. `done`: summary, next, links, Sources.

## Errors fixed from the old lesson

1. The old lesson wrote the helper of a command with a vowel mark: ٱُنْصُرْ, ٱِوْجِدْ and ٱِيْسِرْ. The course writes ٱ bare (L3.08: ٱنصُرْ), and the last two forms are not taught (error 2).
2. "Some grammars note the imperative can optionally keep the و (ٱِوْجِدْ)": no source, and not a form of وَجَدَ. Dropped.
3. The example root و-ف-ع: "past وَفَعَ, present يَفْعَلُ". يَفْعَلُ is the pattern of فَعَلَ itself, not the present of a verb with the root ف ع. Replaced by real roots.
4. "Forms II to X: the و stays." In Form VIII the و turns into ت (ٱتَّقَىٰ, ٱتَّسَقَ). Fixed (`other`, `viii`).
5. The quote of 4:173 ("وَلَا تَجِدُ لَهُمْ مِنْ دُونِ ٱللَّهِ وَلِيًّا وَلَا نَصِيرًا") is not the verse. The verse has وَلَا يَجِدُونَ لَهُم مِّن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ وَلِيًّا وَلَا نَصِيرًا. The lesson quotes يَجِدُونَ (4:173) as a fragment and uses other verses as examples.
6. The etymology of مِثَال (from م-ث-ل, "pattern فِعَال", "they resemble") has no source, and "the و assimilates into the next consonant" describes a drop as an assimilation. Dropped.
7. The tables gave 10 persons for the present and 13 for the past engine. The present has 13 (the table left out you two, you all (men), you all (women)). Fixed.
8. "Three parallel structures using و-ج-د", then two were listed. Fixed: the lesson shows the two verbs of 93:6 and 93:7, and exercise 4 says the root comes back in 93:8.
9. "The deletion happens in all persons... no exceptions" and "No subtypes to distinguish": there are keepers (وَجِلَ, وَدَّ), a kasra and a fatḥa group, and the ي-initial verbs. Fixed (`vowel`, `keep`, `ya`, mistake 4).
10. "Only Form I present is affected, everything else conjugates like sound verbs": the command, some verbal nouns (عِدَة), Form VIII (و becomes ت) and nouns (مِيثَاق) change too. Fixed.
11. "Assimilated verbs are called the easy weak verbs by Arabic grammarians": no source. Dropped.
12. The link "L5.01 Advanced Morphology" pointed to `/learn/level-5/16-sarf-synthesis/` (that is L5.16). Fixed.
13. The old lesson had no `titleArabic`, `kicker` or `template: steps`. Added.
