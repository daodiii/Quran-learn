## What the learner can do at the end

1. Take any verse apart in five moves, in order: cut, kind, jobs, endings, place.
2. Cut a verse into words and into pieces (joined pronouns, prefixes).
3. Say whether a sentence is verbal or nominal by its first word after the little words, and handle the two traps: a phrase first (11:4) and inna first (97:1).
4. Name each word's job, case, mark and reason, including hidden marks and pronouns that count by place.
5. Say what a preposition phrase is tied to, including a left-out word.
6. Say the place of a sentence (none, or the place of the word it stands for), as al-Jadwal reads it.
7. Say where two books give two readings, give the main one, name the other and keep each whole (the Bismillah, ٱلْأَعْلَى in 87:1).
8. Use shape (root, form) and style only where a source supports them.

### 87:1 سَبِّحِ ٱسْمَ رَبِّكَ ٱلْأَعْلَى

| Piece | Role | Case and mark | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| سَبِّحِ | command, Form II, root س ب ح; hidden doer "you" | built on a sukūn, read with a kasra | Standard (L3.08; kasra from L3.12 Ex 5). Corpus: IMPV (II) 2MS |
| ٱسْمَ | object | accusative, -a | al-Jadwal: مفعول به منصوب |
| رَبِّ | second word of the idafah after ٱسْمَ; first word of رَبِّكَ | genitive, -i | Standard; al-Jadwal silent on this word |
| كَ | second word of the idafah | genitive by place | Standard |
| ٱلْأَعْلَى | adjective of رَبِّكَ | genitive, hidden kasra (alif maqṣūra) | al-Jadwal: نعت لربك مجرور وعلامة الجر الكسرة المقدرة. Corpus: ADJ, root ع ل و |
| whole verse | opening sentence | no place | al-Jadwal: لا محل لها ابتدائية |

Al-Jadwal footnote 1: some commentators made ٱسْمَ an extra word; no need. Al-Durr al-Maṣūn: ٱلْأَعْلَى may be genitive (adjective of رَبِّكَ) or accusative (adjective of ٱسْمَ); the second makes ٱلَّذِى (87:2) unable to describe رَبِّكَ. **Agrees with al-Jadwal at sentence level and on ٱسْمَ and ٱلْأَعْلَى; al-Durr gives a second reading of ٱلْأَعْلَى** (named in `hidden`).
