# Content spec: L2.08 The Possessive Construction (Idafah)

Written 2026-10-27. The lesson is in `src/content/lessons/level-2/08-possessive-idafah.mdx`. It follows the L2.01 spec. Built on L2.06 (idafah as a cause of the genitive) and L2.07 (prepositions).

## What the learner can do at the end

1. Spot an idafah and name the muḍāf (first word) and the muḍāf ilayh (second word).
2. Say what the first word loses (al-, tanwīn) and that the idafah takes its definiteness from the second word.
3. Say that the second word is always genitive, and read its sign (-i, -in, -īna, yāʾ, nothing on a pronoun).
4. Find the case of the first word from its job in the sentence (nominative, accusative, genitive).
5. Read an idafah with a joined pronoun (رَبُّهُمْ, رَبِّي) and a chain of two or three links.
6. Explain why the nūn of a dual or sound plural drops as a first word.
7. Name al-Ajurrumiyyah's two kinds (with lām, with min).
8. Tell an idafah from a noun with its adjective.

## Errors fixed from the old lesson

1. **"NEVER has ال" / "if the first word has ال it is NOT idafah".** Too strong: 22:35 has ٱلْمُقِيمِى ٱلصَّلَوٰةِ, al-Jadwal reads ٱلصَّلَوٰةِ as muḍāf ilayh. The lesson keeps the rule for ordinary nouns and names the exception without teaching it.
2. **The nūn.** The old Rule 3 listed the dual and plural genitive as "yāʾ + nūn" and never said the nūn drops in an idafah. Now taught (`dual-nun`, `plural-nun`).
3. **Exercise 4 text.** It named Al-Baqarah for a phrase it then said was from Quraysh, and its رَبُّ ٱلْبَيْتِ ٱلْحَرَامِ is not a Quran phrase and was not labelled made-up. Replaced.
4. **Spelling هَٰذَاْ** in 106:3 (stray alif sukūn). Tanzil has هَٰذَا. *Checked.*
5. **Wrong link:** "L3.06 Complex Idafah Structures" pointed to this lesson. Removed.
6. **Unlabelled made-up sentences** (كِتَابُ ٱلطَّالِبِ and others). Now "Made-up example".
7. **"Absolute, no exceptions" for the second word.** Kept as "always genitive", but the sign is now stated for every kind of word, including the pronoun that shows nothing.
