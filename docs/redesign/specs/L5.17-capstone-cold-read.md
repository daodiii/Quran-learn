# Content spec: L5.17 Capstone, cold read of Luqman's counsel (31:12 to 31:19)

Written 2026-10-28. Lesson file: `src/content/lessons/level-5/17-capstone-cold-read.mdx`. It replaces the old lesson of the same name (file name, `level` and `order` kept; `title` kept except that its em dash became a comma; `kicker`, `titleArabic`, `template: steps` and a new `description` added). Shape decided by the orchestrator (`LEVEL-5-NOTES.md`): for each verse a block whose Stage holds only the verse as a plain `blockquote.lx-quote` with the task in the text, an `Exercise` whose answer is the model analysis, and a later block "Check your work" with a `Parse`. Sentence places follow al-Jadwal (Maḥmūd Ṣāfī), read with `jadwal.mjs` for every verse (four pages cover the eight verses). Roots, forms and moods are checked in the Corpus tags (`verse.py -t`). Every claim about an earlier topic follows the finished lesson named beside it.

## What the learner can do at the end

1. Take a verse of the passage apart on their own with the five moves of L5.01, before looking at any answer, and compare with a model.
2. Read a "when" clause with a left-out verb, a speech that starts with a call, a sentence put in between two parts of one speech, a condition with two answers, and an `inna` sentence that gives a reason.
3. Tell the eight jussive verbs of the passage apart by their cause (a ban, a condition word, a condition joined by فَ, an answer), and tell them from the raised verb يَشْكُرُ, the subjunctive تُشْرِكَ and the commands.
4. Name the root, kind and form of all ten commands (sound, hollow, defective, hamzated, doubled; Forms I, III, IV, VIII).
5. Read the dual (بِوَٰلِدَيْهِ, جَٰهَدَاكَ, عَامَيْنِ), a fronted khabar (إِلَىَّ ٱلْمَصِيرُ) and an elative (أَنكَرَ).
6. Say what the books give as two readings (مَرَحًا, وَهْنًا, مَعْرُوفًا) and what al-Jadwal names as style (turning the surface round, completing, an open metaphor).
7. Say honestly which parts they did alone, with a hint, or only with the model.
