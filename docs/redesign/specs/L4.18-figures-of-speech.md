# Content spec: L4.18 Figures of speech

Written 2026-10-28. This is the old L4.18 "Figures of Speech in the Quran", kept at the same place. It follows the definition of a complete lesson (end of `docs/redesign/CONTENT-INVENTORY.md`). The old lesson is not in the error inventory; the inventory and the error list below are new. The lesson is `src/content/lessons/level-4/18-figures-of-speech.mdx`. It was written at the same time as L4.17 (the map of the three branches) and L4.19 (Ad-Duha), by other writers.

No approved book on balagha exists in this project. The rule used throughout: **a figure is named in a verse only where al-Jadwal's balagha paragraph (or its i'rab) for that verse names it**, and the lesson says "al-Jadwal says". A reason why a verse chose a form is taught only where al-Jadwal gives it; where it gives none the lesson says so (35:28 was listed here as giving none; that was wrong, see the correction at the end). The short definition of each figure is a teaching summary, listed under "What is not checked".

## What the learner can do at the end

1. Find the four parts of a simile (thing compared, thing compared with, little word, point) in 2:74, and name the kinds al-Jadwal names: mursal, mujmal, mufaṣṣal, balīgh, tamthīlī.
2. Tell a simile from a metaphor with al-Jadwal's test (a metaphor leaves the thing described out), and tell the open metaphor (36:37) from the hidden one (17:24).
3. Recognise kināya (18:42, 3:119) and majāz mursal (12:82), and keep them apart from metaphor and ellipsis.
4. Find what is left out (a mubtadaʾ in 12:18, an object in 2:216, the khabar of لَا in 2:255, the answer of "when" in 39:73) and not count English-only gaps.
5. See a khabar put first (64:1) and an object put first (35:28), and say what al-Jadwal says about each, or that it says nothing.
6. Tell a question of confirmation (94:1, 105:1) from a question of denial (88:17).
7. Find ṭibāq (18:18, 2:216), muqābala (9:82, 3:26), jinās (6:26, 18:104), sajʿ (26:78 to 26:81) and tawāzī (2:216).
8. Write a figure with its verse and its source ("al-Jadwal"), and avoid saying what a figure "achieves" without a source.
9. Go through four verses word by word (2:74, 17:24, 12:18, 35:28).

## The lesson, step by step

5 steps, 33 blocks, 28 `Check`s, 10 `Exercise`s, 4 `Parse` panels (7, 7, 6 and 5 cards, 25 in all).
