# L1.05 Roots and Patterns: A First Look (spec)

Lesson file: `src/content/lessons/level-1/05-root-system-preview.mdx`. Level 1, order 5. Written for the stepped layout (5 steps, 24 blocks). A preview: L3.01 (`/learn/level-3/01-root-system/`) teaches roots and patterns in full.

## What the learner can do

1. Say that many Arabic words are a root (usually three letters) plus a pattern.
2. Find the root of a Quran word by stripping the article, prefixes, pronoun ending and pattern letters.
3. Name the family of ك ت ب, ع ل م and ر ح م.
4. Read a pattern name (فَاعِل, مَفْعُول, فِعَال, فَعْلَان, فَعِيل, فَعَّال, فِعْل) when al-Jadwal gives it.
5. Know the limits: little words and pronouns have no root, a root can hide a letter, similar words can have different roots.

## Errors fixed from the old lesson

1. 112:1: أَحَدٌ was given the root و ح د. The Corpus gives أ ح د. Fixed and made a mistake note.
2. "500 roots cover about 75% of the text": the Corpus count is 59.2% of all words (91.7% of words that have a root). The 200-root figure (about 50%) is 48.9%. "About 1,700 roots": 1,642. "Over 300 times" for ر ح م: 339 words (kept, now counted). "10,000 roots in Arabic": no source, dropped.
3. L3.01 link was `/learn/level-3/01-root-extraction/`, which does not exist; now `/learn/level-3/01-root-system/`.
4. 58:21 and 2:2 Arabic was typed with plain ي and ا instead of the Tanzil forms; all verse text is now copied from the Corpus.
5. "ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ means overwhelming mercy, ٱلرَّحِيمِ constant mercy": no source. Replaced by what al-Jadwal says (intensive forms; ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ the stronger).
6. "اسم comes from a root about elevation": al-Jadwal gives the root س م و and the dropped letter, not that meaning. Dropped.
7. "أَرْحَام: wombs because the womb is a place of mercy": no source. Dropped; the Corpus root is kept.
8. أَحْمَد and مُحَمَّد given a root: the Corpus gives none.
9. "The prefix مُ often indicates one who does": a pattern claim with no source in the verse. Dropped (مُعَلِّمٌ is not in the Quran).
10. Examples ٱلْكِتَاب, عَلِيم, تَعَلَّمَ were shown as pure patterns although al-Jadwal gives no pattern for them. The lesson now claims a pattern only where al-Jadwal does.
