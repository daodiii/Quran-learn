# L1.06 The Definite Article (Al-): content spec

Lesson file: `src/content/lessons/level-1/06-definite-article.mdx`. Written 2026-10-28.

## What the learner can do

Say ٱلْ correctly before any letter (sun and moon), read the clue in the marks (sukun on the lam, or shadda on the next letter), explain the joining alif ٱ, write and read ٱلْ after لِ, بِ and كَ (including لِلَّهِ), and tell a definite noun from an indefinite one (tanwin) in a verse.

## Errors fixed from the old lesson

- The article was written أَلْ with a hamza. The Quran text writes ٱلْ with the joining alif (hamzat al-waṣl).
- Both mnemonics were wrong: the moon one has 13 initials (no ع) and two G's; the sun one has 13 initials (no ظ). Dropped.
- The Al-Fatiha count (8 sun, 4 moon) counted ٱلَّذِينَ and missed 1:1 ٱللَّهِ; the Corpus splits off 7 sun and 4 moon article words in the surah. The new lesson no longer counts Al-Fatiha, and says ٱلَّذِينَ and ٱللَّهِ are single words in the Corpus.
- Table gave ٱلشَّمْسُ with the reference Ash-Shams 91:1, but the verse has ٱلشَّمْسِ. New tables use forms taken from verses.
- "Transliteration always writes al-" contradicted the next sentence. Now says: write the sound you hear.
- "Moon letters don't get shadda" is not a rule (a moon letter can carry a shadda for another reason). Now says only that the lam has a sukun.
- Wrong claim that the shadda is the only clue; the unmarked lam is the second clue.
