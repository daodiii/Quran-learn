# Content spec: L3.15 Verb Form V (تَفَعَّلَ)

Written 2026-10-28. Lesson file: `src/content/lessons/level-3/15-verb-form-v.mdx`. Every parse follows al-Jadwal (Maḥmūd Ṣāfī). Counts come from the Corpus file with a script written for this lesson (form tag (V), tense tag, lemma, root).

## What the learner can do at the end

1. Recognise a Form V verb: تَ in front, middle root letter doubled; the Corpus tag (V).
2. Read it in the past, the present and the command, with a real verb (تَوَكَّلَ) and real verses.
3. Know the fatḥa on the present prefix (Forms II and IV take a ḍamma).
4. Read the present when one of its two tāʾs is dropped (تَنَزَّلُ, تَكَلَّمُ) or merged (يَذَّكَّرُ, يَطَّوَّفَ).
5. See Form II / Form V pairs in verses, with no "always".
6. Preview the participle مُتَفَعِّل and the verbal noun تَفَعُّل (L3.19, L3.20); recognise weak Form V verbs (L4).
7. Go through four verses word by word.

## Errors fixed from the old lesson

1. 96:19 was shown as the example of تَوَلَّىٰ. It has no Form V verb.
2. The heading "Example 4: تَقَدَّمَ" showed 11:105 (تَكَلَّمُ).
3. It called تَكَلَّمُ (11:105) jussive. It is indicative (al-Jadwal: مضارع مرفوع).
4. It called تَنَزَّلُ (97:4) "third person plural". The Corpus tags it 3FS.
5. It said Form V present "always" has two ت letters, and that a ت in the past "is definitely Form V". One ت drops (97:4) or merges (3:7), and تَابَ (2:37) is Form I.
6. Made-up verbs (تَكَسَّرَ, تَمَرَّضَ, تَوَضَّأَ and others) were shown without a label as if from the Quran.
7. "تَعَلَّمَ appears throughout the Quran": the Corpus has the root ع ل م in Form V twice (2:102).
8. "Gradual" and "pretending" meanings, and "Form II speaks to, Form V self-initiated", were unsourced.
9. The chapter header "Al-Muzzammil" sat over a verse from Al-Qadr.
