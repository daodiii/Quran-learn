# Content spec: L3.07 Subjunctive and Jussive

Written 2026-10-28. This says what the rewritten L3.07 teaches, with which verses, and how each verse was checked. It follows the definition of a complete lesson at the end of `docs/redesign/CONTENT-INVENTORY.md`. The lesson is `src/content/lessons/level-3/07-subjunctive-jussive.mdx` (old file on `main`: `05-subjunctive-jussive.mdx`).

## What the learner can do at the end

1. Say which of three endings a present-tense verb has (plain, naṣb, jazm) and name the little word in front that caused it.
2. Know the five words of naṣb taught here (أَنْ, لَن, كَيْ, لِ "so that", حَتَّىٰ) and the words of jazm (لَمْ, لَا of a ban, the lām of command), with the conditional words as a preview (L4.03, L4.04).
3. Read the sign of each mood on each kind of verb: -a or sukūn on one person; the lost nūn on the five verbs; no change on the women's plural; the hidden -a on a verb ending in a long ā; the dropped weak letter in jazm.
4. Tell لَمْ (past) from لَن (future), the lām of reason from the lām of command, and a ban from a statement with لَا, by the end of the verb and the word in front.
5. Go through four verses word by word.

## Errors fixed from the old lesson

1. **Counts.** The old lesson said لَمْ occurs "over 1,100 times", لَنْ "130+" and "~80", لِ "200+", حَتَّى "~100". None had a source. Counted by script on the Corpus file: لَمْ is tagged 353 times, لَن 106 times; of 8,330 present-tense verbs, 1,330 carry MOOD:SUBJ and 1,418 MOOD:JUS (`grep -c` on `quranic-corpus-morphology-0.4.txt`: `POS:NEG|LEM:lam`, `POS:NEG|LEM:lan`, `MOOD:SUBJ`, `MOOD:JUS`, `IMPF`). The lesson says these are Corpus tags, not checked verse by verse. The other counts are dropped.
2. **106:3.** The old lesson used لِيَعْبُدُوا۟ as a verse (106:3) and said "both readings exist in recitation traditions". The Tanzil text of 106:3 is فَلْيَعْبُدُوا۟, the lām of command. The "same verse, two readings" table is removed. 14:1 teaches the lām of reason and 65:7 the lām of command.
3. **"Prohibitive لَا addresses you; negating لَا describes he/she/they."** False. In 49:11 (لَا يَسْخَرْ) a ban is about "a people", and a statement can speak to "you" (3:153 تَلْوُۥنَ). The test is the verb's ending. The lesson says so.
4. **"Every single time you encounter لَمْ ... MUST be in jussive."** Absolute wording removed. The claim is true of the verb right after لَمْ, but the text used it to inflate the count.
5. **"All subjunctive particles point forward / mood of the not yet."** Too broad (أَنْ can follow past-tense contexts). Dropped.
6. **"لَنْ is emphatic future negation."** Al-Jadwal says negation, naṣb and the future (17:37). "Emphatic" is not in the allowed sources. Removed.
7. **"This happens to all hollow verbs" (shortening in jussive).** It happens when a sukūn follows the middle letter. With -ū or -ī the long vowel stays. The lesson says so and sends to L4.12.
8. **حَتَّى and لِ as plain naṣb words.** Al-Jadwal reads both as followed by a hidden أَنْ. The lesson gives the book's list and al-Jadwal's reading in a Note.
9. **Literal meanings** ("raised", "set up", "cut off") for the three names. Not in an allowed source. Removed.
10. **Old numbers.** Links to L3.04, L3.06 (imperative), L3.08 (subject pronouns) were old numbers. Now L3.06 present tense, L3.08 imperative, L3.09 passive, L3.12, L3.13, L3.15.
