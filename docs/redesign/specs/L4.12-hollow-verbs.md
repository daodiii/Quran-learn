## What the learner can do at the end

1. Say what a hollow verb is (middle root letter و or ي) and find its root from the long vowel (قَالَ: take out the ā, ask the present: يَقُولُ gives ق و ل).
2. Tell the three kinds by the present: ū (يَقُولُ), ī (يَزِيدُ), ā (يَخَافُ).
3. Say when the long vowel stays (the last root letter has a vowel: قَالَ, قَالَتْ, قَالُوا) and when it is cut (the last root letter has a sukūn: قُلْتُ, قُلْنَا, قُلْنَ).
4. Pick the short vowel: ḍamma for ū, kasra for ī and ā (past); fatḥa in Forms IV, VIII, X.
5. Build the past (13 forms), the present (13 forms), jazm and the command of قَالَ, and apply them to other hollow verbs.
6. Keep the long vowel before -ū, -ā, -ī in jazm and the command (لَمْ يَسِيرُوا, قُولُوا, قُولِي).
7. Know that كَانَ can lose its nūn in jazm (لَمْ تَكُ) and that كُنْ is a complete verb.
8. Read the passive قِيلَ / يُقَالُ and the participles قَائِل (hamza) and مَلُوم (one و drops).
9. Go through four verses word by word (20:68, 36:82, 2:13, 2:229).

## The lesson, step by step

5 steps, 31 blocks (4, 7, 6, 11, 3).

**Step 1: Spot the verb** (hero; 4 blocks)
1. `overview`: Formula (stem + ending = cut stem), Start with 7 terms.
2. `see`: 71:10 (قُلْتُ against كَانَ). Check 1.
3. `root`: finding the root, definition of hollow, sound against hollow table, counts note. Check 2.
4. `kinds`: three kinds by the present, table of common verbs with Corpus counts, counts of present words by kind. Check 3.

**Step 2: The past tense** (7 blocks)
5. `past-long`: Conjugation (5 forms), 28:23. Check 4.
6. `past-short`: Conjugation (8 forms), 18:14 (qulnā). Check 5.
7. `why`: Formula, 2:55, al-Jadwal's notes, teaching-summary note. Check 6.
8. `vowel-endings`: the test (last root letter), 18:14 (qāmū, qālū), Tiles. Check 7.
9. `which-vowel`: ḍamma or kasra, table, four-verbs table, 482/479 count, مَاتَ note. Check 8.
10. `other-forms`: Forms IV, VIII, X (fatḥa), 18:81, table, 64/64 count, present of these forms, shadda note. Check 9.
11. `hamza`: جَآءَ, شَآءَ (counts), L4.15 pointer. Check 10.

**Step 3: Present, jussive, command** (6 blocks)
12. `present`: Conjugation (13 rows, two marked), 61:2, 35:1. Check 11.

## Errors fixed from the old lesson

1. "Hollow-waw verbs have damma on the prefix (يَقُولُ)": the prefix has a fatḥa (يَـ). The long ū is on the middle letter (kinds).
2. Principle 3 said the و drops because "Arabic does not allow و or ي with sukūn in this position": a و with a sukūn is fine in قَوْل. The reason is two silent sounds in a row, the long vowel and the sukūn (`why`, with al-Jadwal's notes).
3. The command was derived from "ٱُقْوُلْ (uqwul)", a form that does not exist. The command comes from the jazm form with the prefix taken away (`command`).
4. "In the imperative and jussive the middle weak letter drops": it stays before -ū, -ā and -ī (قُولُوا, قُولَا, قُولِي, لَمْ يَسِيرُوا). 65 of the 200 jazm words and 72 of the 425 commands of Form I hollow verbs keep the long vowel (`jussive-long`, `command`).
5. The past table said "و drops (before sukūn)" for قُلْتُ: the و had already become the long ā. What is cut is the long vowel (`why`).
6. Only two kinds (waw, ya) were given, and only a ḍamma for the short past. The ā kind (خَافَ, يَخَافُ, خِفْتُ) and the kasra were missing; 70 of the 482 short past words have a kasra (`kinds`, `which-vowel`).
7. Example 2 used 2:183, which has the verbal noun ٱلصِّيَامُ, as an example of how a verb changes, with "Form I hollow-waw" and a past, present and imperative. A noun has none of these. The Quran has no past صَامَ and no command صُمْ; it has تَصُومُوا۟ (2:184) and فَلْيَصُمْهُ (2:185) (`kinds`, exercise 2).
8. The table gave نَارَ, يَنُورُ, نُرْ, "flee": the Corpus has no verb from the root ن و ر. The row is dropped.
9. The sound command was written "ٱُكْتُبْ" (alif with a ḍamma on it). It is written ٱكْتُبْ (`root` table).
10. كَانَ was called "a hollow verb AND a defective verb (modifies nominal sentences)". نَاقِص has two meanings: "incomplete" for كَانَ and its sisters (L2.11), "defective" for a weak last letter (L4.13). كَانَ is hollow, and نَاقِص only in the first meaning (`kana-nun` note).
11. The table gave the present يَجِيءُ and the command جِئْ for جَاءَ as Quran forms: the Corpus has 278 words of this verb and all are past (2 of them passive). The verb is hollow and hamzated (L4.15) and moved to `hamza`.

## Counts and how they were made

Counted with a script over `src/data/morphology/quranic-corpus-morphology-0.4.txt` (verb parts: tag V with STEM; Form I = no `(II)`..`(X)` tag; root from `ROOT:`; person, mood and passive from the tags). Hollow = the Corpus root has three letters, the middle one و or ي. These are Quran Learn's counts; Corpus tags are not reliable for every word.

- 5,162 hollow verb words of 19,356 (26.7%), all forms, simple rule (the L3.05 and L4.11 figure). 4,214 are Form I. Leaving out لَيْسَ (89) and roots with a weak last letter too (32) gives **4,093** Form I words (used for the rest).
- قَالَ 1,618 and كَانَ 1,358 words: 2,976 of 4,093 (72.7%).
- Present words (Form I, active): 992. With a long ū 553, ā 235, ī 66 (854 in all), cut short 138. Found by looking for `uw`, `aA`, `iy` in the Buckwalter stem.
- Past (Form I, active): 2,595. Short-stem persons (I, we, you in all forms, they (women)): 482. Short vowel read from the Buckwalter stem: 412 ḍamma, 70 kasra. 479 follow the table; 3 do not (مِتُّ ×2, مِّتَّ ×1, from مَاتَ). مِتْنَا is tagged passive in the Corpus and is left out; مُتُّمْ ×2 follows the table.
- Short stems in Forms IV, VIII, X: 64 (45, 5, 14), all with a fatḥa.
- Jazm (Form I): 200; cut short 135 (he, she, you (man), I, we, they (women)); long 65 (ū-, ā-, ī-endings). كَانَ in jazm without the nūn: 18 (يَكُ 8, تَكُ 7, نَكُ 2, أَكُ 1).
- Commands (Form I): 425. One man 352 (332 are قُلْ, matching L3.08), you all 67, the two 3, one woman 2, women 1. Short 353, long 72.
- Present for women (Form I): two words, يَكُنَّ (49:11) and يَحِضْنَ (65:4).
- Persons in the Corpus for قَالَ in the past: he 530, she 43, the two (men) 3, the two (women) 2, they (men) 332, they (women) 2, you (man) 4, you all (men) 9, I 3, we 27. No you (woman), you two, you all (women).
- Active participles (Form I, hollow): 91; 79 with a hamza in the lemma; 12 are تَوَّاب.
- Passive of قَالَ: 52 (49 past, 3 present), the count of L3.09.
- جَآءَ 278 words, all past (276 active, 2 passive). شَآءَ 236.
- Per-root counts in the "common verbs" table: Form I words with that root (قَالَ 1,618, كَانَ 1,358, خَافَ 83, زَادَ 49, قَامَ 33, عَادَ 18, سَارَ 16, نَالَ 11, صَامَ 2, طَارَ 1, بَاعَ 0).
