## What the learner can do at the end

1. Name eight words that say "not" (لَمْ, لَمَّا, لَن, لَا, مَا, إِنْ, لَيْسَ, غَيْر) and say for each what it does to the word after it: jazm, naṣb, nothing, an accusative khabar, or (غَيْر) that it is a noun.
2. Tell a plain لَا ("does not") from the لَا of a ban ("do not") by the ending of the verb, and both from the lā that denies the kind (L4.05) by the ending of the noun.
3. Say what time each word points to: لَمْ the past, لَمَّا "up to now", لَن the future, a ban "from now on"; مَا and the plain لَا take the time from the verb and the sentence.
4. Know that مَا negates past and present verbs and can work like laysa before a noun, and that the negative إِنْ usually comes with إِلَّا.
5. Read the 13 forms of لَيْسَ and its khabar with an extra بِ.
6. Choose the word for a made-up sentence and mend wrong ones.
7. Go through four verses word by word and use the Corpus counts (and know why the tags are not clean).

## The lesson, step by step

5 steps, 31 blocks, 18 `Check`s, 9 `Note`s.

**Step 1: Eight words** (hero; 4 blocks)
1. `overview`: Start, formula "a word of negation changes what comes after it".
2. `see`: 63:6 (part): لَمْ, لَن, لَا in one verse. Check 1.
3. `map`: Tiles of the eight words; the table of what each does (jazm, naṣb, nothing, accusative khabar, a noun). Check 2.
4. `books`: al-Ajurrumiyyah's two lists (copied from L3.07); the ban "and the request" (2:286). Check 3.

**Step 2: Before a verb** (7 blocks)
5. `la-say`: 20:52; 1,406 tags; the rare past verb after لَا (note). Check 4.
6. `la-ban`: 17:23 (part); 332 PRO tags, the 110 NEG tags before jussive-tagged verbs; note on أَلَّا. Check 5.
7. `lam`: 105:1; 347 + 6 tags; 125 with the question أَ; note on lam in a condition (2:24). Check 6.
8. `lan`: 12:80 (part); 106 tags; note on "never" with the 10 verses with حَتَّىٰ and 2:95. Check 7.
9. `lamma`: 62:3 (part); the eight verses of L4.04; teaching summary "still expected". Check 8.
10. `ma`: 53:2 and 36:69 (part); 705 tags and their split; warn note on the three jobs of مَا. Check 9.
11. `same-verb`: nine negations of عَبَدَ (Tiles, table). Check 10.

**Step 3: Before a noun** (6 blocks)
12. `laysa`: 95:8; the extra بِ; 89 tags; know note (verb or particle). Check 11.
13. `laysa-forms`: `Conjugation` of لَيْسَ, 13 forms; the 7 forms in the Quran. Check 12.
14. `ma-laysa`: 58:2 (part) and 41:46 (end); the pair أُمَّهَٰتِهِمْ / أُمَّهَٰتُهُمْ; know note on 7:184. Check 13.
15. `in`: 36:69 (end) and 6:116 (end); 114 tags, 106 with إِلَّا; know note (two kinds). Check 14.
16. `la-noun`: 2:256 (part) and 2:38 (part); the pronoun case (109:3). Check 15.
17. `ghayr`: 84:25 (part) and 11:108 (end); 147 tags. Check 16.

**Step 4: Side by side** (7 blocks)
18. `table`: Tiles; the big table with counts; how the counts were made. Check 17.
19. `choose`: four questions; the table "after the word you see"; tip on مَا. Check 18.
20. `mistake-form`: lam + past verb, mā + jazm, lan + plain -u (made-up).
21. `mistake-time`: who sets the time (real verbs).
22. `mistake-claims`: four claims that are too strong.

## Errors fixed from the old lesson

The old lesson is not in `CONTENT-INVENTORY.md`. These 22 were found while inventorying it (the old text is `git show main:src/content/lessons/level-4/10-negation-particles.mdx`).

1. "Five negation particles", "five dominate Quranic usage": the lesson now compares eight words and counts them (adds لَمَّا, إِنْ, غَيْر and the ban).
2. "In L3.06 Present Tense & Moods you learned the three moods": the moods are L3.07 (L3.06 is the present tense).
3. "لَمْ is the ONLY particle with present form, past meaning, jazm" (two callouts, the summary, the rule, the mnemonic): false. لَمَّا does the same; the ban and the lām of command also give jazm.
4. "مَا: past negation" (title, table, rule, tree, summary): false. 141 of 705 negative مَا stand before a present verb and 207 before a noun; it can work like laysa. *Checked* in the Corpus.
5. "لَمْ is formal, emphatic; مَا is casual; lam very common, mā less common": unsupported; the Corpus has 705 مَا and 347 لَمْ.
6. "Why indicative? Because the speaker states a fact, not a possibility or a command": wrong reason. The verb keeps its plain ending because nothing acts on it.
7. لَا described only as present or future with the plain ending: the ban (jazm), the lā of the kind and لَا before a noun were missing. L4.19 relies on the ban.
8. "لَنْ: emphatic, 'will absolutely never', stronger than لَا": one view; 10 of the 106 verses with لَن have حَتَّىٰ setting an end.
9. The decision tree: "nominal sentence → laysa" (مَا, لَا and إِنْ also negate nominal sentences); "past: formal → لَمْ, simple → مَا"; no ban, لَمَّا, إِنْ or غَيْر.
10. "laysa negates states of being, not actions; present (nominal)": overstated. laysa has no tense of its own (L2.11) and its khabar can be a phrase.
11. 2:177 typed "لَيْسَ" where the Tanzil text has "لَّيْسَ". *Checked.*
12. 109:4-5 typed "عَابِدٌۭ" with an extra mark; the Tanzil text has "عَابِدٌ". *Checked.*
13. The laysa table lists a first-person "dual" (لَسْنَا) as its own entry; Arabic has no first-person dual. Rebuilt as the 13 forms.
14. Exercise 3.3: "يَخْلُقِ has a kasra because the final letter is ق": the kasra is the sukūn moving before the silent alif of ٱللَّهُ. The example "Allah did not create the heavens in vain" is 3:191, which uses مَا خَلَقْتَ, so "formal means لَمْ" fails on the Quran's own sentence.
15. Exercise 4.1: لَمْ يُؤْمِنُوا quoted as 2:6; 2:6 has لَا يُؤْمِنُونَ (and أَمْ لَمْ تُنذِرْهُمْ). *Checked.*
16. Exercise 4.2: "لَا يَسْتَوِي" quoted as 35:19; the verse has وَمَا يَسْتَوِى. *Checked.*
17. Exercise 4.3: the root of تَنَالُوا given as ن و ل; the Corpus gives ن ي ل. *Checked.*
18. 112:3: "absolutely never occurred and never will", "perfect-aspect meaning of لَمْ captures this": unsupported. لَمْ points to the past; the wider sense comes from the verse. *Needs a source* for any other claim.
19. 109:2 to 109:5: verse 4 read as "past action", verse 5 as "future state", "why لَا throughout: timeless": an interpretation, not grammar, and given as fact. Dropped.
