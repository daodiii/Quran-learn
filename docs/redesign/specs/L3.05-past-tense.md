# Content spec: L3.05 The Past Tense

Written 2026-10-28. This is the old L3.03 "Past Tense Conjugation (al-Madi)", moved to L3.05 by the new Level 3 order. It follows the definition of a complete lesson (end of `docs/redesign/CONTENT-INVENTORY.md`). The lesson is `src/content/lessons/level-3/05-past-tense.mdx`.

## What the learner can do at the end

1. Read the ending of a past verb and say who did the action (13 forms, five groups of endings).
2. Build all 13 forms of a real Form I verb (كَتَبَ) and put the sukūn on the **last** root letter (كَتَبْتُ).
3. Say that the -at ending is an ordinary tāʾ (tāʾ al-taʾnīth), not a tāʾ marbūṭa, and tell -na (they, women) from -nā (we).
4. Explain the shadda in ءَامَنَّا (the last root letter ن meets the ending -nā).
5. Recognise hollow and defective verbs (قَالَ, كَانَ, دَعَا, رَمَىٰ) as verbs that take the usual endings with a changed stem, and know that L4.11 to L4.13 teach the rules.
6. Write the iʿrāb line of a past verb: فعل ماض مبني على الفتح / الضم / السكون, with the doer.
7. Go through four verses word by word.

## The verb for the table

كَتَبَ (kataba, "he wrote"), root ك ت ب. It is a Form I verb in the Corpus (`LEM:kataba`, no `VF`). Not all 13 forms of it occur in the Quran (7:145 has كَتَبْنَا); the lesson says so and shows real forms of other verbs in verses. The old lesson used the same verb.

## The lesson, step by step

5 steps, 27 blocks.

**Step 1: One verb, many endings** (hero; 4 blocks)
1. `overview`: Start, formula "stem plus ending equals a past verb".
2. `see`: 96:1 (end), "he created", one word. Check 1.
3. `name`: al-Ajurrumiyyah (the three kinds of verb; the past verb is "open at the end"); fiʿl māḍin. Check 2.
4. `doer`: 7:145 (start), the ending -nā; the root stays, the ending changes; pronouns are L3.01. Check 3.

**Step 2: All 13 forms** (8 blocks)
5. `third`: Conjugation table, third person (6 forms). Check 4.
6. `second-first`: Conjugation table, you, I and we (7 forms); the count 6 + 5 + 2 = 13; Corpus counts (note).
7. `sukun`: Tiles of كَتَبْتُ, sukūn on the last root letter. Check 5.
8. `ta`: -at is the tāʾ of "she", not the tāʾ marbūṭa; made-up جَآءَ / جَآءَتِ. Check 6.
9. `u-aa`: 20:121 (part): -ā, -at; -ū and the silent alif (2:6). Check 7.
10. `na`: 2:234 (part): -na; كَتَبْنَ / كَتَبْنَا. Check 8.
11. `shadda`: 3:53 (start): the shadda in ءَامَنَّا. Check 9.
12. `groups`: the five groups of endings (table).

**Step 3: Other verbs** (3 blocks)
13. `same`: the same endings on other verbs (three Form I vowel patterns; Form IV; the 108:1 promise). Check 10.
14. `t-verses`: 26:19, 2:54, 28:16: -ta, -tum, -tu in verses.
15. `weak` (required, "Verbs that change shape"): hollow (قَالَ, كَانَ; 26.7%) and defective (دَعَا, رَمَىٰ; 18.0%), table of he / I / they, 5:116 (two parts), 17:3, 8:17. Recognition only, send to L4.11 to L4.13. Check 11.

**Step 4: I'rab and mistakes** (9 blocks)
16. `irab-line`: past verbs are built (mabnī): الفتح / الضم / السكون; the doer line. Check 12.
17. `mistake-sukun`: a vowel before -tu (كَتَبَتُ).
18. `mistake-lookalike`: -na / -nā, past -na / present يَكْتُبْنَ, -ā / -atā, -at / ة.
19. `mistake-attached`: كَتَبْتُهُ: doer -tu and object -hu (L3.02).
20. `mistake-forms`: the endings belong to all forms.
21. to 24. `parse-1` to `parse-4`: 7:145, 2:6, 8:17, 7:189 (20 cards).

**Step 5: Practice** (3 blocks)
25. `practice-1`: exercises 1 to 5. 26. `practice-2`: exercises 6 to 10. 27. `done`: summary, next, links, Sources.
