# Content spec: L5.03 Analyzing Ayat al-Kursi (2:255)

Written 2026-10-28. Lesson file: `src/content/lessons/level-5/03-ayat-al-kursi.mdx`. It replaces the old lesson of the same name (file name, `title`, `level` and `order` kept; `kicker`, `titleArabic` and `template: steps` are new). Modelled on L4.19 (a surah, one `Parse` per verse) with the clauses of one long verse taking the place of verses. Sentence places follow al-Jadwal (Maḥmūd Ṣāfī) on 2:255; where scholars differ, al-Durr al-Maṣūn (al-Samīn al-Ḥalabī) is read too. Roots, forms and moods are checked in the Corpus tags (`verse.py -t`). Claims about Levels 1 to 4 follow the finished lessons (L2.01, L2.02, L2.06, L3.10, L3.11, L4.04, L4.05, L4.10, L4.12 to L4.15, L4.17, L4.18).

## What the learner can do at the end

1. Split 2:255 into its ten parts and say, by al-Jadwal, which hold the five pieces of news about ٱللَّهُ (parts 1 to 4) and which are the six sentences after them (parts 5 to 10).
2. Read each part word by word: role, case, mark, reason, the parse as the books word it, and the lesson that taught the point.
3. Explain لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ: the lā that denies the kind, its noun, the left-out khabar, and why هُوَ is nominative (L4.05).
4. Tell the three إِلَّا of the verse apart (a complete negative sentence, and two sentences with a restriction), and the five negative words (the lā of the kind, four plain ones, one of them extra).
5. Name the kind, root and form of the seven verbs of the verse, using L4.11 to L4.15.
6. Say what al-Jadwal names as the style of the verse (brevity, ījāz; leaving out the joining word, faṣl) and stop there.
7. Say where the books differ (ٱلْحَىُّ, ٱلَّذِى, the "them" of أَيْدِيهِمْ, the object of شَآءَ) and what each reading does to the meaning.
