# Content spec: L1.04 Three Kinds of Word

Written 2026-10-28. The lesson is `src/content/lessons/level-1/04-three-word-types.mdx` (old title "Three Word Types (Ism, Fi'l, Harf)", same address). It follows the definition of a complete lesson at the end of `docs/redesign/CONTENT-INVENTORY.md`. This is a reading-and-recognising lesson: it makes word-type claims, not case claims, so most checks are against the Corpus tags. Parse cards add the role and the Arabic parse line from al-Jadwal.

## What the learner can do at the end

1. Say that every word is a noun (ism), a verb (fiʿl) or a little word (ḥarf), in exactly one box.
2. Name the noun signs from *al-Ajurrumiyyah* (genitive ending, tanween, al-, a preposition before it, an oath letter before it) and find them in a verse.
3. Name the verb signs from the same chapter (qad, sa-, sawfa before it; a silent feminine tāʾ at the end) and find them in a verse.
4. Say that a little word is a word with neither kind of sign.
5. Split joined little words (wa-, fa-, bi-, li-, la-, sa-) and joined end pronouns before sorting.
6. Avoid the mistakes: deciding by length or by letters alone, expecting al- or tanween on every noun, expecting a verb in every sentence, counting a joined word as one.
7. Go through four short verses word by word.

## Errors fixed from the old lesson

1. The introduction and Example 1 said وَٱلْعَصْرِ "contains all three word types". It has two: a little word and a noun. The lesson opens on 23:1, which does have all three.
2. Example 1 said the wāw "connects this oath to what came before (the previous surah)". It is an oath letter ("by"). Al-Jadwal ties the phrase to a left-out verb "I swear".
3. The verse text did not match the Tanzil text: 103:2 with لَفِيْ (should be لَفِى) and 103:3 with ءَامَنُوْا (should be ءَامَنُوا۟); and 103:3 and 103:1-3 were quoted with ۝ marks, with 103:3 cut short without "(part)".
4. Examples 3 and 4 called ٱلَّذِينَ a noun "because it has ال". The ٱل of alladhīna is part of the word (the Corpus tags it REL with no definite-article prefix). It is a noun by other signs, such as a preposition before it (3:15).
5. Example 4's whole-surah count said 8 little words. The Corpus has **10** (103:1 one, 103:2 three, 103:3 six). The listed particles were only 8 of them. Nouns, 7, and verbs, 4, were right.
6. Exercise 5 quoted قَالَ رَبِّ ٱغْفِرْ لِي as Nuh 71:28. Nuh 71:28 begins رَّبِّ ٱغْفِرْ لِى with no قَالَ. The quoted words are in 7:151 and 38:35. The exercise now uses 7:151.
7. Exercise 7 used two made-up sentences (ٱلْحَقُّ وَاضِحٌ, قَالَ ٱلْحَقَّ) without a label. They are now labelled.
8. The rule "if it has tanween or ال, it is a noun; if it is short and connects things, it is a particle" had no other test. The lesson gives the book's signs, adds that a short word can be a noun (هُوَ, مَن), and gives the exclusion definition of a little word.
