# Content spec: L1.08 Singular, Dual and Plural

Written 2026-10-28. Lesson file: `src/content/lessons/level-1/08-singular-dual-plural.mdx`. Follows the model spec (L2.01). Level 1 lesson: it introduces and previews, and uses the same terms and transliteration as Level 2. Parses follow al-Jadwal (Maḥmūd Ṣāfī). Number claims were checked against the Quranic Arabic Corpus tags (`verse.py -t`: MD/FD dual, MP/FP plural). The Corpus tags a broken plural and a sound plural the same way (P), so the sound/broken split rests on the ending and on al-Jadwal's word-form notes.

## What the learner can do at the end

1. Say that Arabic has three numbers (single, dual, plural) and that the dual means exactly two.
2. Make a dual (-āni / -ayni; the tanwīn goes; a feminine ـَة becomes ـَت).
3. Tell the three plurals apart by shape: sound masculine (-ūna / -īna), sound feminine (-āt), broken (the word changes inside).
4. See which case endings go with which number (a preview; L2.04 to L2.06 teach them) and that -ayni and -īna serve two cases.
5. Avoid four look-alike traps: -ūna on a verb, -ān on a single noun, every ـَة word taking -āt, reading the case from the ending alone; and the claim "two is always a dual".
6. Go through four verses word by word, saying the number of each noun first.

## The lesson, step by step

5 steps, 29 blocks (4, 5, 8, 9 and 3 counted; the first step also has an uncounted overview block). Each block has its own Stage.

**Step 1: One, two, many** (`start`, hero)
- `overview` (uncounted): Tiles رَجُلٌ / رَجُلَيْنِ / رِجَالٌ; goal, 40 minutes, before, 7 terms.
- `see`: 2:282 (part), five numbers in one verse. Check 1.
- `three-numbers`: three numbers; English has two. Note: *mufrad* here vs L2.01. Check 2.
- `single`: 113:1; table of four single words; gender link to L1.07.

**Step 2: The dual** (`dual`)
- `dual-make`: how to make a dual; table; -āni / -ayni.
- `dual-nom`: 55:46 جَنَّتَانِ. Check 3.
- `dual-ayni`: 2:282 رَجُلَيْنِ and 2:83 ٱلْوَٰلِدَيْنِ. Check 4.
- `dual-fem`: ٱمْرَأَتَانِ; ـَة to ـَت; table. Check 5.
- `dual-other`: 55:68 فِيهِمَا (dual pronoun) and 55:6 يَسْجُدَانِ (dual verb); note on النجم.

**Step 3: Plurals** (`plural`)
- `plural-kinds`: sound vs broken; three kinds.
- `smp`: 23:1; -ūna / -īna; table; tip on the letter before the nūn. Check 6.
- `sfp`: 33:35; -āt; table including صَلَوَات. Check 7.
- `broken`: 59:14 and 55:70; table with what changes; patterns (preview); note on نِسَاء. Check 8.
- `which`: table of endings to kind. Check 9.
- `grid`: gender and number together (corrected table).
- `ending-preview`: al-Ajurrumiyyah quote (marks of the dual and the sound masculine plural); table of endings by case; nūn drops in an idafah (L2.08).
- `agree`: number agreement preview, 12:39 and three made-up sentences. Check 10.

**Step 4: Mistakes, word by word** (`mistakes`)
- `mistake-verb` (-ūna on a verb; Check 11), `mistake-an` (رُمَّانٌ; Check 12), `mistake-ta` (قُرًى), `mistake-case` (-ayni twice), `mistake-dual` (66:4).
- `parse-1` 2:282 (8 cards), `parse-2` 12:39 (8 cards), `parse-3` 2:83 end (4 cards), `parse-4` 55:46 (3 cards).

**Step 5: Practice** (`practice`)
- `practice-1`: exercises 1 to 4. `practice-2`: exercises 5 to 9. `done`: summary, next lessons, Sources.

## Errors fixed from the old lesson

1. **"Dual example" had no dual noun.** The old "Dual Example" section is 55:68, which shows only the dual pronoun فِيهِمَا. No dual noun appeared in a verse. *Certain.* Fixed with 55:46, 2:282 and 2:83.
2. **55:6 sat under "Plural Examples (Broken)".** That verse has a dual verb and no broken plural. *Certain.* Moved to the dual step.
3. **"Internal vowel change" for every broken plural.** كِتَابٌ to كُتُبٌ drops the alif, رَجُلٌ to رِجَالٌ adds one, and بَيْتٌ to بُيُوتٌ and قَلْبٌ to قُلُوبٌ add a wāw. Three of the four rows in the old table are wrong or incomplete. *Certain* (the forms are in the verses). Fixed with a "what changes" column.
4. **"Remove ة and add ات"** contradicted the old table's own صَلَوَاتٌ row (the alif also turns into وَا). *Certain.* Fixed in `sfp`.
5. **"Used for most feminine nouns (those ending in ة)."** Many ة nouns take a broken plural, for example قَرْيَةٌ, plural قُرًى (59:14, Corpus: plural). *Certain.* Fixed in `mistake-ta`.
6. **"If you're talking about exactly two things, you MUST use the dual."** 66:4 has قُلُوبُكُمَا, a plural noun, for the hearts of two people (Corpus: قلب plural; -kumā dual). *Checked* with the Corpus; the reason for the form is not given in the lesson. Fixed in `mistake-dual`.
7. **Wrong lesson numbers in "Related Lessons".** It sent the learner to "L2.01: Grammatical cases", "L2.02: Noun-adjective agreement" and "L2.03: Verb-subject agreement". L2.01 is the nominal sentence, the cases are L2.04 to L2.06, adjective agreement is L2.09, and L2.03 is the verbal sentence. *Certain.* Fixed.
8. **55:6 was translated "The star and trees".** Al-Jadwal explains النجم here as a plant with no stem, and ٱلشَّجَرُ is tagged singular. *Checked* in al-Jadwal (Sarf). Fixed with "the plant and the tree" and a note on "star".
