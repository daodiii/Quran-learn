# Content spec: L2.04 The Nominative Case

Written 2026-10-27. Lesson file: `src/content/lessons/level-2/04-nominative-case.mdx`. Follows the model spec (L2.01). Every parse follows al-Jadwal (Maḥmūd Ṣāfī); al-Durr al-Maṣūn was not needed (no disputed point in these verses).

## What the learner can do at the end

1. Say which jobs in a sentence are nominative: mubtadaʾ, khabar, doer (fāʿil), stand-in for the doer, ism kāna, khabar inna, and words that follow a nominative word (joined by wa, or an adjective).
2. Find the mark: ḍamma (-u, -un), -ūna (sound masculine plural), -āni (dual), the wāw of the five nouns, a hidden ḍamma.
3. Know that the nūn of -ūna and -āni drops in an idafah.
4. Not confuse the -ūna of a noun with the -ūna of a verb, and not call every first word nominative (inna).
5. Go through four verses word by word.

## Errors fixed from the old lesson

1. "Exactly four" nominative jobs was wrong, and the "four functions" list put a follower (the joined word) next to the real jobs. The new lesson gives the jobs (including stand-in for the doer, ism kāna and khabar inna) and says the list is not complete.
2. "The nominative marks the DOER" and "look for the damma to find the subject of any verb" was misleading: a mubtadaʾ and a khabar are also nominative. Fixed in `raf` with a warning note.
3. "Mubtadaʾ = nominative, no exceptions" ignored al-Jadwal's reading of مِن وَلِىٍّ (2:120): genitive in the word, nominative in place. Added as a note in `mk`.
4. Old exercise 3 called ٱلْجَهْلُ (after "and") a coordinated noun (Function 4). It starts a new sentence; the wa joins sentences, not nouns. Not repeated.
5. Old exercise 1 said the damma on رَبُّكَ is "hidden under the possessive كَ". It is written on the ب. Not repeated.
6. The old marker table left out the five nouns, the hidden ḍamma and the dropped nūn. Added.
7. Old verbs note: "plural verb form". Now says the و is the doer (al-Jadwal: "the wāw is the doer") and the nūn is the verb's ending.
