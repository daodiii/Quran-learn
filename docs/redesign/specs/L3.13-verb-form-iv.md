# Content spec: L3.13 Form IV (أَفْعَلَ)

Written 2026-10-28. Lesson file: `src/content/lessons/level-3/13-verb-form-iv.mdx` (old file on `main`: `14-verb-form-iv.mdx`). Every parse follows *al-Jadwal* (Maḥmūd Ṣāfī); *al-Durr al-Maṣūn* was read on 23:29 and 17:80.

## What the learner can do at the end

1. Read the Form IV shape أَفْعَلَ and say what is added (a hamza in front, sukūn on the first root letter).
2. Say why the hamza is said (hamzat al-qaṭʿ) and tell it from the joining alif of Forms VII to X.
3. Read the past (13 persons), the present يُفْعِلُ (13 persons), the command أَفْعِلْ and the passive أُفْعِلَ.
4. Read the Corpus tag `(IV)`.
5. Give the usual meaning (carrying the action over: نَزَلَ, أَنزَلَ) and say that many meanings are learned one by one.
6. Tell Form IV from Form I verbs with a root hamza (أَكَلَ, أَخَذَ, أَتَى, أَمِنَ) and from the noun pattern أَفْعَل (أَحْسَنُ).
7. Recognise weak Form IV verbs (أَقَامَ, أَرَادَ, ءَاتَى, ءَامَنَ), including the ءَا opening.
8. Preview the participle مُفْعِل and the verbal noun إِفْعَال.
9. Go through four verses word by word.
