# Content spec: L3.12 Verb Form II (Faʿʿala)

Written 2026-10-28. File: `src/content/lessons/level-3/12-verb-form-ii.mdx` (old lesson on `main`, same path). Every parse follows al-Jadwal (Maḥmūd Ṣāfī); al-Durr al-Maṣūn is cited for 3:3 and 74:3. Text is the Tanzil text from the repo's Corpus file. Counts are Quran Learn's, made with a script on the Corpus file (1,300 is also in CURRICULUM-REVIEW.md section 1).

## What the learner can do at the end

1. Spot a Form II verb: shadda on the middle root letter, nothing in front of the past stem; Corpus tag (II).
2. Read its past (فَعَّلَ), present (يُفَعِّلُ), command (فَعِّلْ) and passive (فُعِّلَ, يُفَعَّلُ), with the Conjugation tables.
3. Tell a shadda of Form II from a shadda of a doubled root (وَضَلَّ) and from Form V.
4. Compare a Form II verb with its Form I partner in a verse pair, without a "Form II always means X" rule; know that 84 of 171 Form II verbs have no Form I partner in the Quran.
5. Recognise Form II verbs with a weak letter (صَلَّىٰ, وَفَّىٰ, سَمَّىٰ, يُزَكِّي) and point to L4.11 to L4.13.
6. Preview the participles (مُفَعِّل, مُفَعَّل) and the verbal noun (تَفْعِيل), pointing to L3.19 and L3.20.
7. Go through four verses word by word.

## The lesson, step by step

4 steps, 33 blocks (6, 7, 8, 12). Quick checks 1 to 22. Exercises 1 to 10. Parse panels 4 (3:3, 3:164, 74:3, 75:31).

**Step 1: Spot Form II** (hero): `overview`, `see` (3:3), `compare` (26:193 / 3:3, count of ن ز ل), `shape` (فَعَّلَ, table of pairs), `spot` (Corpus tag, 1,300 and by tense), `common` (nine verbs, Form I partners).
**Step 2: Past, present, command, passive**: `past` (table, 96:5), `present-rule` (12:6), `present-table`, `command` (73:4, table), `command-verse` (74:4, Note on the fāʾ), `passive` (16:44), `glance` (table of eight patterns).
**Step 3: Meanings**: `teach`, `make-state`, `call-false`, `call-true`, `again`, `no-partner` (87 / 84), `weak` (53:37, 323 of 1,300), `nouns` (مُفَعِّل, مُفَعَّل, تَفْعِيل; 92, 97, 126).
**Step 4: Word by word, mistakes, practice**: `parse-1` to `parse-4`, five mistakes (shadda of a doubled root; skipping the shadda; counting the doubled letter as a root letter; one meaning for all; Form V), `practice-1` (exercises 1 to 5), `practice-2` (6 to 10), `done` (summary, Sources).

## Errors fixed from the old lesson

1. The verse labelled 2:89 (فَقَدْ كَذَّبُوا بِٱلْحَقِّ) is 6:5. 2:89 does not contain it. *Checked.*
2. Example texts were typed, not Tanzil (plain alif in آدَمَ, وَأَنزَلْنَا). Fixed by `check-verses`.
3. عَذَبَ (Form I "he punished") does not exist in the Quran: the root ع ذ ب has 41 Form II words and no Form I verb. *Checked in the Corpus.*
4. كَسَّرَ / كَسَرَ, خَرَّجَ, حَسَّنَ and قَدُسَ / قَدَّسَ are given as Quranic examples. The Corpus has no verb of the roots ك س ر, and no Form II of خ ر ج or ح س ن; قَدَّسَ occurs once (نُقَدِّسُ, 2:30) and has no Form I; فَهَّمَ once (21:79) with no Form I فَهِمَ. *Checked.*
5. Exercise 4 says قَدَّسَ is used in "سُبْحَانَكَ وَبِحَمْدِكَ". The Quran verb is نُقَدِّسُ لَكَ (2:30). *Checked.*
6. "Mistake 3" calls عَلَمَ "Form I, if such a form existed" and the removal test (take off the shadda, see if a root remains) is unreliable. Replaced by a three-point test.
7. "The root is always three letters" is wrong (four-letter roots exist, L3.03).
8. Mistake 1's test uses مُحَمَّد (a name) and not a verb with a doubled root. Replaced by وَضَلَّ (6:24).
9. "Form II نَزَّلَ is gradual, Form IV أَنزَلَ is one act": an unsourced claim, dropped. L3.13 can compare them.
10. "Three meanings: causative, intensive, transitive" as a rule with examples, three of which are not in the Quran (items 3 and 4). Replaced by verse pairs and an explicit "not a rule" note.
11. Lesson numbers: L3.14 pointed at "Forms VII & VIII" and L3.17 at Form III as "build on"; renumbered.
