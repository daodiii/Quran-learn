## What the learner can do at the end

1. Read the story of Ibrahim in two passages (Ash-Shuʿarā 26:69 to 26:82, As-Saffāt 37:83 to 37:113) and say, for each verse, which past verb moves the story, who speaks, and what the words said are.
2. Say that the sentence after a verb of saying has the place of an object (naṣb), "what is said" (L1.09), and that it can be a question, a statement, a command or a call.
3. Tell the speaker from the verses around the verb, not from the verb's number (37:102 has two `قَالَ` with two speakers).
4. Read a when-clause with its answer: `إِذْ` (a badal in 26:70 and 37:85), `إِذَا` (26:80), `لَمَّا` with a written answer (37:102) and with an answer left out (37:103).
5. Find the four kinds of ḥāl that the passage has (one word, a sentence with a verb, a sentence with wāw, a phrase) and tell a khabar of ظَلَّ (26:71) from a ḥāl.
6. Read `وَجَدَ` with two objects (26:74, 37:102), the question words `مَا` and `مَاذَا`, the offering `أَلَا`, the call and its answer, and the different jobs of `فَ`.
7. Say what a verse leaves out when al-Jadwal says so (an object, an answer, a step of the story).
