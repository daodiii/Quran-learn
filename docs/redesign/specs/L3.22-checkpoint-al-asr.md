# Content spec: L3.22 Checkpoint, Surah Al-ʿAsr

Written 2026-10-28. Lesson file: `src/content/lessons/level-3/22-checkpoint-al-asr.mdx`. Modelled on L1.11 (Al-Ikhlas) and L2.12 (Al-Kawthar). Every parse follows al-Jadwal (Maḥmūd Ṣāfī); roots, forms, tense, person and voice are checked in the Corpus tags (`verse.py -t`). Al-Durr al-Maṣūn was read for 103:1-3.

## What the learner can do at the end

1. Give the root of each of the nine root-words of Al-ʿAsr, and say what to peel off.
2. Name the form, tense, person and voice of each verb (Form IV, I, VI, VI; past, 3rd masculine plural, active), with the pattern and the root kind.
3. Tell a verbal noun (خُسْرٍ, ٱلْحَقِّ, ٱلصَّبْرِ) from a doer word (ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ) and from a plain noun (ٱلْعَصْرِ, ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ).
4. Read the sentence level: oath, inna sentence as its answer, exception with the joining noun and its ṣila.
5. Say why the final kasra of ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ is accusative and that of ٱلْحَقِّ genitive.
6. Use the same tools on other verses.
