# Content spec: L3.18 The Rare Forms: VI, VII and IX

Written 2026-10-28. The lesson is `src/content/lessons/level-3/18-verb-forms-vi-vii-ix.mdx`. It replaces three old lessons: L3.16 Form VI (the whole lesson), the Form VII part of the old L3.17, and the Form IX part of the old L3.18 (old files on `main`: `16-verb-form-vi.mdx`, `17-verb-forms-vii-viii.mdx`, `18-verb-forms-ix-x.mdx`). The Form VIII part moved to L3.14 and the Form X part to L3.16.

## What the learner can do at the end

1. Say why these three forms share a lesson (77, 51 and 5 verb words) and that the aim is to recognise them.
2. Spot Form VI (تَ plus a long ā after the first root letter), find its root, and say it is Form III with تَ.
3. Say what Form VI often means ("one another") and that it does not always mean that (تَبَارَكَ, تَشَٰبَهَ).
4. Read the dropped تَ in the present (لَا تَعَاوَنُوا۟) and the Form VI that starts with ٱ (ٱثَّاقَلْتُمْ).
5. Spot Form VII (ٱنْ plus three root letters), find its root, and tell it from a Form I command whose root starts with ن (ٱنفِرُوا۟, ٱنظُرْ).
6. Spot Form IX (ٱ, three different root letters, last letter doubled), name its colour meaning, and tell it from a doubled root (ٱنفَضُّوا۟, ٱضْطُرَّ).
7. Read the Corpus tag (VI), (VII), (IX) and the ṣarf notes of al-Jadwal.
8. Go through four verses word by word.

## Errors fixed from the old lessons

1. "Every Form VI verb has a Form III parent" (old mistake 3), and chains such as ع و ن: عَاوَنَ → تَعَاوَنَ, ن ب ز: نَابَزَ, د ي ن: دَايَنَ. The Corpus has no Form III verb from these three roots in the Quran. Now a Note and `from-iii` use a real pair (ظ ه ر).
2. Invented Form I bases: عَانَ, نَبَزَ, "he turned" for ق ل ب, "he released" for ط ل ق are not Quran verbs (the Corpus has no active Form I for ق ل ب or ط ل ق).
3. 5:2: both verbs called a command; the second follows لَا and is jussive. Al-Jadwal and the Corpus agree.
4. The Form VI example titled تَبَايَعَ shows تَدَايَنتُم (2:282).
5. Form VII table words اِنْكَسَرَ, اِنْفَتَحَ and Form IX words اِحْمَرَّ, اِصْفَرَّ, اِخْضَرَّ are not Quran verbs and were not labelled. Yellow and green are participles in the Quran.
6. The old text put ٱبْيَضَّتْ in 3:106; it is in 3:107 and 12:84.
7. "Form VII is ALWAYS intransitive": an absolute. Now stated for the verses in the lesson.
8. "Form VI present has two ت": true only for the full form; the Quran usually shows one (dropped). Added from al-Jadwal.
9. The unsourced Form IX "defects" (blind, crooked) and the invented gloss "faces becoming radiant" are dropped; the lesson cites al-Jadwal's note on white and black faces.
10. Old "Form VI singular is a mistake" claim and the claim that VI is always mutual: replaced by verse pairs.
