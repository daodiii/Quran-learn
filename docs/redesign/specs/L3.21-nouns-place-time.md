# Content spec: L3.21 Words for Where and When (nouns of place and time)

Written 2026-10-28. This says what the rewritten L3.21 teaches, with which verses, and how each verse is parsed. It follows the model spec (`L2.01-nominal-sentence.md`) and the definition of a complete lesson (end of `docs/redesign/CONTENT-INVENTORY.md`). The lesson is `src/content/lessons/level-3/21-nouns-place-time.mdx` (old file on `main`: same name, old number L3.21).

## What the learner can do at the end

1. Say what a noun of place or time is: a word made from a verb root with مَ in front, naming where or when an action happens.
2. Read the pattern مَفْعَل and pick between it and مَفْعِل from the vowel of the present tense (kasra gives مَفْعِل; ḍamma or fatḥa gives مَفْعَل), using al-Jadwal's rule and its reasons (3:121, 2:60, 9:25).
3. Know the eleven words that break the rule (al-Jadwal, notes on 17:80), among them مَشْرِق, مَغْرِب, مَسْجِد.
4. Read the noun of place from a longer verb (مُدْخَل, مُلْتَحَد, مُسْتَقَرّ, مُسْتَوْدَع): the shape of the passive participle.
5. See that one shape can name a place, a time or the action (maṣdar mīmī): 2:196, 18:58, 17:80, 2:36, 97:5.
6. Read the broken plural مَفَاعِل and its case endings (diptote, L2.06), and the tāʾ marbūṭa (مَقْبَرَة).
7. Tell a noun of place from a place word (ẓarf), from a passive participle, and from a word whose م is a root letter.
8. Go through four verses word by word.

## The lesson, step by step

5 steps, 22 blocks (5, 4, 5, 4, 4; the first block of step 1 is the uncounted overview). 11 exercises, 10 quick checks, 4 `Parse` panels.

**Step 1: Where and when** (`start`)
- `overview` (uncounted): Formula; `Start` with six terms.
- `see`: 18:60 (part). Check 1.
- `build`: pattern مَفْعَل letter by letter; Note on noun of time. Check 2.
- `time`: 2:196, place or time; the sentence decides (al-Jadwal, 17:80). Check 3.
- `zarf`: noun of place against ẓarf (2:115: المشرق against ثمّ).

**Step 2: Mafʿal or mafʿil?** (`patterns`)
- `two`: the two patterns. `rule`: the present vowel decides (table; al-Jadwal's reasons on 3:121, 9:25, 2:60); L3.03's مَرْقَد. Checks 4, 5.
- `kasra`: 58:11; Note on weak verbs (L4.13, L4.14).
- `exceptions`: the eleven; the مطلع / مسكن Note. Check 6.

**Step 3: Longer verbs, plurals, one word with three jobs** (`more`)
- `derived`: 2:36 and the table of forms IV, VIII, X. Check 7.
- `three-jobs`: 17:80 and 18:58. Check 8.
- `plural`: 3:121; the table of six plurals.
- `diptote`: five tiles for five endings. Check 9.
- `tamarbuta`: 102:2, مَفْعَلَة, مَأْسَدَة.

**Step 4: Word by word** (`verses`): 18:60, 2:115, 9:25, 97:5. Check 10.

**Step 5: Practice** (`practice`): `method` (four questions, two traps, one Note listing six mistakes), `practice-1` (Ex 1 to 7), `practice-2` (Ex 8 to 11), `summary` with `Sources`.

## Errors fixed from the old lesson

1. The central rule chose the pattern from the **past** vowel (faʿila gives mafʿil). Al-Jadwal chooses by the **present** vowel (17:80, 3:121, 9:25, 2:60). Certain.
2. جَلَسَ was called faʿila; its past is faʿala and its present has kasra. Certain.
3. A table row "faʿula gives مَفْعَل" with the example مَكْبَر: unsourced, dropped.
4. The opening example (2:217) quotes words that are not in the Quran (searched, 0 hits). Certain.
5. Example 1 (3:96) has no noun of place, and "15 times, always" is unsourced. Dropped.
6. Exercise 3 says 2:144 has ٱلْمَسْجِدِ "after مِنَ"; the verse has شَطْرَ ٱلْمَسْجِدِ (an idafa).
7. "Feminine ـَة for institutional places": al-Jadwal says the ة is heard, with no rule.
8. مَذْكَر "place of remembrance" is not an attested word: dropped.
9. "Level 3 complete ... take any root, generate 10 verb forms, conjugate 14 persons": false (weak verbs are Level 4). Its recap used the old lesson numbers.
10. "Must be memorized", "extremely common": unsourced.
