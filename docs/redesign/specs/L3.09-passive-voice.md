# Content spec: L3.09 Passive Voice (al-Majhūl)

Written 2026-10-28. The lesson is `src/content/lessons/level-3/09-passive-voice.mdx` (old file on `main`: `07-passive-voice.mdx`). It follows the definition of a complete lesson in `docs/redesign/CONTENT-INVENTORY.md`. Every parse follows al-Jadwal (Maḥmūd Ṣāfī); al-Durr al-Maṣūn is cited for the one disputed point (2:11).

## What the learner can do at the end

1. Tell a passive verb from an active one by its vowels: past فُعِلَ (ḍamma, kasra), present يُفْعَلُ (ḍamma, fatḥa).
2. Say what a passive verb leaves out (the doer) and what takes its place (the nāʾib fāʿil), and that the stand-in is nominative.
3. Name the kind of stand-in: a written noun, an attached ending (-tu, -ū, -ūna), a hidden pronoun, or the words said after قِيلَ.
4. Say why the verb matches the stand-in in gender (5:3 feminine, 18:49 masculine).
5. Explain what happens with a verb that took two objects (أُوتُوا ٱلْكِتَٰبَ).
6. Recognise that other verb forms and weak verbs have passives (قِيلَ changes shape; L4.12).
7. Go through four verses word by word: role, case, mark, reason.

Points that needed care:
- **2:11.** Al-Jadwal reads the sentence لَا تُفْسِدُوا as the stand-in; its footnote says most grammarians read a hidden stand-in (the saying) that the sentence explains. Al-Durr al-Maṣūn gives both and names al-Zamakhshari for the first. The lesson gives al-Jadwal's main reading and names the other in a "Good to know" note.
- **6:160, the first wa.** Corpus: circumstantial. Al-Jadwal: starts a new sentence. The lesson follows al-Jadwal and says so in a note.
- **Al-Ajurrumiyyah.** The chapter is the text saved in `ajurrumiyyah-wikisource.txt` (line "باب المفعول الذي لم يسم فاعله"). The lesson quotes its three lines with vowel marks added by Quran Learn (labelled). The book calls the stand-in "the object whose doer is not named"; the lesson says al-Jadwal uses nāʾib fāʿil. The book's paradigm of twelve forms with ضُرِبَ is the reason the past table uses ضَرَبَ.
- **Corpus vs al-Jadwal on form or root:** none found among the verses used. The Corpus tags أُوتُوا as Form IV of ءَاتَىٰ (lemma آتَى); al-Jadwal gives no form for it. Same view.

## The lesson, step by step

5 steps, 34 blocks (5, 8, 7, 7 and 7).

**Step 1: The doer left out** (`start`)
- `overview`: formula, goal, terms.
- `see`: 2:183 (part), who made fasting a duty? Check 1.
- `compare`: made-up active next to 2:183; three changes (vowels, doer gone, object becomes stand-in). Check 2.
- `names`: maʿlūm / majhūl; counts note.
- `why`: 18:49 (start); reasons in plain terms; style belongs to L4.17. Check 3.

**Step 2: The two shapes** (`shapes`)
- `book`: the Ajurrumiyyah rule in Arabic and English. Check 4.
- `past-shape`: فُعِلَ; five real verbs. Check 5.
- `spot`: كَتَبَ / كُتِبَ; warning that unvowelled text hides the voice.
- `past-conj`: Conjugation of ضُرِبَ, 13 rows. Check 6.
- `present-rule`: يُفْعَلُ with 6:160; four verbs. Check 7.
- `present-conj`: Conjugation of يُظْلَمُ, 13 rows. Check 8.
- `present-verse`: 57:5. Check 9.
- `tell`: يَظْلِمُ (18:49) next to يُظْلَمُونَ (6:160); pointer to L1.11. Check 10.

**Step 3: The stand-in** (`standin`)
- `written`: 5:3; verb matches gender; Form II. Check 11.
- `agree`: 30:2 next to 18:49; note on the story of the verse; note on L2.03. Check 12.
- `hidden-standin`: 2:24 (end) and the second كُتِبَ of 2:183. Check 13.
- `attached`: 10:72 and 2:144; table of endings. Check 14.
- `two-objects`: 2:144. Check 15.
- `no-object`: 2:11; two readings in a note.

**Step 4: Other verbs, weak verbs, mistakes** (`more`)

- `weak`: قِيلَ (36:26), why it is not قُوِلَ. Check 16.

**Step 5: Word by word, practice** (`practice`)
