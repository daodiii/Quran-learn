The old lesson is not in the error inventory; the inventory below is new. The lesson is `src/content/lessons/level-4/06-emphasis-affirmation.mdx`.

## What the learner can do at the end

1. Name the five ways the Quran strengthens a sentence (little words, the oath, the nūn of emphasis, repeating, words of meaning) and find the word that does it.
2. Read قَدْ, لَقَدْ and the strengthening لَ in a verse, say what each sits on, and say which are tagged how often in the Corpus.
3. Split an oath into the oath letter (وَ, بِ, تَ), what is sworn by, and the answer of the oath.
4. Recognise the nūn of emphasis (heavy and light) at the end of a present verb, and say that the verb is built on the fatḥa and the nūn does not make it subjunctive.
5. Tell tawkid in the narrow sense (a following word: كُلّ, أَجْمَع, نَفْس, عَيْن) from look-alikes: نَفْس as a doer or object, كُلّ as the first word of an idafah, the لَ that ties the answer to لَوْ.
6. Make the following word copy the case of the word it strengthens.
7. Go through four verses word by word (14:39, 14:12, 38:82, 15:30).

## The lesson, step by step

5 steps, 29 blocks, 20 `Check`s, 10 `Exercise`s, 4 `Parse` panels (6, 7, 9 and 6 cards).

**Step 1: Five ways** (hero; 3 blocks)
1. `overview`: Start, formula "plain sentence plus strengthener equals stronger sentence".
2. `see`: 20:56 (`Sentence`, roles). Check 1.
3. `map`: the five ways (list in the stage, table in the text), the narrow meaning of tawkid (a following word), the four followers in al-Ajurrumiyyah; note `know` on the two uses of the word. Check 2.

**Step 2: Little words** (5 blocks)
4. `inna`: 49:12, 14:39; Corpus count 1,682. Check 3.
5. `lam`: tiles of five places for the strengthening لَ; the beginning lām (59:13), لَسَوْفَ (26:49); note `know` with the 1,001 count and its warning. Check 4.
6. `qad`: 87:14, 33:18; counts (406; 398 past; 8 present). Check 5.
7. `laqad`: 95:4; count 183; note `know` (al-Jadwal's oath lām against the Corpus tag). Check 6.
8. `parse-1`: 14:39 (6 cards).

**Step 3: Oath and nūn** (5 blocks)
9. `oath`: 21:57 and the three oath letters (tiles). Check 7.
10. `nun`: 96:15 and counts (243; 176 and 67; the word before). Check 8.
11. `nun-two`: 12:32 (heavy and light) and 3:169 (after a ban); counts 241 and 2. Check 9.
12. `nun-mood`: four forms of نَصْبِرُ (tiles), built on the fatḥa, not subjunctive. Check 10.
13. `parse-2`: 14:12 (7 cards).

**Step 4: Following words** (8 blocks)
14. `repeat`: 102:3, 102:4, 55:13; counts 33 and 31. Check 11.

## Errors fixed from the old lesson

3. 14:12 is quoted as لَنَصْبِرُ عَلَىٰ مَآ ءَاذَيْتُمُونَا, without the nūn. Tanzil has وَلَنَصْبِرَنَّ. The verse was taught as a bare lām on a present verb, when it is the lām and the heavy nūn. Fixed (`nun-mood`, parse 2).
