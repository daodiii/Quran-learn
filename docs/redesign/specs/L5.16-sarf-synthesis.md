## What the learner can do at the end

1. Take a Quran word apart with four questions: root, kind of root, form (I to X) or noun shape, and tense, mood and voice.
2. Peel a word to its root, test the root in the scale ف ع ل, and name the kind (sound, hollow, defective, assimilated, hamzated, doubled).
3. Name the ten verb forms from their shape and avoid the look-alikes (أَكَلَ, ٱسْتَوَىٰ, قَالَ, ءَامَنَ, وَضَلَّ, ٱنفِرُوا۟); know that what a form adds is a tendency.
4. Read the past (13 forms), the present, naṣb and jazm, the command and the passive, and say what each weak or hamzated kind changes.
5. Name the noun built on a root: doer word, done-to word, verbal noun, noun of place; read a root family.
6. Take four verses word by word (71:10, 2:22 part, 87:4, 51:56) and write the sarf line of each word.
7. Say for each point which lesson taught it, and which rules are teaching summaries.
