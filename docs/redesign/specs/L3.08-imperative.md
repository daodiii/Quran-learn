# Content spec: L3.08 Commands (the imperative)

Written 2026-10-28. Follows the shape of `L2.01-nominal-sentence.md`. Lesson file: `src/content/lessons/level-3/08-imperative.mdx` (old file on `main`: `06-imperative.mdx`). Source for every parse: *al-Jadwal* (Maḥmūd Ṣāfī). *al-Durr al-Maṣūn* was tried on 2:21 and 2:110 for the status of the command's ending and has no entry on it.

## What the learner can do at the end

1. Make a command from a present-tense verb: end it like the jussive, take off the prefix, add the helper ٱ when the word starts with a silent letter.
2. Choose the helper's sound (u after a ḍamma, i after a fatḥa or kasra) for a Form I verb.
3. Give and recognise the five commands (one man, one woman, two people, men, women) and say where the doer is: hidden for one man, the ending for the other four.
4. Say what a command is built on (sukūn, dropped nūn, dropped weak letter) in al-Jadwal's words, and say that al-Ajurrumiyyah calls it jussive.
5. Know there is no command form for "he", "she" or "they" (لِ plus the jussive), and that "do not" is لَا plus the jussive (both taught in L3.07).
6. Recognise commands that look different: weak verbs (قُلْ, ٱدْعُ, خُذْ), Form II, III, IV, V, VIII, X commands, and a command with an attached pronoun.
7. Go through four verses word by word.

## The lesson, step by step (5 steps, 24 blocks, 23 counted)

**Step 1, Making a command (hero)** (4 blocks)
- `overview` (uncounted): Formula and `Start` (goal, 45 minutes, 6 terms).
- `see`: 96:1; one word, a hidden "you"; Corpus count of command words (1,876; Form I 1,181). Check 1.
- `recipe`: the three moves (sukūn, drop تَ, add ٱ) with تَقْرَأُ. Check 2.
- `helper`: u or i by the vowel on the middle letter; table of five verbs; silent after وَ and فَ; Form IV warning. Check 3.

**Step 2, Who is told: five ways to say "you"** (4 blocks)
- `five`: Conjugation table of ٱسْجُدْ (five persons); where the doer is. Check 4.
- `from-present`: present, jussive ending, command for all five; the women's nūn stays; al-Ajurrumiyyah on the nūn. Check 5.
- `men`: 53:62; Corpus counts by person (951, 870, 27, 20, 8). Check 6.
- `others`: 3:43, 20:43, 33:34; the ى is not "me". Check 7.

**Step 3, Why the ending does not change** (4 blocks)
- `mabni`: built; three kinds of fixed ending. Check 8.
- `words`: al-Jadwal's wording for each person and for the weak verb.
- `first-book`: al-Ajurrumiyyah's "jussive" and its two signs (Arabic text quoted from Wikisource); al-Jadwal's reading named as the one this course follows.
- `not-he`: 59:18; لِ plus the jussive; "do not" pointed to L3.07. Check 9.

**Step 4, Commands that look different** (4 blocks)
- `weak`: 114:1; five surahs open with قُلْ; 349 Corpus words from قَالَ; table of five weak verbs with the lessons that teach them; the weak letter returns with an ending; قُلِ before ٱل. Check 10.
- `forms`: 2:110; Form II, III, IV, V, VIII, X table with the lesson for each; Form IV starts with أَ. Check 11.
- `pronoun`: 3:147; an attached object pronoun; a command as a request or a prayer. Check 12.
- `spot`: 96:19; three clues for a command form. Check 13.

**Step 5, Mistakes, four verses word by word, practice** (8 blocks)
- `mistakes`: one list of nine mistakes (the old six, two of them merged, plus four new: no written "you", "do not" is not a command, no command form for "he", Form IV starts with أَ).
- `parse-1` to `parse-4`: 3:43, 2:110, 16:125, 7:155 (28 cards in all, each with an `irab` line).
- `practice-1`: exercises 1 to 5. `practice-2`: exercises 6 to 11.
- `done`: summary, next steps, Sources.

## Errors fixed from the old lesson

1. "Six possible imperative conjugations, not fourteen", then it lists five. The lesson says five.
2. It calls قُلْ, كُلْ and خُذْ "irregular imperatives derived from hollow and defective verbs". Only قُلْ is hollow. كُلْ and خُذْ are hamzated (initial hamza). The lesson says so and sends them to L4.12 and L4.15.
3. "Four surahs begin with قُلْ". Five do: 72, 109, 112, 113 and 114 (Corpus, first word of verse 1).
4. "قُلْ always addresses the Prophet." The Corpus has 12 command forms of قَالَ for a group (قُولُوا۟), for example 33:70 (L1.10).
5. "Form IV and some others use damma on the helper." A Form IV command starts with the cutting hamza أَ, a different thing. The helper of Forms VIII and X sounds i.
6. The root of صَلِّ was given as ص ل ي. The Corpus and al-Jadwal (L2.12) have ص ل و. The lesson does not repeat it.
7. Exercise 3 quoted the whole of Surah Al-Falaq, not exactly (مِنْ for مِن, no verse marks) and its answer first said "two imperatives", then corrected itself. Replaced by the 96:19 exercise.
8. "If you see a prefix, it's NOT imperative" is true of the command form but 59:18 shows a prefixed verb that means a command (لِ plus jussive). The lesson separates form from meaning.
9. Example 5 called أَحَدٌ the "predicate" and ٱللَّهُ the "subject", leaving out the other reading of هُوَ (L2.01 error 5). Dropped; the lesson refers back to L1.11.
10. Forward links used old numbers: "Next: L3.08 Subject Pronouns", "L3.05 Subjunctive", "L3.15 Weak Verbs", "L4.08 Prohibition". Replaced by the new numbers and addresses.
11. "Example 2: Commands in Al-Fatiha" quoted 1:5, which has no command. Dropped.
