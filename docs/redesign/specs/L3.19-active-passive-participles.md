# Content spec: L3.19 Doer Words and Done-To Words (active and passive participles)

Written 2026-10-28. Lesson file: `src/content/lessons/level-3/19-active-passive-participles.mdx`. Old text: `git show main:src/content/lessons/level-3/19-active-passive-participles.mdx`. Every parse follows al-Jadwal (Maḥmūd Ṣāfī), including its ṣarf notes for patterns.

## What the learner can do at the end

1. Tell a doer word (ism fāʿil) from a done-to word (ism mafʿūl) and say what each names.
2. Build both in Form I (فَاعِل, مَفْعُول) and in Forms II to X (مُ + present shape; kasra or fatḥa before the last letter).
3. Say that a participle is a noun: case, ٱلْ, tanwīn, gender, number, adjective agreement.
4. Say that a doer word can take an object (accusative after tanwīn or -īna, genitive as first word of an idafah) and still makes a nominal sentence.
5. Tell a participle from other describing words (فَعِيل, فَعْلَٰن, ṣifa mushabbaha) and recognise weak-root participles (قَائِم, مُقِيم, بَاقٍ, هَادٍ).
6. Go through four verses word by word.

## The lesson, step by step

1. **A noun made from a verb** (5 blocks): overview, look (2:30), from a verb (جَعَلَ, ism fāʿil, link to L2.03), two kinds (counts), done-to word in a verse (56:30).
2. **How they are built** (8 blocks): فَاعِل, مَفْعُول, six pairs, the مُ rule, active table II to X, passive table, one vowel apart, weak roots.
3. **A noun that can work like a verb** (7 blocks): a participle is a noun (2:92, L2.12 link), gender and number (Conjugation مُؤْمِن, 80:15), adjective (56:33), not every describing word, object (38:71, 18:18), accusative or genitive (6:95, 5:2), no time of its own (15:46, ḥāl preview).
4. **Four mistakes and four verses word by word** (8 blocks): 4 mistakes, parses of 2:30, 6:95, 13:7, 56:33.
5. **Practice** (3 blocks): 12 exercises, summary, sources.
