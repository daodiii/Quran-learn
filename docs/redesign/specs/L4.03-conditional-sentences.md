# Content spec: L4.03 Conditional Sentences

Written 2026-10-28. This is the old L4.03 "Conditional Sentences (Shart & Jawab)", kept at the same number. It follows the definition of a complete lesson (end of `docs/redesign/CONTENT-INVENTORY.md`). The lesson is `src/content/lessons/level-4/03-conditional-sentences.mdx`. This lesson is the **sentence pattern** (the word of condition, the condition, the answer, the jazm on both verbs, the فَ on the answer). L4.04 is the **words of condition** (إِذَا, لَوْ, مَنْ, مَا and the rest); this lesson links to it and does not teach them.

## What the learner can do at the end

1. Find the word of condition, the condition (and its verb) and the answer in a Quran sentence.
2. Read jazm on both verbs when it shows: a lost nūn (تُقْرِضُوا۟), a sukūn (يُضَٰعِفْهُ, نَعُدْ), a dropped weak letter (يُؤْتِ, تَكُ).
3. Say why a past verb after إِنْ shows nothing ("in the place of jazm") and why the khabar of kāna keeps its nūn (إِن كُنتُمْ تُحِبُّونَ).
4. Say when the answer needs a فَ (a command, a noun sentence, قَدْ, سَوْفَ, لَن, إِنَّ, negative مَا) and when it does not (a verb in jazm, a plain past verb).
5. Tell إِنْ "if" from إِنَّ "indeed" (and know that a third إِنْ means "not").
6. Write the iʿrāb line of the verb of the condition and of the answer.
7. Go through four verses word by word.

### Where scholars differ (the "Good to know" notes)

| Case | Common reading (followed) | Other reading, named in the lesson | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| The role of the condition clause after مَن | al-Jadwal: مَن is the mubtadaʾ and the condition clause is its khabar (29:6) | al-Jadwal's footnotes at 2:197 and 4:123: the khabar may be the condition and the answer together | `other-words` |
| The answer put before the condition (39:13) | al-Jadwal: the answer is left out, the earlier words point to it | "Some grammarians call the earlier words the answer itself": **not confirmed in al-Jadwal or another approved source**; kept as a short flagged sentence, see "What is not checked" | `answer-first` |
| An answer in the plain present (3:120) | al-Jadwal: the answer with an unwritten فَ (he attributes it to al-Mubarrad) | Sibawayh (named in al-Jadwal's footnote): the verb only points to an answer put first in meaning | `plain-present` |

For إِنْ the condition clause has no place of its own in al-Jadwal's reading, and the lesson says so.

## The lesson, step by step

5 steps, 31 blocks (5, 6, 8, 9, 3), 21 `Check`s, 10 `Exercise`s, 4 `Parse` panels (8, 9, 9 and 9 cards).

**Step 1: Condition and answer** (hero; 5 blocks)
1. `overview`: Start (7 terms), formula "condition plus answer equals a conditional sentence".
2. `see`: 8:29 (part). The two halves; both verbs cut short. Check 1.
3. `names`: شَرْط, جَوَاب الشَّرْط, جَزَاء, أَدَاة الشَّرْط, فِعْل الشَّرْط, جُمْلَة شَرْطِيَّة; English comparison; al-Jadwal uses the same names. Check 2.
4. `book`: al-Ajurrumiyyah's list of eighteen jazm words (إِنْ is on it); the book does not say "two verbs". Check 3.
5. `in-inna`: 31:8 against إِنْ; the Corpus count of 578 conditional إِنْ and what follows. Check 4.

**Step 2: The two verbs** (6 blocks)
6. `jazm-both`: 64:17 (part); the table of signs on يَكْتُبُ; the sukūn is for every one-person form. Check 5.
7. `chain`: 8:29; answers joined with وَ stay in jazm. Check 6.
8. `doer`: 4:78 (part); the doer after the verb; hollow-verb yā drops (L4.12). Check 7.
9. `past`: 17:7, 17:8 and 8:19 (parts); past verbs in the place of jazm; the Corpus count. Check 8.
10. `kana`: 3:31 (part); the khabar of kāna keeps its nūn; an answer to a command (يُحْبِبْكُمُ). Check 9.
11. `weak`: 4:40 (part); تَكُ, يُؤْتِ; sends to L4.11 to L4.13. Check 10.

**Step 3: The answer and فَ** (8 blocks)

13. `fa-command`: 5:6 (part), 2:23; the made-up line without فَ. Check 12.

15. `fa-particles`: 8:19, 9:28, 9:80 (parts): قَدْ, سَوْفَ, لَن. Check 14.

## Errors fixed from the old lesson

The old L4.03 is not in `CONTENT-INVENTORY.md`. These 15 were found by reading it against the Corpus text and al-Jadwal.

1. كُنتُمْ (2:23) is called jussive "marked by the deletion of the final nūn" (introduction and example 1). It is a past verb; it has no nūn. The lesson says "in the place of jazm" (`past`, `kana`, Parse 2). *Certain: the old text says the opposite further down in example 1.*
2. "Conditional sentences are COMPOUND verbal sentences": the answer can be a noun sentence (فَهُوَ خَيْرٌ), as the old rule box itself says. Fixed (`fa-noun`). *Certain.*
3. "The condition clause usually has a present-tense verb in form": the Corpus tags a past verb after the conditional إِنْ in 384 of 578 cases and a jussive present verb in 116 (`in-inna`, `past`). *Counted.*
4. The English comparison "If he were to come, then I were to help" is not English, and "BOTH verbs change" is not true of past verbs. Replaced by a plain "if … will" comparison (`names`).
5. Link "L3.05 Past & Present Tense" for the moods: L3.05 is the past tense, L3.06 the present and L3.07 the jazm. Now L3.05 and L3.07 by their right names.
6. Link "L5.07 Complex Conditional Structures", which pointed to L4.04. There is no such lesson. Dropped.
7. The sukūn is said to belong to "singular masculine verbs". Every one-person form takes it (he, she, you, I, we); the old lesson's own نُكَفِّرْ (we) and تُصِبْهُمْ (she) contradict it (`jazm-both`). *Certain.*
8. The verse text is not Tanzil's in three verses: 47:7 typed تَنْصُرُوا۟ (sukūn on the ن; Tanzil has تَنصُرُوا۟), 2:23 with extra marks on رَيْبٍۢ and بِسُورَةٍۢ, 4:78 with حَسَنَةٌۭ. Checked with a script against the Corpus text. All lesson verses now pass `check-verses.py`.
9. Example 3 cites 2:25 for إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ. 2:25 begins وَبَشِّرِ ٱلَّذِينَ…. The phrase with إِنَّ is in 2:277, 10:9, 31:8 and others. Replaced by 31:8 (`in-inna`). *Checked.*
10. The rule box says both verbs "take jussive mood" whenever إِنْ is used, without past verbs and noun-sentence answers, and the old example 1 says the opposite. Rewritten (`done`, `fa-table`).
11. Mistake 1 calls an answer in the plain present wrong (إِن تَكْتُبْ أَقْرَأُ). 3:120 has one, and al-Jadwal gives two readings (al-Mubarrad, Sibawayh). Now `plain-present` (Good to know) and `mistake-own`. The example was also not labelled as made up.
12. "✓ Correct: إِن كُنتُمْ صَٰدِقِينَ فَأْتُوا۟" joins two parts of 2:23 (the end of the verse and a word from the start), shown as correct and not labelled as made up. Replaced by 5:6 and the labelled line in `fa-command`.
13. Exercise 2 pairs تُنْفِقُوا۟ and يُضَٰعِفْهُ "from a conditional sentence" with no reference. They are not in one verse (يُضَٰعِفْهُ is in 64:17, with تُقْرِضُوا۟). Replaced by a real verse (Exercise 2). *Checked.*
14. Exercise 1 types تُطِيعُوهُ۟ with a stray sukūn mark on the ه; 24:54 has تُطِيعُوهُ. Fixed (Exercise 1).
15. Exercise 4 is a made-up sentence and is not labelled. Labelled (Exercise 4).
