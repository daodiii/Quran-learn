# Content spec: L3.14 Form VIII (ٱفْتَعَلَ)

Written 2026-10-28. Follows the shape of `L2.01-nominal-sentence.md`. Lesson file: `src/content/lessons/level-3/14-verb-form-viii.mdx`. Old file on `main`: `17-verb-forms-vii-viii.mdx` (Form VIII part; the Form VII part moves to L3.18). Source for every parse: *al-Jadwal* (Maḥmūd Ṣāfī), including its ṣarf notes. *al-Durr al-Maṣūn* is cited once, for the root of ٱتَّخَذَ (2:51).

## What the learner can do at the end

1. Recognise a Form VIII verb: ٱ in front and an added ت right after the first root letter; read the Corpus tag (VIII).
2. Make and read the past, present and command of a regular Form VIII verb (ٱخْتَلَفَ, يَخْتَلِفُ, ٱتَّبِعْ) and say what happens to the joining alif.
3. Say why the ت turns into ط (after ص ض ط), into د (after ز ذ د) or merges into ٱتَّ (first letter ت, و or أ), and undo the change to find the root.
4. Tell Form VIII from Form VII and Form X by the root (ٱسْتَمَعَ is VIII, ٱسْتَغْفَرَ is X, نَسْتَبِقُ is VIII).
5. Recognise weak Form VIII verbs (ٱتَّقَىٰ, ٱشْتَرَىٰ, ٱخْتَارَ) and point to Level 4.
6. Preview the participle (مُفْتَعِل, L3.19) and the verbal noun (ٱفْتِعَال, L3.20).
7. Go through four verses word by word.

## Errors fixed from the old lesson

1. Frequency counts were wrong. The old callout says ٱتَّقَىٰ 250+, ٱتَّبَعَ 150+, ٱتَّخَذَ 120+, ٱخْتَلَفَ 50+. Counted from the Corpus by lemma: 166, 136, 124 and 35. *Measured.*
2. Example 2 quotes "وَمِنَ ٱلنَّاسِ مَن يَتَّبِعُ مَا تَتْلُوا۟ ٱلشَّيَٰطِينُ" as 2:102. No verse in the Corpus contains this text. *Checked.*
3. The 2:21 word-by-word glosses were shifted: مِن glossed "before" and قَبْلِكُمْ glossed "you". *Checked.*
4. "The root's first letter is either ت, و, or ث" omitted أ in that sentence, and no Form VIII verb with first letter ث is in the Corpus lemma list. *Checked.* It also says "ALMOST ALWAYS".
5. The ذ row gave ٱذَّكَرَ and ٱدَّكَرَ as two variants "in Arabic texts". The Quran has ٱدَّكَرَ (12:45); al-Jadwal says ت becomes د, then ذ becomes د, and the two merge. *Checked.*
6. "The و disappears, leaving only the doubled تَّ" (ٱتَّقَىٰ). Al-Jadwal says the و turns into ت and merges. "The hamza drops" (ٱتَّخَذَ) is also not the account in al-Jadwal (hamza to ي to ت) and it omits the dispute over the root. *Checked.*
7. ٱطَّلَعَ glossed "he viewed / ascended". The verses (18:18, 37:55) have "looked at, looked down". *Needs a source; replaced by the verse sense.*
8. Exercise 5 uses ٱشْتَرَكَ and ٱحْتَكَمَ as Quranic verbs with Form I partners "شَرِكَ" and "حَكَمَ". Neither Form VIII verb is in the Corpus (only the participle مُشْتَرِكُون). *Checked.*
9. "Reflexive action with effort" is taught as the core meaning, and glosses such as "committed following, not casual walking" and "Allah chose Abraham as HIS OWN friend" have no source. Replaced by verse pairs and "tendencies". *Needs a source.*
10. The old table of examples calls اِجْتَبَى the result of "جَبَى he collected" and خَلَفَ as "went a different way" for ٱخْتَلَفَ: unsourced etymology. Not kept.
11. The scale word was written اِفْتَعَلَ with kasra alif; the lessons now write ٱفْتَعَلَ, as the Quran does.
12. Two absolute claims: "Form VIII … Can be transitive or intransitive" is fine, but "These rules are not optional" and "ALMOST ALWAYS Form VIII" are removed.
