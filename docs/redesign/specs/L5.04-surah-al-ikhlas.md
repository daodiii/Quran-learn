# Content spec: L5.04 Analyzing Surah Al-Ikhlas

Written 2026-10-28. Lesson file: `src/content/lessons/level-5/04-surah-al-ikhlas.mdx`. It replaces the old lesson of the same name (file name, `title`, `level` and `order` kept; `kicker`, `titleArabic` and `template: steps` are new). Modelled on L4.19 (Ad-Duha) and L1.11 (Al-Ikhlas at Level 1). Sentence places, the readings of هُوَ and of the khabar, the form of ٱلصَّمَدُ and كُفُوًا and the style notes follow al-Jadwal (Maḥmūd Ṣāfī) and al-Durr al-Maṣūn (al-Samīn al-Ḥalabī); roots, forms and moods are checked in the Corpus tags (`verse.py -t`); every claim about an earlier lesson follows the finished lessons (L1.11, L2.01, L2.07, L2.11, L3.07, L3.09, L3.19, L4.01, L4.02, L4.10, L4.12, L4.14, L4.17, L4.18).

## What the learner can do at the end

1. Take all four verses apart word by word: role, case, mark, reason, and the lesson that taught the point.
2. Give the two readings of هُوَ in 112:1 (the pronoun of the matter, and a pointer to the One asked about), say which roles change and that the endings do not.
3. Say what لَمْ does to يَلِدْ and يُولَدْ, why يَلِدْ loses its و and يُولَدْ keeps it, and why قُلْ and يَكُن are hollow.
4. Find the ism and the khabar of يَكُن in verse 4 by their endings, tie لَّهُۥ to كُفُوًا, and give the two readings of the khabar (khabar of kāna, or ḥāl).
5. Name the two style points al-Jadwal names on this surah (brevity in verses 1 and 2, taqdīm in verse 4), give its reason for each, and keep them apart from what the lesson only notices (the endings in -ad, the repeated أَحَدٌ, the count of لَمْ).
6. Sort a statement as al-Jadwal's, al-Durr's, or a teaching observation with no source.

## Errors fixed from the old lesson

1. The old lesson said لَمْ gives "eternal" negation, "He has never and will never beget". L4.10: لَمْ puts the present verb in jazm and gives it a past meaning. Now in `v3`, Mistake 2.
2. ٱلصَّمَدُ was given the pattern "faʿalu, intensive or exaggerated quality". Al-Jadwal: a ṣifa mushabbaha on faʿal, with the meaning of "the one aimed at in needs" (mafʿūl). Now in `v2`, Ex 4.
3. ٱلصَّمَدُ's root was glossed "solid, eternal". Al-Durr gives "the one turned to" as the main view and "with no hollow" as one other. Now in `v2`.
4. كُفُوًا was called "second predicate OR ḥāl OR tamyīz". Neither al-Jadwal nor al-Durr gives tamyīz. Now: the khabar of يَكُن (al-Jadwal), or a ḥāl (al-Durr's second view). Blocks `v4`, `views`, Mistake 5.
5. لَّهُۥ was called the "khabar of kāna, fronted", and كُفُوًا a second predicate, in the same entry. Al-Jadwal ties لَّهُۥ to the khabar, كُفُوًا. Now in `v4`, `order`, Ex 2.
6. The old "standard order" لَمْ يَكُنْْ أَحَدٌ كُفُوًا لَهُ was not labelled as made up and had a doubled sukūn. Now a labelled "Made-up example", typed once.

8. "Taqdīm is theological: ALLAH first, then everything else." Al-Jadwal's reason: the verse was spoken to deny a match, and that meaning rests on the phrase that holds "Him". Now in `taqdim`.

## What is not checked
