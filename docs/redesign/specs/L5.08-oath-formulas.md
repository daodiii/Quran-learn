## What the learner can do at the end

1. Name the three parts of an oath (oath letter, sworn-by noun, answer) in an oath of the Quran, and say that al-Jadwal ties the oath phrase to a left-out verb, "I swear".
2. Tell the oath wāw from the joining wāw, by what stands before it and not by the case of the noun. Say that in a chain only the first wāw is the oath letter.
3. Read the bāʾ of oath (26:44) and the tāʾ of oath (16:63), and say that the Corpus tags 9 tāʾ words, all with ٱللَّهِ.
4. Read a sworn-by item that is a noun, a noun with a describer, an idafah, a doer word or a clause with مَا, and say al-Jadwal's two readings of مَا.
5. Read the time word إِذَا after a sworn-by noun as al-Jadwal does.
6. Find the answer, say how it starts (إِنَّ with لَ, لَقَدْ, مَا, إِن with لَمَّا, a verb with لَ and the nūn), say that it can wait several verses, that sentences can be joined to it, and that al-Jadwal reads it as left out in 91:1 to 91:8, 85:1 to 85:3 and 89:1 to 89:4.
7. Say what the counts show (28 oath wāws tagged, at least 33 in all, at least 21 surahs open with one) and what they do not show (why the Quran swears).
