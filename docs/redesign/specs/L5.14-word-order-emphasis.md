# Content spec: L5.14 Word Order and Emphasis (taqdīm wa-taʾkhīr)

Written 2026-10-28. The lesson is `src/content/lessons/level-5/14-word-order-emphasis.mdx`. It replaces the old lesson (`git show main:src/content/lessons/level-5/14-word-order-emphasis.mdx`). Source for every parse: al-Jadwal (Maḥmūd Ṣāfī), read with `jadwal.mjs`; al-Durr al-Maṣūn (`--book aldur-almasoon`) where it speaks about the order. The English meanings and the Arabic parse lines are unreviewed by an Arabic speaker.

## What the learner can do at the end

1. Take a verse with a word out of its usual place and say what moved and where it would usually stand (khabar before mubtadaʾ; object before doer; phrase before verb).
2. Tell an order that grammar forces (indefinite mubtadaʾ, phrase as khabar: 2:10) from an order the verse chose (109:6, 2:142, 2:284).
3. Say what al-Jadwal says about a chosen order: restriction (ḥaṣr) in 35:28, importance (ihtimām) in 3:122 and 5:11, or no reason in 109:6, 2:142 and 2:284. Say what al-Durr al-Maṣūn adds for 3:122.
4. Keep what a book says apart from "our own look".
5. Go through four verses word by word (109:6, 2:284, 3:122, 35:28).

## The lesson, step by step

5 steps, 25 blocks, 19 `Check`s, 10 `Exercise`s, 7 `Sentence`s (4 verses, 3 made-up), 4 `Parse` panels (26 cards).

**Step 1: A word out of place** (hero; 4 blocks)

3. `orders`: the two usual orders (tiles); the three moves of the lesson. Check 2.
4. `questions`: three questions for a word out of place (the move, the grammar, the source). Check 3.

**Step 2: The khabar first** (6 blocks)
5. `forced`: 2:10 (quote), the forced order (L2.01, L2.02, al-Durr "wājib al-taqdīm"); al-Jadwal's balagha note is about the word, not the order. Check 4.

9. `two-more`: 2:142 (`Sentence`); 2:115 pointer. Check 7.

**Step 3: Reasons from al-Jadwal** (6 blocks)
11. `phrase-3122`: 3:122 (end) and a made-up usual order; importance (al-Jadwal), restriction and endings (al-Durr). Check 9.
12. `parse-3122`: Parse 3, 3:122 (end) (10 cards).

**Step 4: Six mistakes** (6 blocks): `m1` "put first means only"; `m2` a reason for a forced order; `m3` a singular verb as a sign of fronting; `m4` writing the usual order wrongly; `m5` taking the khabar for the mubtadaʾ; `m6` a reason for a word that did not move. Checks 14 to 19.

**Step 5: Practice** (3 blocks): `practice-1` (exercises 1 to 5), `practice-2` (exercises 6 to 10), `done` (summary, next, links, `Sources`).
