# Content spec: L2.06 The Genitive Case

Written 2026-10-27. The lesson is in `src/content/lessons/level-2/06-genitive-case.mdx`. This spec says what it teaches, with which verses, and how each verse is parsed. It follows the L2.01 spec.

## What the learner can do at the end

1. Say that the genitive (jarr) is the third case, usually -i, and that the noun in it is called majrūr.
2. Name the three causes (al-Ajurrumiyyah): after a preposition, second word of an idafah, a word that follows a genitive word (adjective, word joined with "and").
3. Say that a preposition has no case, and that each genitive word has one cause (the first word of an idafah is not genitive for that reason).
4. Recognise the marks: -i, -in, yāʾ (dual and sound masculine plural), a hidden kasra, -a on a diptote; and that a pronoun counts as genitive without showing it.
5. Tell the accusative from the genitive for a dual or a sound masculine plural by the sentence.
6. Go through short verses word by word: role, case, mark, reason.

L2.07 teaches the prepositions and L2.08 the idafah in full; this lesson introduces both ("what genitive looks like and why"). L2.09 teaches adjectives. The diptote is shown in one block as a mark only.

## Errors fixed from the old lesson

1. **"Genitive in exactly TWO situations."** The old lesson's own examples (رَبِّ, ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ, ٱلرَّحِيمِ, مَٰلِكِ) are genitive by copying, a third cause (al-Ajurrumiyyah: "a following word"). The old lesson taught it only inside examples and then denied it in the rule.
2. **"ٱسْمِ is genitive for TWO reasons" / "gets genitive from the preposition and passes it on"** (old exercise 2). Wrong: being the first word of an idafah never gives a case. The only cause for ٱسْمِ is بِ. Fixed in `both` and `mistake-two`.
3. **"Broken plural: kasra/kasratain."** Incomplete. A broken plural that is a diptote (مَصَٰبِيحَ, مَسَٰجِدَ) takes -a. Now said in `marks-table`.
4. **The diptote was missing**: genitive in -a was not taught, so a learner would call إِلَىٰ فِرْعَوْنَ accusative. Added (`diptote`, `mistake-i`).
5. **Hidden kasra was missing** (هُدًى, مُوسَىٰ): added (`hidden`).
6. **Example 2 was headed "Genitive in multiple prepositional phrases"** but 1:6 has no genitive word. Removed with the Fatiha examples; the lesson no longer uses a non-genitive example under a genitive heading.
7. **Spellings إِلَىْٰ, عَلَىْٰ** (with a stray sukūn on the yāʾ) in the preposition table: now إِلَىٰ, عَلَىٰ.
8. **Odd glyphs in the quick-reference table** (كِتَابٌ۟, كِتَابًاْ۟, كِتَابَانِْ۟): replaced by clean forms.
9. **Made-up sentences** (كِتَابُ ٱلطَّالِبِ, فِى ٱلْكِتَابِ فَوَائِدُ, etc.) were not labelled; now "Made-up example".
