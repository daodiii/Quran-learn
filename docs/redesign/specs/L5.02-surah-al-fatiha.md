# Content spec: L5.02 Analyzing Surah Al-Fatiha

Written 2026-10-28. Lesson file: `src/content/lessons/level-5/02-surah-al-fatiha.mdx`. It replaces the old lesson of the same name (file name, `title`, `level` and `order` kept; `kicker`, `titleArabic` and `template: steps` are new). Modelled on L5.04 (Al-Ikhlas), L4.19 (Ad-Duha) and L5.01. Sentence places, ties, word-level lines and the style notes follow al-Jadwal (Maḥmūd Ṣāfī); al-Durr al-Maṣūn (al-Samīn al-Ḥalabī) is read for the places where scholars differ (the left-out word of the Bismillah, رَبِّ, مَٰلِكِ, إِيَّاكَ, غَيْرِ). Roots, forms and word types are checked in the Corpus tags (`verse.py -t`). Every claim about an earlier lesson follows the finished lesson (L1.03, L1.10, L2.07 to L2.09, L3.01, L3.02, L3.09, L3.11, L3.19, L3.20, L4.05, L4.11, L4.13, L4.17, L4.18, L5.01, L5.14 for consistency).

## What the learner can do at the end

1. Take all seven verses apart word by word: role, case or mood, mark, reason, and the lesson that taught the point.
2. Give al-Jadwal's reading of the Bismillah (a left-out mubtadaʾ, "my beginning"), name the verb view from al-Durr, and keep one view whole.
3. Give the two readings of رَبِّ (naʿt or badal), say what ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ is, the two readings of the first word of verse 4 (مَٰلِكِ, مَلِكِ), the readings of إِيَّاكَ, and the three readings of غَيْرِ, and say that the ending does not change.
4. Say the shape of each verse: five main sentences (1; 2 to 4; 5 twice; 6 to 7), the ṣila in verse 7, and the stand-in for the doer of ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ.
5. Name the style points al-Jadwal names (takrīr, the order of the two names, the statement used to make praise, ikhtiṣāṣ, iltifāt, the object put first and said twice, an open metaphor, explaining after a vague mention, tasjīʿ, the extra لَا, the doer of the anger not named, istihlāl) and keep them apart from what the lesson only notices (the endings in -īm and -īn, the count of cases).
6. Sort a statement as al-Jadwal's, al-Durr's, or a teaching observation with no source.
