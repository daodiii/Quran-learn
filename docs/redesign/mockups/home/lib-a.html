Arabic in 7 principles · the always-open card

  1. Every word is a noun, a verb or a particle.
  2. Every verb comes from a three-letter root.
  3. The verb changes, so you need no separate pronoun.
  4. Nouns change their endings by their job.
  5. Masculine is the default; feminine is marked.
  6. Definite is ال, indefinite is tanween.
  7. Word order is flexible; the endings carry the meaning.
