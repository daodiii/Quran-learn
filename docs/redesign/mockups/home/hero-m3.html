Step 3 of 9Arabic leaves out “is”

English needs a small word like is: “The sky is blue.” Arabic does not. It puts two words side by side.

A sentence like this is called a nominal sentence, جُمْلَة اِسْمِيَّة.

Remember
You add “is” when you say it in English. There is no Arabic word for it.
