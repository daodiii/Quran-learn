# Level 5 surahs: points for a reader who knows Arabic grammar

Written 2026-10-29. The nine Level 5 surahs (78 to 85 and 88) were checked verse by verse against al-Jadwal, and each got
a plain-English line per word and a question per verse (`src/data/surah-study-level5/`). Nobody who knows Arabic grammar
has read the result yet. These are the places where the writers were not sure al-Jadwal was read right, or where a
claim stayed in the file without a source. Where al-Jadwal gives two readings, the page teaches the first and names the
other.

## Where al-Jadwal's wording was hard to place

- **82:19** yawma'idhin: the online al-Jadwal text calls it a badal of the yawm before it. The page teaches it as a time
  word and says al-Jadwal calls it a badal. Check.
- **82:3-4** al-Jadwal says "doer of an unspoken verb" even though the spoken verb is passive. The page keeps that and adds
  that the noun is also the passive subject in meaning (the writer's gloss).
- **81:19** al-Jadwal's note on the lam ("in the place of the oath lam, a replacement for the displaced lam") is hard to read.
  Hedged only.
- **83:12** al-Jadwal says hal "or resumptive"; the page teaches hal first. **83:26** "the fa answers an unspoken condition"
  is a paraphrase of al-Jadwal's text.
- **78:31** "tied to the khabar of inna" was read as a phrase standing for an unspoken khabar; may be under-read.
- **84:19** tabaqan: al-Jadwal only calls it a noun with the meaning of a state, and says `an tabaq` describes it. The page
  says "object".
- **80:20** the pronoun returns to al-sabil (ishtighal), not the usual "made it easy for him" reading.
- **80:12** al-Jadwal calls sha'a the khabar of man; kept, with the alternative.

## Online copy has gaps

- 81:27-29 and the Sarf notes on 81:20-21; for these the standard parse was left.
- 80:39 mustabshiratun: the role is not named online. The page says "second khabar", the standard parse.
- 80:30 ghulban and 80:42 al-fajaratu: standard parse.

## Claims left in that al-Jadwal does not address

- 85:15 al-Majidu (damma) can only describe dhu, and another recitation reads al-Majidi. The recitation point is from memory
  and unchecked. 85:2-3 the wa's before al-yawm and shahid stay oath wa's, and only the one before mashhud is a conjunction.
- 82:18 "ma adraka is repeated here" and the Balagha on 82:19; 88: the Balagha on the camel listed first, the passive
  hiding the agent and the order of the furnishings (hedged, not removed); 79:2 "hard and gentle angels", 79:22 thumma and
  deliberation, 79:36 past tense for certainty (softened); 84:9 hal for masruran (al-Jadwal does say hal).
- 83:4-5, 16, 21 thumma and the time gap, ula'ika as doer, "those brought near" (softened).
- 78: the Form V claim for yatakallamun, the case roles of shidadan, jannatin and a'nab.
- 88:11 the ta' of tasma'u is left as "you"; al-Jadwal does not choose between "you" and "she".

## Choices between readings

- 82:8 is taught as a condition (al-Jadwal gives three readings; the question-of-reproach reading is the one named).
- 84:7 and 84:10 man is a conditional noun (the relative reading is named). 84:25 the exception is disconnected.
- 85:13 and 85:16: first readings followed. 88:2-6 the faces get a late khabar across five verses; this changes the whole
  shape of the passage, so read it first.
- 79:25 al-akhira and al-ula describe unspoken nouns ("the last word", "the first word"). Only al-Jadwal was read for
  79; al-Durr al-Masun and al-Daʿʿās were not fetched.
