# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Adults who care about the Quran and know little Arabic, from many countries. English is the language they use to learn Arabic, so it is plain B1 English, never a hurdle. Many read on a phone in short sittings. They start from the alphabet. Success for a learner is reading a verse, naming the grammar role of each word, and following the meaning of most verses without a translation.

## Product Purpose

Quran Learn (the site is called Quranic Grammar) teaches Quranic Arabic in two tracks: a Classical Grammar course (81 lessons, five levels) and a Vocabulary course built on the most frequent Quran words. Learners should master a lesson before moving on. Every lesson shows where its information comes from. The owner is a person who asks for corrections and wants learners to tell them about mistakes.

## Brand Personality

Welcoming, clear, encouraging. A good teacher at a kitchen table, not a tech demo. Colourful and engaging enough that people want to read the page, calm enough to study from.

## Anti-references

- The current site: dark, gold-on-black, monospace capital labels, starfields and scroll animation. It reads as a tech demo.
- Games: streaks, XP, badges, leaderboards. Progress is shown plainly.
- Walls of text, stacks of callout boxes, filler introductions.
- Anything that makes Arabic harder to read.

## Design Principles

1. One idea per screen. Short steps with a clear "where am I and what is next".
2. Show the example first, then name the rule.
3. Colour teaches. Word types and parts of a sentence keep the same colour everywhere, and every colour also has a text label.
4. The Arabic is the hero. It is large, correctly shaped, and never cramped or decorated.
5. Motion only when it explains something. Respect reduced motion.
6. Show the source. Each lesson says where its facts come from and how to report a mistake.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA at least, with AAA contrast (7:1) for Arabic text as already decided. 44 px touch targets. Colour is never the only signal. Works at 390 px wide and up to desktop. Text must stay readable for people reading English as a second language: short sentences, no idioms, terms explained on first use. Arabic letter-spacing stays at 0 so letters never disconnect. Fonts for Arabic (UthmanicHafs, Amiri) are kept exactly as they are.
